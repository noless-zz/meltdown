## Summary

- **Summary row on the bay:** while the pilot picks a slot and the stick is on a summary
  row, the ship gives way to the summary's bars. They are the Crew Quarters' bars
  (`HubBoard.bars`, now public): a fill for the current value, a tick at stock, and the
  number beside them. A slot row brings the ship back.
- **A mount's before and after:** while a slot is chosen, the selected item gets one strip
  per attribute it would change. Each strip holds two bars on one scale, before over after,
  with one stock tick across both, and the "Thrust 40 to 44" words beside them. Those words
  leave the rows (`HubFitBoard.trim`), so each is said once. The screen then shows
  `compare_rows_visible` rows (2) above the bars. The scale is the larger of stock ×
  `bar_headroom`, before, after, and stock plus the crew cap.
- **Data:** new optional `fit_bars` section in `hub.json` (`meta/hub_fit_bars.gd`; every bad
  key is named). It holds the rows shown, the floor, strip and bar sizes, and the before and
  after colours. Without it, or without `boards`, the bay keeps its words.
- **Model and view:** `meta/hub_fit_board.gd` describes the bars as data inside
  `HubFitBay.drawing()["board"]`. Values come only from `HubModel.summary()` and
  `preview()`. `HubFittingView` holds a `HubBoardView`, because a station's view is made
  once. `HubBoardView` draws the new `pair` item.
- **Acceptance:** the `ship_summary` check now also requires the summary bars on the
  summary row and one pair per change on the mount row, and it takes two screenshots. The
  step in `suites/m6/base.json` is unchanged; its check is extended.
