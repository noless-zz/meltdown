## Summary

- `MeltdownWiring.burning_fires(snapshot)` picks the fire entries that burn at the
  snapshot's tick out of a `SessionSnapshots.debris` copy: kind `fire`, not held, no time
  left until it catches. It returns their centres and radii.
- `MapRouteWiring` hands those to `MapHologram.show_fires` with every route it hands the map,
  and also at once when another fire catches. Fire never changes the route or the search
  cadence, so it gets no new search. `clear` (before the meltdown, after escape or loss, on a
  new session) also passes empty arrays.
- Why the call is in `MapRouteWiring` and not in `MeltdownWiring.update`: the route is
  updated there, and that is the only place the hologram can be reached without `main.gd`
  growing. The criterion's selection logic lives in `MeltdownWiring`, as the issue asks.
- New `game/tests/test_main_map_fires.gd` (eight tests) reuses the #499 fixture
  `fire_debris_level.json`. No other file changed.
