## Summary

- `meta/update_check.gd`: new `STATUS_NOT_READY`. When the release's commit is not the
  running one, `evaluate` answers "not ready" instead of "update available" if the body
  is the server-only text ("Latest dedicated server build…"), or if the APK asset's
  `updated_at` (else `created_at`) is more than 600 s before the body's "Built:" stamp.
  Why 600 s: `publish-clients` uploads the APK first and stamps the time to the minute
  after it, inside a job with a 10-minute timeout, so an APK uploaded more than 10 minutes
  before the stamp is always an earlier build's. A release without upload times is not
  judged by time.
- `meta/update_request.gd` (one line, outside the issue's file list): "not ready" shares
  the "unknown" notice. A start-up check shows nothing; an asked check shows
  "Update check: the newest build's APK is not uploaded yet" rather than "Update check
  failed". `UpdateWiring` offers only on "update available", so no update row appears and
  `update_wiring.gd` is unchanged.
- New fixture `tests/fixtures/main_latest_release_two_halves.json`: the trimmed release
  with the 22 Nov two-halves state. Body from the server-only step for d0b568b, built
  06:58 UTC. APK uploaded 06:43 UTC. Server assets 06:57. The seconds are mine, the
  minutes are the issue's.
- New `tests/test_update_check_not_ready.gd`, 8 tests: the two-halves release is not ready
  (running 719b634 or any other build); the server-only body alone; an APK 15 minutes
  older than a whole-text stamp; whole releases with the APK in the stamp's minute, the
  minute before, and exactly 10 minutes before are still updates; the old fixture without
  upload times is still an update; the body's own commit is up to date; the notice text;
  the start-up check through `UpdateWiring` offers nothing.
