## Summary

- New check `eyes_same_frame` (`acceptance/checks/eyes_same_frame_check.gd`). Once the step
  is otherwise done, it samples every drawn frame for 10 s (`AcceptanceEyeFrames`). Each
  frame is judged by `AcceptanceEyePair`, which checks three things:
  - Each eye's four view edges match the runtime's on the step's first frame.
  - The right eye mirrors the left one.
  - In the head's space, the two eyes point the same way, sit 50 to 80 mm apart side by
    side, and are centred on the head. Eyes posed from different frames of a turning head
    fail the first and last of these.
- New check `view_not_magnified` (`acceptance/checks/view_not_magnified_check.gd`). It puts a
  magenta 0.5 m square 2 m ahead of the camera, drawn over everything, and waits 3 drawn
  frames. Then it measures the square in the drawn picture (`AcceptanceMagnify`) against the
  rectangle the eye's projection puts it in. The limit is 6 % on width and height. Render
  scale 0.7 stretched over the view (27 Nov) measures ×1.43 and fails as "magnified".
- Three new m1 steps: `eyes_same_frame` (test tunnel), `eyes_same_frame_base` (base, via run
  12345 and the idle replay, as `m6/base.json` does) and `view_not_magnified` (test tunnel).
  None is `required`. Without a stereo runtime, or where nothing is drawn, each reports
  skipped. `m1.json` is the only suite file edited.
- Tolerances and sizes are in `acceptance/checks/eye_frames.json`, not in step fields.
  Step fields would have meant editing the shared `acceptance_step_parser.gd`; check files
  keep their data beside them, as `level_load.json` and `render_budget.json` do.
- `xr/eye_views.gd` (lane-b) is only read, for its `edges_of`. No `xr/` file is edited.

## Open questions

- The two checks have only run headless here. On the headset, three assumptions need its
  first device run:
  - `XRServer.get_hmd_transform()` and `get_transform_for_view(view, IDENTITY)` are in the
    same space.
  - The main viewport's image is the left eye.
  - The 0.5° eye-rotation and fov tolerances do not trip on Quest 3 noise.
  The step messages print the runtime's fov per eye, the IPD, both pixel sizes and the
  render scale, so a wrong assumption shows in the first device report.
- "The runtime's recommended field of view" is taken as the runtime's projection on the
  step's first frame. Godot does not expose OpenXR's fov separately from the projection it
  renders with.
