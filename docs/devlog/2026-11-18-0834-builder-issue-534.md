## Summary

- New `game/tests/bot_sweep_losses.gd`: counts robots alive and awake on the tick the ship
  was destroyed (read only, MP-2), buckets losses by tick (under 600, 600 to 3600, over 3600,
  so 10 s and 60 s exactly fall in the middle bucket), takes the awake median (mean of the
  middle two for an even count, `null` with nothing lost) and prints one
  `MELTDOWN_BOT_LOST {"seed":S,"seconds":X,"awake":A}` line per lost seed.
- `bot_escape_sweep.gd`: `SeedResult.awake`, a `lost` list in the run result, and with
  `--robots` the summary gains `lost_under_10_s`, `lost_10_to_60_s`, `lost_over_60_s` and
  `lost_awake_median` after `"robots":true`. Without `--robots` the line is unchanged.
- `tools/bot_escape_rate.sh combine` adds the buckets over shards and takes the median over
  every shard's `MELTDOWN_BOT_LOST` lines; it fails a shard whose buckets or lost lines do not
  match its `lost_before_reactor`. The `bot clear rate` line gains
  `; lost under 10 s A, 10 to 60 s B, over 60 s C, median M robots awake`.
- `test_bot_escape_debris.gd`'s expected nightly line was updated to the new shape.
