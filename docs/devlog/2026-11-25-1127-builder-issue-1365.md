## Summary

- `data/hub/icons.json`: 32 icons, each a few flat shapes (rect, disc, ring, tri) on a
  unit square with one colour per icon: 4 components, 8 weapons, 4 part families
  (engine, hull, shield, module), 4 utility modules (aim assist, look aim, mine scanner,
  wide radar), 4 crew roles, 8 attributes (the issue's seven and the Surveyor's map
  reveal). All drawn here from data; no image files, nothing from the original games.
- `xr/hub_icons.gd` (`HubIcons`) reads it; `icon(group, id)` gives the fallback marker
  and a warning for an id without an icon, and records it in `fell_back`. A missing or
  refused file still draws markers. `xr/hub_icon_parts.gd` reads one icon.
- `xr/hub_icon.gd` (`HubIcon`): the parts as unshaded flat meshes facing +Z, shared
  meshes and materials. `xr/hub_icon_count.gd` (`HubIconCount`): icon plus "7" or
  "3/5", the number in the short colour while have is under need.
  `xr/hub_icon_sheet.gd` (`HubIconSheet`): the whole set, one row per group, shrunk to fit.
- Acceptance: `hub_icons` step in `suites/m6/base.json` and `checks/hub_icons_check.gd`.
  It opens the room the way `hub_room` does, draws the sheet on the first station's
  screen in place of its words, saves `hub_icons_sheet.png` (skipped when headless),
  and fails if any icon fell back or the count is short.
