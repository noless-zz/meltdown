## Summary

- New `game/core/escape_path.gd` (`EscapePath`, static). `length_mm(walls, from, to)`: the
  graph has the start, the exit, every box centre and the centre of every opening (the
  positive-volume overlap of two boxes) as nodes; nodes sharing a box are joined by straight
  edges, since a box is convex. Points and edges are whole millimetres; Dijkstra settles the
  lowest-numbered node on a tie, and nodes are numbered in box order. A point outside every
  box, or no joining opening, gives -1. `longest_for_ships(sim)` takes each ship that has
  neither escaped nor died to its own start (its exit, RUN-3); straight line without walls;
  0 when no ship has a path.
- `ReactorState` reads the optional `meltdown_base_seconds`, `meltdown_seconds_per_metre`,
  `meltdown_min_seconds`, `meltdown_max_seconds`, all four or none (named error otherwise;
  non-negative; min at least one tick; max at least min). `meltdown_ticks_for_path(mm)`
  gives clamp(base + per_metre * metres, min, max) * 60, or `meltdown_ticks()` without the
  keys. `meltdown_seconds` stays required and is unused while scaling is on.
- `ProjectileHits`: on the hit that destroys a scaling reactor, the countdown is reset from
  `EscapePath.longest_for_ships`. Nothing else changes, so the path search runs once a run.
- `to_json` writes the four keys only when set, so the replay reactor line without them is
  byte for byte as before; no format version bump.
- G2: base 30 s, 0.4 s per metre, 45 s to 120 s. From beside the reactor the path is
  142.8 m, about 87 s, close to the old fixed 90 s. Placeholders; feel is the owner's call.
- `level_def.gd` and `replay_reactor_line.gd`: doc comments only.
