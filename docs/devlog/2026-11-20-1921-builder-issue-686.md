## Deviation from the criteria

The issue asks that hull goes below its start value. Wall damage goes through `apply_damage`, so
the 50-point shield takes it first (CMB-2); two boost hits cost about 34 points and hull stays at
100. The check therefore counts hull or shield. Getting hull below 100 would need three or more
boost-speed hits. Raised on the pull request for the Reviewer.
