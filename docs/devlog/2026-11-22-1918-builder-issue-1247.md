## Summary

- New `xr/auto_level_choice.gd` (`AutoLevelChoice`): the saved choice in
  `user://meltdown_auto_level.json` (`{"enabled": bool}`) and `applied`, which sets
  `auto_level` on a copy of the preset. On a fully manual roll (preset 4) the copy's roll
  becomes manual, since `levels_roll` never levels a fully manual roll; the shared preset
  file is never changed. Presets whose roll is "Off, auto-level" (1 and 2) are left alone.
  Nothing saved means nothing chosen: every preset keeps its file's value.
- `ComfortWiring.set_auto_level_choice` applies it at once to the active preset and every
  later one, the custom mode included; `set_preset` applies it after the swivel choice.
- New `dev_menu_auto_level.gd` (`DevMenuAutoLevel`): the menu row. On presets 1 and 2 it
  shows "On (preset)" and cannot be switched or saved; elsewhere On / Off is saved and
  applied. `start` applies the saved choice when the game starts.
- Menu data: `auto_level` kind, label "Auto-level", texts `auto_level_on_text`,
  `auto_level_off_text`, `auto_level_preset_text`. `DevMenuModel` holds the toggle in its
  value table only, so it grew 6 lines (288).
- `comfort_pilot.tres`: `auto_level = false`.
- Acceptance: new m1 step `auto_level_option` (`AcceptanceAutoLevelCheck`, preset 4, no
  replay). It turns the scene's option on, holds the right grip through the default layout
  and the scene's comfort filter on a scratch `ShipState` to 60 degrees, releases for 3 s,
  then flies the same with the option off. It puts the scene's choice back.
