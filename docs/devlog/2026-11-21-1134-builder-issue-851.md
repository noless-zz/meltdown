## Summary

- New suite field `refresh_120` (`game/acceptance/acceptance_refresh_120.gd`,
  `AcceptanceRefresh120`): a bool, only with a `refresh_rate` or `frame_budget` check, and
  only with a numbered `preset`. When a step starts, after its preset is set, the scene's
  `RefreshRate` gets a `RefreshChoice` made for the step with 120 Hz on, and the rate is
  asked for again; when the step is recorded the previous choice is put back and its rate
  asked for again. Nothing is read from or written to `user://meltdown_refresh.json`.
- `refresh_rate` with `refresh_120` wants 120 Hz; it fails if the preset does not allow
  120 Hz, and is skipped (fails when `required`) where the runtime offers no 120 Hz rate,
  with the offered rates named.
- `frame_budget` with `refresh_120` is skipped the same way where 120 Hz is not offered; it
  already judged against the rate in use (`FrameStats.load_budget(in_use_hz)`).
- `m1.json`: `refresh_rate_120` and `frame_budget_120` (preset 4, the `frame_budget`
  replay and timings), appended at the end so every earlier step flies as before. The
  existing `refresh_rate` and `frame_budget` steps are unchanged.
- The step's choice is set and restored in `AcceptanceChecks` (`begin_scene`, new
  `end_step`), so `acceptance_runner.gd` grows by one line and stays under its 260 limit.
