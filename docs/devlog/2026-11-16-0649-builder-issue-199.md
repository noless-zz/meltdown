## Summary

- `StatusPanel` now draws every label and both bar quads with `no_depth_test = true`, so
  the solid shell of cockpit presets 1 and 2, nearer than the panel, no longer hides it.
- New named constants in `status_panel.gd`: `RENDER_PRIORITY = 10` (track) and
  `RENDER_PRIORITY_TOP = 11` (labels and the filled bar). Both sit above the cockpit
  frame's materials (default 0) and below the fade quad's `RENDER_PRIORITY_MAX`. The extra
  step keeps the filled bar from being covered by its track now that depth no longer
  orders them.
- `COCKPIT_POSITION`, the tilt and the cockpit JSON files are unchanged; nothing writes
  `ShipState` or `Sim`.
