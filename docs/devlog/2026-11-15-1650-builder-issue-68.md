## Summary

- `game/xr/gripped_stick_mapping.gd` (`GrippedStickMapping`, `RefCounted`). Input: each
  hand's `Transform3D` relative to its grab point (origin in metres, basis the turn since
  the grab, player axes x right, y up, -z ahead), each grip 0..1, and the button dictionary
  of `ThumbstickMapping.BUTTONS`. Output: `ShipInput.from_floats`; `axes()` also exposes the
  six -1..1 values.
- Stick (right hand, rotation only): the top tilted ahead pitches the nose down (aircraft
  convention, `invert_pitch` flips it), tilted right rolls right, twisted clockwise seen from
  above yaws right. Tilt and twist come from a swing-twist split about the stick's up axis,
  so a tilt does not leak into yaw.
- Throttle (left hand, position only): ahead is forward thrust, sideways strafes, up lifts.
- A hand does nothing unless its grip is at least 0.5. Afterburner: the throttle hand's lower
  face button (X), only while the throttle grip is held.
- Dead zones and full deflections are named constants with units: tilt 4 to 30 degrees,
  twist 5 to 25 degrees, throttle 0.02 to 0.10 m. Turn rates reuse `ThumbstickMapping`'s, so
  both schemes turn equally fast. Each axis is clamped to -1..1.
- Left-handed mode swaps poses, grips and the afterburner button (A).
- `game/tests/test_gripped_stick_mapping.gd`: 11 tests.
