#1451: the controllers are not shown at the home base or the shaft choice, so pointing is hard
to aim (playtest, the owner's priority, first in lane-b).

## Summary

The Planner's guess was right. `HubRoom.show_for` set the head camera's `cull_mask` to the
room's layer alone, so the controller models on the default layer were culled. At the shaft
choice and end screens the controllers sit under the black fade quad.

This is the smallest coherent part of the issue: the hands at the base, and the aim ray at the
base and the shaft choice.

- `view/hub_room.gd`: new `HANDS_LAYER_BIT` (render layer 19). While the room shows, the camera
  draws `SHOWN_MASK`, which is the room's layer plus the hands. The mine and its fade stay
  hidden, and the room's lights still light only the room's layer. `shows_hands()` added.
- `xr/hand_layer.gd`: puts every drawn node of a controller on the hands layer, except the
  control labels. The labels name flight actions, which do nothing at the base.
  `ControllerModel` tags itself on `build`, and again (deferred) when a runtime or vendor model
  arrives.
- `xr/aim_ray.gd`, `xr/aim_ray_spec.gd` and `xr/aim_ray.json` (ARC-4): a thin ray with a dot
  where it lands. With nothing hit it fades out to its tip at `length_m`. A bad file names its
  field, and the ray then uses built-in values. The ray draws on the hands layer, and at the top
  render priority so it shows above the fade at the shaft choice. It reads no comfort setting.
- `xr/aim_targets.gd`: where the ray lands. The stations' own `HubStations.pick` and
  `ShaftChoiceWiring.point_in` choose the entry. The ray ends at the point on the aim ray
  nearest that entry, so the ray, the dot and the highlight agree. The ray shows only while the
  room's stations or the shaft panel are shown, never in flight.
- `xr/xr_rig.gd`: hangs the ray and draws it each frame from `RightHand`, the controller both
  selections read, while it is tracked.
- No lane-c file was edited, and nothing in `core/` changed.
