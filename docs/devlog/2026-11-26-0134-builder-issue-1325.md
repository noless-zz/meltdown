  (marks with remark, time, preset and source; next unmarked step; results; restore),
  `ChecklistStore` (one JSON file per checklist at `user://meltdown_checklist_<id>.json`,
  written through `SafeFileWrite` after every mark, read back at start), `ChecklistMarkdown`.

  `test_checklist_docs.gd` fails when the two differ.

  (one label, text only). Stick up/down steps, left/right choices, A confirms, B/Y step
  the remark. Choices: Pass, Fail, Skip, Remark, Do (when the step has an action), Pin,
  Close. Pinned, one small line up and left shows the next step and takes no input.
- `ChecklistWiring`: development builds only (`OS.is_debug_build()`); the dev menu's new
  top-level "Test checklist" row (`DevMenuChecklist`) shows only once a checklist is
  attached. While open it takes the controls like the dev menu and pauses solo play under
  its own reason. A step's action becomes the dev menu's own choice (`comfort:N`,
  `level:seed:N`, `level:<path>`, `level:`) and goes through `DevMenuWiring.apply`.
- `DevMenuModel._confirm` now maps action rows to results through a table, which kept the
  file under 300 lines. `main.gd` stays at 300 lines (two comments moved inline).
