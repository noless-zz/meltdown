## Summary

The two meanings of the left stick click swap, as the Planner chose. One `ClickHold` still
decides both: a press released before `MapHologram.HOLD_SECONDS` (0.4 s) toggles the map on
its release, and the map stays open with the button up until the next click; a press held
to 0.4 s flips the labels once, on the frame it gets there (new
`ClickHold.long_hold_started`), and leaves the map as it was. The toggle lives in a new
`xr/map_toggle.gd` so `map_hologram.gd` stays under its 300-line test. A `show_map` remapped off the
label button toggles the map on each press of its own control. `MapWiring` closes the map
when a new session starts (restart, level switch) and on every frame the ship is destroyed.
The labels read "click: map, hold: labels". Decision record 0004 has the "Amendment (#914)"
section, marked proposed until the owner's headset session. View and xr only; `main.gd`
and the simulation are untouched, so no suite hash can change.
