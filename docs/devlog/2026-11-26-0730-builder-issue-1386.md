## Summary

- `core/crew_bonus.gd` (`CrewBonus`, new): `hold_slots` and `meltdown_seconds` as
  start-of-run data. `Sim.set_crew_bonus` installs it before the first tick. Every
  ship's new `hold_capacity` is `HOLD_CAPACITY + hold_slots`, and the reactor's new
  `crew_seconds` is added to the countdown after the level's own min/max clamp. A zero
  bonus is never installed, written or hashed.
- `SimHash` adds the bonus only while it is set. Every pinned hash and replay stays the
  same; the m6 suite's hashes all held.
- `core/replay_crew_line.gd` (`ReplayCrewLine`, new): replay format 21 adds
  `crew {"hold_slots":1,"meltdown_seconds":5}`. It is written only for a non-zero bonus,
  so other replays keep their version and text. It is chained through `ReplayRulesLine`
  like the alarm line.
- `meta/crew_benefits.gd`: `CrewBenefits.bonus(totals)` turns the capped totals into a
  `CrewBonus`. The class comment no longer says the other stats are "applied by the
  issues that use them".
- `RunWiring.crew_bonus()` → `LevelStart.run_rules` → `RunLevelRules.load_files(...,
  p_crew)` → `install` puts it on the sim and in the replay. Nothing in `view/` or `xr/`
  writes it.
- The change to `level_start.gd` is one line, in `run_rules`. Lane-a's #1424 edits
  `session_for` in the same file, a separate hunk.
- New m6 acceptance step `crew_bonus` in `suites/m6/effects.json`, with check file
  `acceptance/checks/crew_bonus_check.gd`. On run 12345 it flies `run_advances.replay`
  twice through the scene's `LevelStart`: once with a Cargo Rigger and a Reactor Hand in
  the roster and once with no crew. It judges the hold (2 parts against 1), the
  countdown (3903 ticks against 3603) and that the crewed replay flies back to its hash.
