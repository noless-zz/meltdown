## Summary

- `ComfortPreset`: new fields `auto_level_rate_deg_per_s` (30 in all four files, a guess)
  and `auto_level_snap_threshold_deg` (10 in Anchored, `NO_LIMIT` elsewhere; `NO_LIMIT`
  means smooth). `levels_roll()` is `auto_level` and not fully manual roll.
  `has_smooth_rotation()` also counts a smooth auto-level.
- `game/xr/comfort_auto_level.gd` (`ComfortAutoLevel`): bank is `atan2(x.y, y.y)`, which
  is the angle about the nose whatever the nose elevation. Above 80 degrees of elevation
  bank is undefined and no roll is added. The roll rate is truncated towards zero, so it
  never passes level. Snap mode levels in one tick above the threshold and does nothing
  below it; smooth mode is capped at the rate.
- `ComfortInputFilter.filter`: with `levels_roll()` and no roll input, adds that roll,
  measured on the orientation after this tick's pitch and yaw (roll is the last turn of a
  tick and does not move the nose).
- #99: `ComfortInputFilter.copy_input` and `LatestInputSource._copy` copy every script
  property of `ShipInput`, so `reset_to_start` and later fields survive. `core/` untouched.
- Tests: `test_comfort_auto_level.gd` (10), `test_ship_input_copies.gd` (6), the preset
  table updated, and `test_comfort_input_filter.gd`'s preset 1 check now allows a roll that
  levels the ship completely in one tick (pitch and yaw snapped in the same tick bank it).

## Open questions

- `main.gd` (lane-b) still builds `ShipInput.new(thrust, turn, afterburner)` in two places
  and so drops `reset_to_start` and any later field; it should use
  `ComfortInputFilter.copy_input` or the same approach.
- `main.gd`'s `snap_show_angle` only looks at yaw and pitch snaps, and a preset 1 roll snap
  can be as small as the 10 degree threshold, below half a 30 degree snap. Such a tick
  would be slerped over one frame, which is brief smooth rotation (CMF-3). The display
  check should treat any tick turned by a roll snap as a snap.
- Headset: is 30 degrees per second a comfortable levelling rate, and is a 10 degree snap
  threshold right for preset 1?
