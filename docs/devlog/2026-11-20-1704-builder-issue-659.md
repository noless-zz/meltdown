## Summary

- Each `xr/cockpits/*.json` gains a `"look"` object, read by the new `CockpitLook`:
  glow (self-light), edge highlight colour and width, the frame's own light (colour,
  energy, range), panel size, pattern contrast, rivet size and a brightness floor.
- Interior lightened from (0.08, 0.10, 0.13) to (0.30, 0.33, 0.38); ledge, pillars and
  rim from (0.24, 0.27, 0.32) to (0.44, 0.47, 0.52), in every preset file.
- `xr/cockpit_surface.gdshader` (new): lit surface with panel lines (a whole number per
  10-degree cell, so neighbouring cells line up) and optional rivets, an edge highlight
  on the long edges of the ledge, pillars and rim, and an emission term of `glow` times
  the surface colour. No texture: the pattern fades out where a panel is under about
  ten pixels, the computed stand-in for mipmapping. Not fogged.
- `CockpitMaterials` (new) builds the materials and a `CockpitLight` omni light 0.2 m
  above the eye, no shadow, reach under the 1 m hull so it lights no wall.
- `CockpitSpec` rejects a file whose interior or structure colour, at its darkest
  pattern tone with no ambient light at all, shows below `min_lit_brightness`.
- Presets 1 and 2: 12 cm panels (at least 10 degrees wide on the farthest surface),
  contrast 0.08 and 0.10, no rivets. Presets 3 and 4: 8 cm panels, contrast 0.15, rivets.
- #665 (#651) merged while CI was due, leaving this branch in conflict, so no CI ran.
  Rebased on `main`: kept #665's guard keys beside the lighter colours and the look.
