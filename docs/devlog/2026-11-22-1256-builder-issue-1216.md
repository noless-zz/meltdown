## Issue

#1216: hull, shield and energy through one gauge interface, and cockpit files can declare
instruments. This is step 2 of #1207 (ARC-3, CMF-6, ARC-4, DEV-7).

## Summary

- New `xr/ship_gauges.gd` (`ShipGauges`): a plain snapshot with hull, shield and energy
  as fractions 0..1 and the meltdown countdown in seconds (`NO_COUNTDOWN` when none).
  - `take(ship, reactor)` builds it from session copies; `of(...)` builds it from plain
    values.
  - `value(name)` reads a gauge by name, for the instruments later.
  - A non-finite fraction reads 0, as the bars always drew it.
- `StatusPanel.show_gauges(gauges)` is now the only way the three bars and the countdown
  row get their values. `show_defence` and `show_meltdown` are gone, and `show_values`
  lost its energy argument. The panel keeps the last snapshot in `gauges`.
- `CombatWiring.update` takes the snapshot once a frame, where it already fed shield and
  hull, with a copy of the reactor. `MeltdownWiring.update` no longer hands the panel the
  countdown, and `RigViews.show_ship` no longer hands it energy. Both now reach the panel
  inside the snapshot. These three files were outside the issue's list, but the
  criterion "reads its bars and countdown only through it" needs them.
- New `xr/cockpit_instrument.gd` (`CockpitInstrument`). Each entry of a cockpit file's
  optional `instruments` list has `id`, `gauge` (`hull`, `shield`, `energy` or
  `countdown`), `yaw_deg` and `pitch_deg`, seen from the eye point, each within 90
  degrees. `CockpitSpec` accepts and validates the list. Errors name the field
  (`instruments[1].gauge ...`), and `load_file` adds the path. The four shipped cockpit
  files have `"instruments": []`. Nothing is drawn yet.
