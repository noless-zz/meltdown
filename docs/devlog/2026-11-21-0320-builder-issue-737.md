## Summary

- New `game/acceptance/acceptance_view_flat_check.gd` (`AcceptanceViewFlatCheck`): while the
  step runs, the runner hands it each frame's duration; every `sample_interval_seconds` (2 s)
  it reads the eye image through the same grab the #718 screenshots use and judges a 64 x 64
  copy. A sample is flat when the luminance standard deviation is under
  `flat_luminance_stddev_below` (0.02) and fewer than `flat_colours_below` (12) colours remain
  after quantising each channel to `colour_levels` (16) steps.
- Thresholds in `game/acceptance/view_flat.json`, checked on load (unknown or missing keys,
  bools, an interval over 2 s and out-of-range values are rejected by name).
- Measurements: `samples`, `flat_samples`, `flat_sample_seconds`, `lowest_luminance_stddev`,
  `lowest_at_seconds`, `unread_samples`. Fails naming the first flat sample's time, standard
  deviation and colour count; fails when no image could be read at all.
- Skipped off the headset with the same rule and reason as `frame_budget` (OpenXR and Android),
  so Monado's CI run skips it; failed when `required`.
- `view_seconds` step key (2 to 600 s), required by the check and refused without it.
- `m1` gains `view_not_flat` as its last step: 60 s holding still in the test tunnel (no
  replay; the headlight is always on). Appended at the end so the earlier steps keep their
  places; `test_m1_comfort_replays.gd` now compares the first eight ids, not the whole list.
- `acceptance_runner.gd` dispatches the check and waits for its samples; comments folded so the
  file stays at 298 lines.
