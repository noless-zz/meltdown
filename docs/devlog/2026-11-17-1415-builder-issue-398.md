## Summary

- New `game/data/gen/debris.json`: `count`, `radius` and `after_seconds` as
  `[lowest, highest]` ranges, plus `min_start_distance` and `min_reactor_distance` (metres of
  clear space from an entry's surface to the start point and to the reactor's surface).
- New `game/gen/debris_plan.gd` (`DebrisPlan`): parses the file (every unknown or missing key
  named in one message; ranges checked for shape, bounds and order), and `place` builds the
  level's `MeltdownDebris`. Spots are the centres of every box (and doorway) of the modules the
  shortest route from the reactor to the start passes through (found with `KnownRoute`, every
  box explored, no debris), then the centres of the openings between those boxes. Each entry
  draws a radius, a fall time and up to 64 spots from an RNG seeded with
  `hash([output_hash(), "debris"])`; a spot is used once.
- `GeneratedLevel.build` and `MineGenerator.to_level_def` take an optional `DebrisPlan` after
  the robot plan. Without it `level.debris` stays null. No reactor means no debris.
  `MineGenerator.debris_note` says why fewer entries than drawn were placed.
- `seed_level.gd` and `seed_sweep.gd` were not changed: wiring debris into the flown seeds and
  the nightly sweep is not in the issue's criteria.

## Open questions

- Entries are not kept clear of robots, so a robot can end up inside debris once it falls.
- `after_seconds` tops out at 60 s against the 90 s countdown; once #417 scales the countdown
  with the escape path, a short level's countdown could end before late debris falls.
- An entry centred in a box but clear of its openings blocks nothing the route guard sees
  (`KnownRoute` only checks openings), so it may narrow a room without ever being held back.
