## Summary

- `MineGenerator.generate(seed, modules, count)` in `game/gen/`: places a random first
  module as authored at the origin, then joins modules one by one to a random open
  connector, connector to connector (same size class, outward axes opposite), turned by
  one of the 24 rotations in 90° steps, all in whole millimetres. A placement that
  overlaps a placed box is rejected; after `MAX_TRIES` (64) rejections in a row, or with no
  open connector left, it stops short and says so in `note`.
- Randomness only from a `RandomNumberGenerator` seeded with the given seed, read with
  `randi()`.
- Output: `placements` (`ModulePlacement`: id, turn, offset, parent and the two connectors
  joined, its box range), `union` (a `BoxUnion` in placement order), the same corners in
  millimetres, `open_connectors` in level space, and `output_hash()`.
- `ModuleTurns`: the 24 rotations as integer matrices, turn 0 the identity.
- Doorway boxes: joined connectors meet exactly, so the two modules only touch at the
  opening, and `LevelDef.joined` needs 2 m of overlap (a ship cannot cross between boxes
  that only touch). Each join adds a box the size of the opening reaching 3 m into each
  module. This is the only way to meet both "connectors meet exactly" and "one piece under
  `LevelDef.joined`"; it is noted in the pull request for the reviewer.

## Open questions

- A doorway assumes the module box at each opening is at least 3 m deep; every shipped
  module is. A shallower one would only push the doorway into its own module's rock, which
  the overlap check does not stop. A `ModuleDef` rule for it could come with more modules.
- Modules may sit flush against an unrelated module's wall (touching is not overlapping);
  an open connector can then face another module's wall. The level validator (#317) is the
  natural place to decide whether that matters.
