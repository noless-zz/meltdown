## Issue

#1240: measure the thinnest wall between tunnels across 100 seeds, follow-up to the
playtest #1225 (GEN-3, GEN-5, ARC-1, ARC-2, DEV-7).

## Summary

- New `gen/wall_gap.gd` (`WallGap.thinnest(union)`): for every pair of boxes and
  each axis on which they are apart, the gap between A's face and B's facing-away
  face, when the two faces overlap in projection. Overlapping or touching boxes are
  an opening and give nothing. Where other boxes fill the slab between the faces (a
  connector between two ends, or a box opening through A's face), that part is not
  rock; a pair counts only if some part of the overlap has rock all the way across
  (the overlap is cut at the other boxes' edges and each cell checked). Returns
  `{gap_m, box_a, box_b}`, `INF` and -1 when there is no such pair. RefCounted,
  no nodes, plain loops in box order (ARC-1, ARC-2).
- New `tests/test_wall_gap.gd`: two tunnels 0.5 m apart give 0.5; the thinnest
  of several walls is found on any axis; overlapping and touching boxes are not
  counted; an L-shaped pair whose faces do not overlap is not counted; a connector
  filling the space is not a wall, while a narrower one leaves rock that counts; the
  same union always gives the same answer.
- New `tests/wall_gap_sweep.gd` (SceneTree script like `validate_seeds.gd`, not
  in `run_tests.gd`): per seed the thinnest wall, then min, 5th percentile, median
  and the seeds below 1 m.
- Generator unchanged; no level or pinned hash moves.
