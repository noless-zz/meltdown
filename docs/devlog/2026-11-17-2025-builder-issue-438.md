## Summary

This is a partial run. The mechanism lands, but its shipped value is 0, and #438 stays
open with `needs-human`.

- `generator.json` gains `loop_bias` (0..1). `LoopBridges` reads it alongside the other
  keys, and a missing or bad value is named.
- With a bias above 0, a seeded draw against the bias (in thousandths) decides for each
  module whether the generator looks further. When it does, it draws up to 16 placements
  that fit (`LoopBias.CANDIDATES`). It keeps the first one that leaves a new open
  connector facing an open one inside the bridge limits (`LoopBias.faces`), and otherwise
  the first that fit. With a bias of 0 nothing extra is drawn.
- `MineGenerator._place_one` is split into `_draw`, which makes one candidate as a
  `PlacementChoice`, and the choice between candidates.
- Looped seeds of 0..999: 58 at 0, 128 at 0.25, 194 at 0.5, 214 at 0.6, 253 at 0.75 and
  297 at 1.
- **Blocked.** At every bias above 0, 5 to 14 seeds fail the validator's `passage`
  check. The cause is a latent flaw in the #411 Z shape: a short gap, or offsets on two
  axes, leave sliver joints. Fixing it changes some bias-0 pins, which conflicts with
  #438's criterion that bias 0 changes nothing. The seeds and options are in the #438
  comment, and the follow-up is #472 (`triage`).
