## Summary

- A step can name `custom_file` (a `res://` path, instead of `preset`). New
  `acceptance_custom_comfort.gd` parses it and, when the step starts, reads the file with
  `ComfortCustom`, hands it to the scene's `ComfortWiring` (`custom_path` and its cached
  file) and chooses `CUSTOM_NUMBER` through `set_comfort_preset`. A rejected file fails
  the step as `custom_file: <path> rejected, field "<field>": <reason>` and flies nothing.
  The runner gains one call.
- `comfort_motion` judges the custom mode by the custom preset's own values: any smooth
  turn fails when the file turns every smooth setting off, otherwise its yaw and pitch
  caps apply. Presets 1 to 4 are judged as before.
- Sample `acceptance/comfort/m1_custom.json`: base 2, yaw cap 40 °/s (preset 2: 60),
  vignette off (strength 0, trigger NEVER).
- Step `comfort_custom` at the end of `m1.json`, checks `comfort_motion`, `final_hash`,
  `no_script_errors`, hash 1450670154.

### Departure from the issue

The issue asks the step to fly `m1_comfort_preset_2.replay`. A replay stores the inputs
the comfort filter let through and is flown without the filter, so that replay keeps
preset 2's hash in any mode and turns at preset 2's 60 °/s, which the custom file's
40 °/s cap fails (a unit test shows this). The step instead flies
`m1_comfort_custom.replay`, recorded by the same pilot as presets 1 and 2
(`m1_comfort_source.gd`) through the comfort wiring in the custom mode, which is how
those two steps got their own hashes. Re-record with
`MELTDOWN_RECORD_M1=comfort_custom`.
