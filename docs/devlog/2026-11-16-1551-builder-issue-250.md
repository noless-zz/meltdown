## Summary

- New `game/view/salvage_view.gd` (`SalvageView`, `Node3D`), mirroring `RobotView`:
  `show_salvage(previous, current, alpha)` draws one pooled mesh per item at the blended
  position. Items are paired by index; a partner of a different part or further away than
  two ticks of either copy's speed (+0.1 m) is treated as a different item (an earlier one
  was picked up and the array shifted), so nothing slides across the tunnel.
- Spin is the view's own `spin_angle`, advanced in `_process` (1.2 rad/s about an axis
  tilted 0.5 rad, so rings show their hole). Never written back.
- New `game/view/salvage_look.json`: `default` plus one look per weapon id. Shapes add
  `cylinder` and `torus`, which no robot uses; parts are rods and rings in pale colours,
  emissive at 2x energy. Parsing and errors as in `robot_look.json`, top keys `default` and
  `parts`; size is a multiple of `SalvageItem.RADIUS`.
- Not wired into `main.gd` (lane-b).
