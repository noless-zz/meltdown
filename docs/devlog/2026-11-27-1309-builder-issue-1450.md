## Summary

- Places are data (ARC-4): each entry in `xr/dev_menu.json` has a `places` list (`mine`,
  `base`, `shaft`, `end`), read by `xr/dev_menu_places.gd`. Level, Restart level, Return to
  base and Flight test list `mine` only. Everything else lists all four places. A section
  with nothing to show at the place, such as Mine at the base, is left out. `DevMenuModel`
  has a `place` that hides the other entries.
- New `dev_menu_where.gd` decides where the pilot sits: base when the hub is open, shaft
  when the shaft panel is open, end when a destroyed or escape line shows, mine otherwise.
  The tap is still ignored during the flight test and while an end screen fades in. The
  menu opens once the screen is dark. It is also ignored during the hub's launch fade. A
  menu left open closes when the place changes. `DevMenuWiring` delegates to it and shrinks
  from 299 to 290 lines.
- The menu takes the controls everywhere: the hub, shaft panel and end screens read the
  menu's idle values, and the session pause holds them, as in the mine. #1408's tap at the
  base that opened only the pinned checklist is gone. The menu's Test checklist entry opens
  the page over the hub instead.
- The menu, its Info lines and the checklist page are now also drawn on the home base room's
  layer. The room's camera draws only that layer, so without this nothing would show at the
  base.
- "Update available" sign: new `xr/update_sign.gd`, with `xr/update_sign_look.gd` and
  `xr/update_sign.json` for its place and words. It hangs in the room by the seat, low and to
  the right, and shows only at the base while a build waits.
- `xr/dev_menu_lists.gd`: the corner and scheme readers moved out of `DevMenuSpec` to keep it
  under 300 lines.
- `main.gd` and `hub_wiring.gd` are unchanged.
- Acceptance: new `menu_at_base` and `menu_at_shaft` steps in `suites/m6/base.json`, with the
  check file `checks/menu_at_base_check.gd`. The `checklist_at_base` check now opens the page
  through the menu.
