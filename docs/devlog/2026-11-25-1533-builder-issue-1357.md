## Summary

- `meta/ship_summary.gd` (`ShipSummary`), pure: every attribute a part file multiplies or
  a crew role raises, from the data (ARC-4), each with stock, current and the sources
  (part by upgrade name, crew by role and count) that make up the difference. Current is
  worked out as `RunStartShip.build` does; a test holds the two equal. `changes` gives
  before and after between two summaries.
- `HubModel`: `summary()`, `preview(action)` (what a fit or move would change, without
  changing anything) and `strip()` (empties the fitting; crafted upgrades and crew stay).
- `meta/hub_summary_rows.gd`: the rows. "Ship: now / stock", then one row per attribute
  ("Thrust 44 / 40"), its sources as lines under it while it is selected ("Trim Vanes +4",
  "Shield Technician ×1 +5"). Named by the hub file's `effect_names` / `stat_names`, so no
  id shows. Under a mount or move row, while selected: "Thrust 40 to 44".
- Fitting Bay rows moved out of `hub_text.gd` into `meta/hub_fit_rows.gd`; the strip row
  ("Strip ship to stock", note "Parts go back to the locker") shows while anything is
  fitted, then the summary. Crew Quarters shows the summary under the crew rows.
- `HubText`: a row may own detail lines by an `owns` key; the window now keeps the detail
  lines under the selected row in view when it can (`selection_end`), so a fit's before
  and after is on screen with it. `HubWiring` saves the fitting after a strip.
