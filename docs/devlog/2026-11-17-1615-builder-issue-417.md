## Summary

- `game/data/gen/reactor.json` turns on RUN-5 scaling for generated levels:
  `meltdown_base_seconds` 30, `meltdown_seconds_per_metre` 0.2, `meltdown_min_seconds` 45,
  `meltdown_max_seconds` 180. `meltdown_seconds` 90 stays, because `ReactorState` requires it
  and uses it when scaling is off.
- Reasoning: escape paths (reactor to exit, `EscapePath`) over seeds 0..999 run from 9.5 m
  to 347 m, median 135 m. At the validator's 10 m/s that is 1 to 35 s of flying. With
  30 s + 0.2 s/m the countdown is 300/p + 2 times the flying time for a path of p metres,
  so never under 2 times below the max clamp, and the 180 s clamp only bites past 750 m.
  The 1.5x rule would hold to 1200 m. The 45 s floor covers 99 short levels (paths under
  75 m), where a shorter timer would feel arbitrary rather than tense.
- New `game/tests/test_generated_meltdown_timer.gd` (4 tests).
