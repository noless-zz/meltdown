# 2026-11-15 Builder, issue #65 (lane-c)

Scheduled Builder run in lane-c. No open pull request closes a lane-c issue (#74 is lane-a,
#75 is lane-b), so the run took the lowest-numbered `ready` lane-c issue in M1: #65, snap yaw
and pitch for comfort preset 1 as an input filter (CMF-3, CMF-8, CMF-6, CMF-1, ARC-3).

## Summary

- `game/xr/comfort_input_filter.gd`: new `ComfortInputFilter` (`RefCounted`) holding a
  `ComfortPreset` and one armed flag per snap axis. `filter(input)` returns a new
  `ShipInput`; thrust and afterburner are copied unchanged.
  - Snap yaw and snap pitch: when the rate passes half of `ThumbstickMapping`'s full rate on
    an armed axis, that tick's rate becomes `snap_angle_deg` per tick (60 Hz) with the
    stick's sign, and the axis disarms. It re-arms once the rate falls below a quarter.
    Every other tick is zero. 30 degrees is 31416 mrad/s in `ShipInput.turn_q`.
  - Roll is zeroed in `OFF_AUTO_LEVEL`; smooth axes and manual roll pass through.
- `game/tests/test_comfort_input_filter.gd`: nine tests. Preset 1 with a stick sweep through
  `ThumbstickMapping` and four seeded random sequences gives only 0 or one snap for pitch and
  yaw and zero roll; holding gives exactly one snap per axis with the right sign; the
  re-arm hysteresis, including the other direction; 12 snaps through a real `Sim` turn the
  ship 2 pi within 0.01 rad (and pi after six); preset 4 passes 600 random inputs unchanged;
  smooth yaw and manual roll pass through; the argument is not modified.

## Open questions

- Reversing the stick from full one way to full the other within a single tick does not
  re-arm, because the rate never falls below a quarter. Real sticks pass through the centre
  over several ticks, so this should not show in play; check in the headset.
- A 30 degree snap is a single 60 Hz tick of 1800 degrees per second. At 90 Hz the view's
  interpolation (#71) may show it as one or two in-between frames rather than a clean cut;
  the owner should check whether that reads as a snap. A fade or a view-side cut would need
  a decision record, since the Planner chose the ship-turn approach.
