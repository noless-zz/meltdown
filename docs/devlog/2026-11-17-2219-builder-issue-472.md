## Summary

- `LoopShapes.z_shape`: each leg now stops `LEG_INTO_CROSS_MM` (3 m) into the cross box
  instead of running half a tunnel width past the middle. The legs never meet each other
  (3 m is under half the 8 m small connector) and never reach the far doorway box for any
  gap of at least a tunnel width, which `z_shape` already requires. Each leg still meets the
  cross box through its whole square, 3 m deep, above `LevelDef.MIN_OVERLAP_M`.
- New `LoopShapes.joints_clear(boxes, width)`: every two boxes that touch or overlap must
  share at least `width` on the two larger of their three overlaps. `LoopBridges._bridge`
  runs it over the tunnel and both doorways and leaves the pair unbridged if it fails. With
  the new shape it never fails on the shipped data; it guards against a broken shape.
- No pair is rejected, so looped counts stay the same at every bias.
- `loop_bias` stays 0 in `generator.json`.
