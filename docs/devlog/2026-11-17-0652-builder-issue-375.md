## Summary

- New `game/net/bot_flank.gd` (`BotFlank`). When the bot's target is out of sight only
  because an intact reactor is in the way (the walls would let the shot through), it picks a
  firing point: candidates at 15, 10, 20 and 7 m from the robot in the 26 directions with
  components -1, 0 or 1, in that fixed order. A candidate counts if the walls hold it with the
  ship's radius plus 1 m, its line to the robot clears the walls and the reactor plus 1 m, and
  the route joins it to the ship. The one nearest the ship wins; ties go to the lowest index.
  The point is kept while it still serves, so the bot does not hop between near-equal points.
- `BotPilot._routed_input` flies to that point with the nose on the robot. It does not wait
  for robots closing or for its shield there. Once the line opens, the usual attack takes over.
- **Change beyond the new step:** `BotPilot.under_fire` now treats a robot behind an intact
  reactor as no threat, which is what #345 made true. Without this, the first try still stalled.
  The bot fell back to its last safe point at (0, -35.9, -45.2) and stayed there for good,
  because it counted the warden's blocked line as fire and never felt safe again.
- The wall check `_reaches` moved into `BotFlank.reaches_walls` (static, same steps) so both
  files share it. `bot_pilot.gd` is still 284 lines.
- Without a reactor nothing changes. Both changes test for `sim.reactor` first.
