## Summary

- New `game/xr/status_panel.gd` (`StatusPanel`, `Node3D`): unshaded `Label3D`s for the
  preset, speed, `BOOST` / `recharging` and the build, plus an energy bar quad on a darker
  track. `show_values(...)` takes plain values only. A new preset number shows the preset
  large for `PRESET_LARGE_SECONDS` (2 s), driven by `advance(delta)`; the preset the game
  starts on is shown large once too.
- Placement: a child of the rig origin, at `StatusPanel.rig_transform(cockpit)` where
  `cockpit` is the ship's centre in the rig's space (`head_recentre.offset.affine_inverse()`):
  0.3 m down and 0.55 m ahead of the eye point, tilted 30 degrees back to face the eyes,
  inside the ship's 1 m collision sphere. It moves with the ship, not the head.
- `game/main.gd`: `attach_status_panel()` in `_ready`; `update_status_panel(delta)` after
  `place_rig()` every frame, from `session.current_ship()` (speed, energy fraction,
  `afterburner_active`, `afterburner_locked`), `comfort.number` / `display_name` and
  `BuildInfo.describe()`. `log_afterburner()` prints `Meltdown: afterburner on` / `off`
  when the state changes. Read from state, not `SimEvent`s: `Sim.last_events` holds only
  the last tick of a frame, so events on earlier ticks of a multi-tick frame would be lost.
