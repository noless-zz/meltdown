## Summary

- New `game/net/bot_pilot.gd` (`BotPilot`, extends `ShipInputSource`): built with a `Sim`
  and a ship index; `next_input` reads the sim and returns a `ShipInput`.
- Targets the nearest live robot (ties to the lower index). Pitch and yaw towards it in
  ship space, proportional to the angle off (`TURN_GAIN` 6/s), capped at
  `MAX_PITCH_RATE`/`MAX_YAW_RATE` = pi/2 rad/s, the thumbstick mapping's limits (repeated
  as constants because `net/` may not depend on `xr/`; a test checks they do not exceed
  `ThumbstickMapping`'s). No roll, no afterburner.
- Thrust along the line to the target to hold `ENGAGE_DISTANCE` (15 m): wanted closing
  speed 1 m/s per metre off, full thrust once 4 m/s short; backs off inside 15 m.
- Holds `fire_primary` when the target is within the selected primary's range (speed x
  lifetime) and within `FIRE_CONE_HALF_ANGLE_DEG` (5 degrees) of the nose. Empty slot or
  no target: no fire. No target: zero input.
- `core/` and `local_session.gd` untouched.
