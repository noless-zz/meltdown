## Reading confirmed

Both suspected causes were real. A robot fired whenever it faced the ship, with no line of
sight, and a shot was only dropped once a tick ended outside every box, so it crossed into
any box it landed in, including face-to-face neighbours, and hit-tested its whole segment
before that. Measured on `main` with the bot pilot: on seed 31, 1,178 of 1,192 robot shots
were fired with no line to the ship, 517 shot-ticks crossed a wall, about 25 hits came from
such shots, and the bot was destroyed. Seed 1: 249 of 257 shots fired blind.

## Summary

- New `game/core/line_of_sight.gd`: where a segment leaves open space. A line passes from
  one box into another only through the volume they share; boxes face to face are a wall
  (as the view draws them and as ships feel them). Deterministic, about 11 µs a call on a
  22-box level, with a fast path when one box holds the whole line.
- `projectile_hits.gd`: a shot (robot's or ship's) ends where it leaves open space, and
  the hit tests only see the part before that point.
- `robot_tick.gd`, `robot_state.gd`: robots fire only with a clear line to the ship's
  centre. After `lose_target_seconds` (new optional robot data key, default 2 s) without a
  line the robot drops the target; when it sees the ship again its next shot waits a full
  fire interval. Movement is unchanged. `unseen_ticks` joins the robot hash only while
  non-zero, the new key only when not the default.
- Bot pilot (`bot_flank.gd`, `bot_pilot.gd`): `reaches_walls` uses the same line, so the bot
  no longer shoots at robots through walls. It also stops waiting after 10 s for a robot
  that creeps along the far side of a wall (seed 6 stalled otherwise).
- `acceptance_state_checks.gd` / `acceptance_step.gd`: new `no_hidden_hits` check, which
  re-flies the replay and fails on any robot shot fired with no line or crossing a wall.
- New m3 step `bot_generated_sight` on seed 31, with its recorder in
  `tests/test_m3_sight_step.gd`.
