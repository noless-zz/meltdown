## Summary

- `Sim._advance_projectiles` keeps each shot's position before it moves and tests the
  segment flown this tick against every live robot, before the lifetime and wall checks,
  so a shot on its last tick or leaving through a wall still hits what it passed.
- The nearest entry along the segment wins; only a strictly nearer entry replaces the
  best, so ties go to the lower robot index. A hit removes the shot.
- `RobotState`: `is_alive`, `apply_damage` (never below zero, negative ignored) and
  `segment_entry` (segment against sphere, entry fraction 0..1 or -1).
- Robots at zero hull are removed after the robots move, at the end of the tick, in order.
- `SimEvent.Kind` gains `ROBOT_HIT` (owner, damage, unit direction) and
  `ROBOT_DESTROYED` (owner of the killing shot), appended after `WALL_IMPACT`. They are
  collected while projectiles move and appended after the ship loop's events.
- Stale comment on `Projectile.damage` updated.
