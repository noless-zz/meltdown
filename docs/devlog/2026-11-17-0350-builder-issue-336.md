## Summary

- New `game/view/reactor_view.gd` (`ReactorView`, `Node3D`): one `Core` sphere and one
  `Light` omni light (no shadow, GFX-4). `show_state(position, radius, hull_fraction,
  destroyed, meltdown_seconds_left)` takes plain values only. The mesh is a unit sphere built
  once in `_init`; the radius is the node scale, so neither an unchanged nor a changed state
  rebuilds it. A radius of zero or less hides the reactor.
- Intact: the pulse period runs from `pulse_period_full_hull` (3 s) down to
  `pulse_period_no_hull` (0.8 s) as hull drops. Destroyed: the core takes the dim wreck
  colour, the light the meltdown colour, and the flicker runs from `flicker_period_slow`
  (1.2 s) at 90 s left to `flicker_period_fast` (0.4 s), held from 30 s down
  (`FASTEST_UNDER_SECONDS`, same threshold as the status panel in #315).
- The phase advances by fractions of the current period, so a rate change never jumps the
  brightness. Only emission and light energy change; nothing moves (CMF-3).
- New `game/view/reactor_look.gd` (`ReactorLook`) parses `game/view/reactor_look.json`; split
  out so the node stays small. All periods are at least 0.34 s (under 3 flashes a second, the
  usual photosensitivity limit), and the fast period of each pair may not be longer than the
  slow one. A rejected file keeps the built-in look and the warning names the field.
- Still unwired: `main.gd`, `net/` and `xr/` unchanged.
