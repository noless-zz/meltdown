## What moved the panel

None of the Planner's three candidates did it. #1306 (cockpit look) and #1321 (placement
settings) left the corner positions as they were, and the panel still hangs at the head
point with the cockpit frame (`RigViews.place_at`). Two things together moved it:

1. **#1356 (tally, merged 25 Nov, first in the 26 Nov build) grew the panel upward.** The
   carried and banked lines went above the countdown, at +0.12 and +0.15 m on the panel, so
   they would stay within the 30 degree glance of the *centre* place. In a bottom corner the
   panel's top moved from about 6 degrees below the eye line to the eye line. The carried line
   is the panel's widest row, so it sat over the tunnel mid-left. That matches the owner's
   26 Nov screenshot.
2. **With no saved choice the panel used the centre place, not bottom left.** #1321 said
   "bottom-left stays the default", but `PanelCorner` gave `centre` for a missing or bad file.
   A fresh install, a lost save or a run that starts without the file (acceptance runs) put
   the panel below the middle of the view.

Even before #1356, no part of the bottom-left panel was more than 12 degrees from straight
ahead (the hull bar's end). So criterion 3's 25 degree cone and criterion 2's "same place
as before within 2 cm" cannot both hold with this panel layout. I followed the cone, which is
the owner's complaint, and moved the corners out. The pull request says so.
