ARC-3).

## Summary

- `main.gd` holds the active preset (`comfort_number`, `comfort_preset`) and a
  `ComfortInputFilter`. `apply_controls` maps the values, then filters them with
  `session.current_ship().orientation`, then sets the input.
- Start preset `START_COMFORT_PRESET = 2` (Guided). `--comfort-preset=1..4` (normal or user
  arguments; the last one wins) overrides it in `_ready`; other values give 2.
- The left upper face button (Y; B when left-handed) cycles 1 > 2 > 3 > 4 > 1 once per press
  and prints `Meltdown: comfort preset <n> <name>`. Read from the sampled buttons in
  `main.gd`, not bound in the mapping.
- Frames and ticks do not line up, and the filter fires a snap once per stick push. Without
  care, a snap set on a frame that runs no tick (about one frame in three at 90 Hz) was lost,
  and a hitch frame flew it on every tick. So a fired snap is held until a tick flies it, and
  a frame carrying a snap is split at the next tick boundary: the snap tick runs alone, the
  rest of the frame runs with the snap axis at zero. The resumed frame after a pause still
  runs at most one tick.
- `interpolated_pose` takes a snap angle: while the preset snaps yaw or pitch, a tick that
  turned at least half a snap is shown at the current orientation without slerp; position is
  still interpolated.
- `game/tests/test_main_comfort.gd`: 12 tests.
