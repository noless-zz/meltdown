## Summary

- New `game/net/bot_escape.gd` (`BotEscape`) holds the reactor and escape steps, so
  `bot_pilot.gd` only gained the hook (now 291 lines).
- **When it takes over:** the sim has a reactor, and either the reactor is destroyed or no
  live robot is in reach. A robot is in reach when a shot gets to it or the route joins the
  ship to it. Without a reactor it never takes over, so earlier flights are unchanged.
- **Reactor step:** the bot routes to the reactor with `LevelRoute` and holds 15 m once a
  shot gets there. It fires only when `reactor_shot_reaches` is true, with the nose on the
  reactor's centre. That check is the reactor's version of `shot_reaches`: the shot must
  stay inside the walls up to the sphere's surface.
- **Escape step:** once the reactor is destroyed, the bot routes to `sim.start_positions[i]`
  (the exit). It asks for full thrust and 35 m/s, and holds the afterburner while it is more
  than 2 m/s below the wanted speed. The sim locks the afterburner out when energy runs dry.
  It plans to slow at 15 m/s² before each opening, so it reaches each corner at
  `CORNER_SPEED` plus whatever that braking allows.
- **Robots during the escape:** a robot in sight gets the nose and a shot when it is in the
  cone. Thrust is in ship space, so this never slows the run.
- `BotPilot._turn_towards` is now public, as `turn_towards`, so `BotEscape` can use it.

## Open questions

- Hull ends at 11.9, mostly from the warden's last shot in flight. If #377's sweep shows
  bots dying right after a kill, the bot could keep dodging for a moment after the last kill.
- On generated levels, a robot the route cannot join to the ship is skipped. The bot then
  goes for the reactor while that robot lives.

- **Afterburner reading (for the Planner to tune at G3):** the criterion says "full
  thrust with the afterburner while energy allows". The bot always asks for full thrust,
  but brakes before each opening and holds the afterburner only while it is more than
  2 m/s short of the speed it wants, so it does not burn energy it would brake away. With
  an 83 s margin on G2 that is enough; #377's sweep may show whether it needs to boost
  harder.
