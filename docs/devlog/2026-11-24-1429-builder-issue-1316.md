## Issue

#1316: playtest, controller labels overlap each other and the controller (PLT-5, CMF-8,
ARC-3, ARC-4, DEV-7).

## Why they overlapped

Since #1212 each label was placed in the hand's own frame, beside the part it names, with
rows pushed apart along the hand's y axis. The tests measured that with the hands level.
In a resting grip the hand is tilted, so rows apart on the hand fall on top of each other
in the eye's view, and the column 3 cm from the hand's origin sits inside the Quest 3
controller.

## Summary

- `ControlLabelSet` now stands every label of a hand in one column in a plane square to
  the eye's view (as the billboarded text is), beside the controller. The controller is a
  sphere from data (`controller_centre_m`, `controller_radius_m`); the column's inner edge
  is `gap_m` outside the sphere's outline as the eye sees it. Off the middle of the view a
  sphere's outline is wider than its radius, which `ControlLabelLayout.outline_extent`
  works out from the two tangent planes through the eye. Rows stack in `CONTROLS` order,
  each its text's height (at least `row_height_m`), `row_gap_m` apart. Because all rows
  share one depth, they cannot overlap however the hand is turned. The set turns to the
  camera every frame (`_process` -> `face_global`); the tests call `face` directly.
- Leaders are flat strips `leader_width_m` wide (`ControlLabelLeader`), from each row's
  inner edge to its part, which still moves with the hand.
- Text size is data: the em is `text_deg` (0.9 degrees) at `arm_length_m` (0.6 m), up
  from about 0.86 degrees before.
- The right trigger now reads "fire both" (decision record 0011). X has no label and the
  left menu reads "tap: menu, hold: reset", as before.
- `ControlLabelClearance` measures, from the eye: label boxes against each other, against
  both controllers' spheres, and leader length. It runs at the resting grip
  (`held_hand_m`, `held_pitch_deg`) and with the hands `close_apart_m` (0.2 m) apart,
  then puts the hands back.
- Acceptance: step `control_labels_clear` in `suites/m6/effects.json`, check file
  `acceptance/checks/control_labels_check.gd`. It needs no replay. It is skipped when the
  scene has no rig with both hands.

## Changed tests

- `test_label_layout.gd`, `test_control_label_place.gd`: updated to the column (the
  leader starts at the row's inner edge; the cross is centred within its row, no longer
  over the stick). The "within `max_distance_m` of its part" check became "leader no
  longer than `max_leader_m`" (0.16 m). A column beside a 13 cm controller cannot be
  within 6 cm of every part.
- `test_cockpit_preset1_view.gd`, `test_stick_labels.gd`: the "labels inside preset 1's
  window" checks now hold the hands 20 cm apart instead of 30 cm. With the column outside
  the controller, the widest left row ("tap: menu, hold: reset") reaches 42 degrees at
  30 cm apart, past the 38 degree window. The labels draw without depth test, so they
  stay visible past the window edge. The owner should say whether that matters.
