## Summary

- **Why the room was dark and flat:** the room's lights were on render layer 1 while the
  camera, showing the room, draws only layer 20. Godot culls a light by the camera's mask
  as it does a mesh, so the room's five lights never drew and the room was lit by ambient
  light alone. `HubRoomLights.build` puts each light on the room's layer.
- `view/hub_room_lights.gd` (new): lights read from the file with a `role` (key, fill,
  practical, ship) and a `type` (omni, or spot with `aim_m` and `angle_deg`). No shadows
  (GFX-4).
- `view/hub_room_ship.gd` (new): the optional `ship` object places a `ShipModel` (#1362)
  at a position, turned `facing_deg` off nose-on to the seat, at a scale, on the room's
  layer with its engine at idle. `ship_model.gd` is read, not edited.
- `view/hub_room_surface.gdshader` (new): worn patches, panel seams and bare metal along a
  box's edges, worked out from the position on the part in metres; a material uses it when
  it sets `wear`, `edge` or `panel_m`. Screens keep their plain material.
- `data/hub/room.json`: capsule, wing, canopy, fin and engine props removed, ship at 35
  degrees and scale 1.3 on a cradle turned with it; 7 lights (key spot, fill, ship spot,
  ship rim spot, screens, pad and workbench practicals); ceiling lamp fixtures as the
  brightest glow, away from the stations; the pad's glow lowered under the screens';
  ambient 0.7 to 0.45.
- `HubRoom.cost()` and `MAX_DRAW_CALLS` (80): the shipped room is 52 draw calls,
  19 materials and 7 lights.
