## Issue

#1303: adaptive music, calm, alarm and meltdown layers that crossfade with the run's
state (AUD-3, RUN-2, RUN-6, ARC-1, ARC-4, NAM-3).

## Summary

- `data/audio/music.json`: the music's own `volume_db` (apart from the cues' table),
  `crossfade_seconds` 2.5, `meltdown_seconds` 0.6 (at most 1, enforced),
  `end_fade_seconds` 2.0, and five layers: `calm` (run, from alarm 0), `tension_low`
  (from 0.35), `tension_high` (from 0.7), `meltdown` and `hub`.
- `MusicTable` and `MusicLayer` read and check the file; `MusicMix` gives each layer's
  target from the mood (hub, run, meltdown, ended) and the alarm, and fades every gain at
  the mood's rate, never a cut; `MusicPlayer` (view) has one looping, non-positional
  player per layer on Master (out of the room reverb, as the #1291 devlog asked), joining
  layers at the music clock so they stay on the beat. Headless (dummy driver) it plays
  nothing but still follows the mix.
- `MusicWiring` (beside `main.gd`) reads the mood from session copies: hub while it is
  open, ended once the outcome is not RUNNING (escape or destroyed screen), meltdown once
  the reactor copy says destroyed. `CombatWiring` owns it and calls it in `update`;
  `main.gd` only passes `hub.is_open()` on the existing line (still 300 lines).
- Placeholder music: five 8 s loops at 120 bpm synthesised by `tools/synth_music.py`
  (standard library, seeded), each tone a whole number of cycles a loop so they repeat
  without a click; imported with forward loop. Recorded in `docs/licences.md`.

## Not done: the alarm

The core has no alarm level yet (RUN-6; the #1290 review noted the same), so
`MusicWiring.alarm_level` returns 0 and only the calm layer plays in flight. The tension
layers, thresholds and mix are built and unit-tested, but no replay can raise an alarm.
The acceptance step `music_follows_alarm` (m6 `sound.json`, on `bot_g2_escape.replay`,
hash 2519309766 as in m3) therefore expects `calm`, `meltdown`, `faded` from data in
`acceptance/checks/music.json`; it also checks the meltdown track takes over within one
second of the reactor, that no gain moves faster than a fade, and the pinned hash.
Follow-up #1342 (`triage`) adds the core alarm and the tension moment to the step.
