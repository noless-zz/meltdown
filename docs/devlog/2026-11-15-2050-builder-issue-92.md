## Summary

- New `game/tests/test_comfort_limits.gd` only; no filter or preset change.
- Each preset file flies 20 seeded random sequences of 600 ticks (each axis holds a value
  for 1 to 90 ticks, 30% of holds at zero, afterburner toggled at random) through
  `ComfortInputFilter` with the ship's current orientation, then `Sim.step`. Each preset is
  flown once and every row test reads the recorded first failure (sequence and tick).
- Player roll is checked by feeding a second filter the same input without its roll: the
  outputs must be equal on presets 1 and 2.
- Preset 1 also checks that any auto-level roll levels the ship in that tick (a snap).
- Pitch limit tolerance: one tick of full-stick pitch (1.5 degrees), as in
  `test_comfort_rate_limits.gd`.
- CMF-6: one thrust-only sequence flown through all four presets gives bit-identical speed
  and energy on every tick.

## Test results

Godot 4.7.2-stable, run locally exactly as CI does: 325 tests, 0 failed; the whole suite
takes about 5 seconds. No comfort limit failed, so no `triage` issue was opened.
Mutations of `ComfortInputFilter` checked (each reverted): passing player roll through
(presets 1 and 2 roll fail, and preset 1 pitch exceeds the limit), removing the rate caps,
removing the pitch limit, smooth pitch in preset 1, changing thrust, and auto-level on every
preset (preset 4 fails).
