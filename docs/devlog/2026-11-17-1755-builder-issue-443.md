## Summary

- `LevelRoute.close_blocked` closes every opening between joined boxes that
  `OpeningClearance.blocked` (the test `KnownRoute` uses) finds blocked by solid debris.
  `chain` skips closed openings, and a straight leg must also miss solid debris inflated by
  the ship's radius. Held entries are never solid, so they block nothing.
- `BotEscape` plans the route again when the count of solid entries grows. It only does
  this after the reactor is down, and only on levels that have debris.
- `bot_escape_sweep.gd --debris` reads `data/gen/debris.json` into a `DebrisPlan` and passes
  it to `to_level_def`. The summary ends with `,"debris":true`.
