## Summary

- Five new robot types in `game/data/robots/`, all original (NAM-3). `RobotDef` has no
  `_note` key, so the roles are described here and in the pull request:
  - `needler`: small, fast chaser (chase, hull 12, 22 m/s, 240 deg/s), rapid weak shots.
  - `warden`: big, slow guard that holds its ground (hover, hull 90, 2 m/s, 45 deg/s),
    slow heavy slugs.
  - `skimmer`: medium strafer that circles (strafe, hull 30, 14 m/s), rapid shots.
  - `hauler`: heavy, slow chaser (chase, hull 60, 6 m/s, 60 deg/s), heavy slugs.
  - `glider`: fast strafer (strafe, hull 40, 18 m/s), fast short-range lance bolts.
- Three new weapon files that parse with the unchanged `WeaponDef`: `needle_gun`
  (every 4 ticks, 3 damage), `slug_thrower` (every 40 ticks, 30 damage, slow),
  `arc_lance` (every 20 ticks, 14 damage, fast and short-lived). Four weapon ids across six
  types; each robot drops the weapon it fires (SLV-1).
- Every pair of types differs in hull, radius, max speed and turn rate, so well over the
  two-field minimum. `robot_def.gd` and `weapon_def.gd` are untouched.
- `game/tests/test_robot_roster.gd` (9 tests) and its `.gd.uid`.
