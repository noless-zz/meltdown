## Summary

- New `game/core/meltdown_debris.gd` (`MeltdownDebris`): parses the level's optional
  `debris` list (metres and seconds, held as whole mm and whole ticks), rejecting bad entries
  as `debris <index>: <key problem>`. `after_seconds` must round to at least one tick.
- Timing follows the countdown: an entry with `after_ticks` k is solid from the k-th tick
  after the tick the reactor died on, which is the tick `meltdown_ticks_left` has gone down by
  k. It stays solid. The clock is the only state, so it is the hash (capped at the latest entry).
- Ships: pushed out along the line between centres with the wall rule of
  `BoxBounds.contain_impact` (mirrored overshoot, restitution, wall damage and min speed). A hit
  is reported as a `WALL_IMPACT` event with the outward normal. A ship exactly at the centre
  goes up.
- New `game/core/ship_contact.gd` (`ShipContact`): debris first, then walls, so a push out of
  debris never leaves the level. This also moved the wall block out of `Sim.step`.
- Shots: `ProjectileHits.advance` cuts each shot's segment where it meets solid debris, tests
  hits only on what lies before, and drops the shot. This file was not in the issue's list; it
  is the only place shots are stopped.
- `Sim.set_debris` (before the first tick; an empty list is no debris). `sim.gd` is 295 lines.
- Replay format 15: optional `debris <json>` line after the reactor line (`ReplayDebrisLine`),
  written only when there is debris. `replay.gd` is 298 lines, at the limit its test allows.
- G2: one placeholder entry in the shaft, (-3, -15, -45), radius 1, 10 s after the reactor
  dies. It leaves 7 m beside it on x. Size and timing are the owner's call.
- `LocalSession.for_level` does not pass debris on yet (later lane-b issue), so the bot flight
  and the G2 acceptance step are unchanged.
