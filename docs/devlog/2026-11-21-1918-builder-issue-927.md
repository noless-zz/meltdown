## Summary

- `game/data/run/shafts.json`: `offer_min` 2, `offer_max` 3, an odd `seed_salt`, and three
  kinds in order: `steady`, `deep`, `hot`. Each has a short original `label`, `risk`
  (`robot_count` and `alarm` multipliers) and `reward` (`tier_weights` for component tiers
  1 to 3). Nothing reads the modifiers yet.
- `game/core/shaft_offer.gd` (`ShaftOffer`): parses the file (errors name the key with its
  path, for example `shafts[1].risk.alarm`), `draw(seed)` gives 2 or 3 distinct kinds, and
  `seed_for(base, kind)` gives the seed of a level reached through a kind. Both mixes use
  `RunPlan.fmix32` and masked 32-bit arithmetic and are documented in the class.
- `RunPlan.offer_after(index, shafts)`: the offer after level `index`, drawn from that
  level's seed (so from the run seed and index); none after the last level.
  `RunPlan.level_seed_through(index, shafts, kind)`.
- `RunState` takes an optional `ShaftOffer`. With one, `advance` into a level waits in
  `needs_choice` until `choose(kind)` with an offered kind; `current_level_seed`,
  `current_risk` and `current_reward` then follow the chosen shaft. Without one it behaves
  as before. `state_hash` now includes the choices.
- Nothing in the game uses `RunPlan` or `RunState` yet, so replay and suite hashes cannot
  change.
