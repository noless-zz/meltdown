## Summary

- `view/guide_light_rules.gd`: parses the new optional `guide_lights` section of
  `view/level_look.json` (spacing, dash length, width, inset, margin, energy, tunnel width,
  openings that make a junction, colour per kind). A bad key names its field. Without the
  section nothing is drawn.
- `view/guide_light_placement.gd`: places strips from the boxes and the openings that
  `LevelFaces.face_cells` already finds. Tunnels get dashed strips along their length. Rooms,
  junctions and the reactor room are outlined on every edge. Every opening is framed by a
  solid strip `margin_m` outside it. Strips are cut away from every opening. Box kinds:
  `reactor` (holds the reactor), `junction` (opens into at least 3 boxes), `tunnel` (middle side
  up to 13 m, the ambient rules' threshold) or `room`. Pure function of the union, so level and
  simulation hashes do not change.
- `view/guide_lights.gd`: the strips of one built level, coloured by kind. `set_on` switches
  the glow through the shader uniform `strip_energy`. #1454 can use the same uniform for the
  emergency state.
- Drawing: strips go into each box's existing bevel surface (`LevelViewMeshes.bevels`), with their
  glow in the vertex colour (black on the bevels). `level_wall.gdshader` adds
  `COLOR.rgb * strip_energy` to the emission. No new draw call, material or light. The ambient
  check's two-surfaces-per-box rule still holds.
- `level_view.gd` +7 lines, `level_look.gd` +2. Both were already at or over 300 lines; #1446
  splits them next.
- Acceptance: `acceptance/checks/guide_lights_check.gd` (lane-a) with `guide_lights_drawn` and
  `guide_lights_brighter`. Two steps in `suites/m6/look.json`: `guide_lights` (stress replay,
  hash pinned as in `perf`) and `guide_lights_g2` (G2 with `bot_g2_level`, hash as in
  `effects`/`wall_depth`). The second step exists because the stress level is a single reactor
  room: it has no tunnel or junction to tell apart or photograph.
