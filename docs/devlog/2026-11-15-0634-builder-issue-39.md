## Issue

#39 Afterburner with a recharging energy pool (FLT-2, FLT-1, ARC-1, ARC-2).

## Summary

- `ShipInput` has an `afterburner` bool, defaulting to off in the constructor and
  `from_floats`.
- `ShipState` has `max_energy`, `energy`, `afterburner_max_speed`,
  `afterburner_acceleration`, `afterburner_drain`, `energy_recharge`,
  `afterburner_restart_energy` and `afterburner_locked`. Defaults: 100 energy, 4 s of
  boost, 8 s to refill from empty, restart at 25. Boost raises acceleration 40 to 80 and
  the cap 20 to 35 m/s.
- When the boost ends the speed is not clamped to the normal cap in one tick; it may not
  grow and damping brings it back within about a second.
- `energy` and `afterburner_locked` are in `ShipState.state_hash()`.
- Replay format version 2 adds a seventh number per ship (0 or 1). Version 1 still loads
  with the afterburner off. `basic_flight.replay` stays a version-1 file; its hash changed
  from 4190313060 to 861662111 only because energy is hashed (checked: the old hash
  formula still gives 4190313060 on this branch, and energy ends at 100).
