## Summary

- `data/hub/room.json` has a new `details` list of 58 primitive pieces and 5 worn materials:
  - ribbed wall panels and rails;
  - floor markings around the cradle and in front of the shaft;
  - cable runs;
  - a gantry with a hoist over the ship;
  - a tool rack with tools;
  - a framed shaft mouth with a dim amber rim.
- `view/hub_room_details.gd` (new) merges the details into one mesh per material. Each vertex
  keeps its piece's position, normal and size in the custom channels, so the worn surface
  still finds the piece's edges and seams. The wear code is now in `hub_room_wear.gdshaderinc`,
  shared by the single-part shader and the new detail shader.
- `view/hub_room_sight.gd` (new): the spec rejects any prop or detail on the line from the eye
  to a station's screen, widened by 0.15 m.
- `HubRoom.MAX_DRAW_CALLS` went from 80 to 60. The room took 52 draw calls before and 58 after.
  No lights were added: there are still 7.
- The `hub_room` acceptance check now saves a wide view from the seat and a view towards the
  ship. `suites/m6/base.json` is unchanged because the step itself did not change.
- The shaft mouth is in the far wall behind the ship's tail. The ship faces the seat at 35°,
  so the space ahead of its nose is where the stations stand.
