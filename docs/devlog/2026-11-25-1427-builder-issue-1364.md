## Issue

#1364: room-and-portal visibility, draw only the current room and rooms seen through
openings (GFX-3, PRF-2, PRF-4, PRF-5, ARC-1). The only ready lane-b issue.

The issue is too large for one run, so this PR does the level part (routine step 8): the
per-box level view, the visibility search, the per-frame culling, the tests and the
acceptance step. Hiding robots, pickups and effects with their box went to `triage` issue

## Summary

- `view/level_visibility.gd` (`LevelVisibility`, pure): the boxes and the openings between
  them (every pair sharing volume, as `LevelFaces` leaves open). From each box holding the
  eye, a search passes through an opening only when it lies inside the window of view
  tangents left by the openings already passed, and narrows the window to it. Conservative
  throughout: openings grown by `margin_m` (each eye's offset and head motion), the opening
  cut at `near_m` and projected exactly beyond, no narrowing when the eye is so close to an
  opening that the cut could lose a line, a box searched again only with a window not
  inside one it already had, every box drawn past `max_steps` or with the eye outside
  every box.
- `view/level_visibility.json` + `LevelVisibilitySettings`: `enabled` (the dev switch),
  window 1.8 by 1.6 tangents (about 122 by 116 degrees, wider than a Quest 3 eye pair),
  `near_m` 0.02, `margin_m` 0.15, `max_steps` 4096. A bad file names the field and falls
  back to the defaults.
- `LevelView` builds one node per box (`Box<i>`) holding its walls, frames and bevels.
  Frames are drawn with the box they sink into (`LevelView.frame_box`), since a frame
  lies behind its face. Each frame `_process` (priority 1000, after the rig) reads the
  viewport's camera and shows only the visible boxes, counting boxes and surfaces drawn.
  `set_culling(false)` shows all. Mesh building moved to `LevelViewMeshes`.
- Acceptance: step `portal_visibility` in `suites/m6/effects.json` on
  `bot_g2_level.replay` (hash 802400918, unchanged). The new `portal_visibility_counted`
  check re-flies it, culls a view built from the flight's walls with the eye at ship 0
  each tick, and compares with culling off.

Generated levels: eye at each box centre looking along each of the six axes. The test
tunnel is its own scene, not drawn by `LevelView`, so it is unchanged (its draw calls are
what they were). Before this change the whole level was one mesh of 3 wall surfaces and
one bevel surface: 4 draw calls but every triangle every frame. Split per box, a box
costs up to 4 surfaces, so walls alone now cost more draw calls than before, with about a
fifth of the triangles. The gain comes when M6 puts materials, effects and models in every
room. A follow-up can bring a box to 2 surfaces (tint chosen by normal in the wall shader,
which already tells floor, wall and ceiling apart).
