## Summary

- New `game/map_route_wiring.gd` (`MapRouteWiring`), owned by `MapWiring` as `route_wiring`.
  While the meltdown runs (reactor destroyed, outcome `RUNNING`) it calls
  `EscapePath.known_route` for the local ship: the explored boxes, the ship's snapshot
  position and radius, the exit from the replay's start line (where the exit marker is drawn)
  and a copy of the simulation's debris. It hands the result to
  `MapHologram.show_route` in metres.
- At most one search every 15 ticks (`ROUTE_EVERY_TICKS`), and at once when a box is explored
  or the count of solid debris grows (the only way an opening becomes blocked). Before the
  reactor dies and after escape or loss the route is cleared; a new session clears it too.
- Why a new file although `map_wiring.gd` would have stayed under 300 lines: the existing
  `test_main_map.gd::test_the_wiring_reads_only_session_copies` forbids `map_wiring.gd` from
  naming `sim.`, and the meltdown state (reactor, outcome, debris) lives only on the
  simulation. The new file reads it the way `MeltdownWiring` does: the reactor through
  `SessionSnapshots.reactor`, the outcome through the read-only `Sim.outcome`, and the debris
  through `MapRouteWiring.debris_copy`. `map_wiring.gd` is 139 lines; `main.gd` untouched.
