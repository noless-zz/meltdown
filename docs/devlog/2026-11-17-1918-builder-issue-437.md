## Summary

- `game/data/gen/debris.json` gains `latest_fall_fraction`, shipped as 0.8. It is required
  like the other keys: missing is named, and anything but a number 0.1..1 is rejected with
  the key named.
- `DebrisPlan.fall_limit_ticks(level)` is that fraction of
  `ReactorState.meltdown_ticks_for_path` for the level's reactor-to-start `EscapePath`
  (the unscaled countdown if there is no route), at least 1 tick.
- `DebrisPlan.place`: a fall time drawn past the limit is **drawn again** in
  `lowest after_seconds..limit`, from its own generator seeded from `output_hash` and
  "debris fall". It is clamped to the limit only when even the lowest `after_seconds` is
  later. The main draws do not change, so spots, radii and entry counts stay the same.
  Fall times inside the limit stay the same too. With no reactor there is still no debris.
- `debris_plan.gd` is 285 lines (was 243).

  difference, but it is not tested.
