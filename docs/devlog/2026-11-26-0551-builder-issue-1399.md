## Summary

- `tools/synth_room_tones.py` writes six 4 s loops: tunnel (air and faint drips), room
  (machinery hum), chamber (deep rumble and distant clanks), reactor pulse, meltdown
  rumble and hangar. Re-running writes identical files. Licence row added.
- `data/audio/room_tones.json`: the crossfade time (1 s), the spaces by box span (tunnel
  from 0 m, room from 10 m, chamber from 16 m), the reactor pulse's near and full
  distances, and every file and level. `RoomToneTable` checks every key and names the bad
  field; a rejected table leaves the tones silent.
- `RoomToneMix` (pure): one bed at a time (the space of the box whose sound is heard, as
  the reverb picks it; the meltdown rumble once the reactor is gone; the hangar at the
  base; nothing on end screens), crossfaded linearly over the table's second, plus the
  reactor pulse by distance before the meltdown.
- `RoomTones` (node): one non-positional looping player per layer, on Master like the
  music so the room reverb is not applied twice; players start when a gain leaves 0.
- Wired through `MusicWiring` beside the engine, sharing its moods and its new-level
  reset. `CombatWiring` and `main.gd` are untouched.
- m6 `sound.json`: new step `room_tones` on the G2 escape flight with the new check
  `room_tones_change`.
