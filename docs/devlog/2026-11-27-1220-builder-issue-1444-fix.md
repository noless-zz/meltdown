## Summary

- `AcceptanceTimeLimit.seconds_for` now adds `flight_seconds(step)`: the ticks of the step's
  `replay` and `next_replay` at `SLOWEST_HZ` = 72 Hz, the lowest rate the headset may fall back
  to (PRF-1). The runner flies one tick per frame, so without this `m4/shaft_rewards` (about
  16,960 ticks, about 236 s at 72 Hz) and every `crew_joins` step (about 10,500 ticks, about
  146 s) would time out on the device. A looped replay (the soak) adds nothing: its flying time
  is its `measure_seconds`, already counted. A step's own `time_limit_seconds` still wins.
- The runner works out the step's deadline once when it starts
  (`AcceptanceTimeLimit.deadline_msec`), since the limit now reads replay files; the clock is
  read after the files, so reading them is not charged to the step. The runner stays at 258
  lines, under `test_runner_and_suite_files_stay_short`'s 260.
- New test `test_the_default_leaves_room_for_the_replays_flight_at_72_hz` loads the real m4
  suite and requires `shaft_rewards` and `crew_joins` to get at least the default plus their
  ticks ÷ 72, with a pinned minimum tick count so an uncounted replay cannot pass. The
  timed-out-replay test now expects the limit including the flight time.
