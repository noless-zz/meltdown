## Summary

- `xr/map_choice.gd` (`MapChoice`): the two settings, saved in `user://meltdown_map.json`
  as `{"place": "hand" | "cockpit", "turn": "fixed" | "follows_ship"}`, the way
  `PanelCorner` keeps the corner. No file (an older install) gives hand and fixed, today's
  map. A bad file or value is ignored with a warning.
- Two menu rows in the Display section after Panel corner: **Map place** (hand / cockpit)
  and **Map turn** (fixed / follows ship). New entry helper `dev_menu_map.gd`; one line in
  `dev_menu_wiring.gd`. No new button (the Planner's choice on the issue).
- Each cockpit file has a `map_mount`: `{"yaw_deg": 27, "pitch_deg": -10, "distance_m":
  0.4, "size_m": 0.075}`, parsed by `CockpitMapMount`. The spot is on the right; when the
  panel is in a right-hand corner the map moves to the mirrored spot on the left. In the
  cockpit the map is fitted into a 7.5 cm cube instead of the hand's 25 cm, so it fits
  between the panel and the edge of preset 1's window.
- `MapPlacement` mounts the map under the `CockpitFrame` node (which the view guard
  places at the eye point), or leaves it to the hand logic. `CockpitFrame.set_spec` now
  keeps a mounted map when a preset change rebuilds the frame.
- *Follows the ship*: the hologram is turned by the inverse of the ship's orientation, so
  the ship marker always points ahead. *Fixed* is today's behaviour.
- `MapPanelClearance`: seen from the eye, a mounted map (its bounding sphere, so any turn)
  must lie inside the cockpit's window, in front of its shell, and at least 1 degree clear
  of the panel in its corner and of every declared instrument.
- Acceptance: new check `map_places` (`acceptance/checks/map_places_check.gd`) and step
  `map_places` in `suites/m6/ship.json`. On the live scene it cycles 4 presets x 5 corners
  x 2 places x 2 turns (80 cases), sets the choices in memory only, and puts everything
  back.
