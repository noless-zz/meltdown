## Summary

`game/dev_menu_wiring.gd` went from 324 to 236 lines. Each dev menu entry now has a helper
with one shared shape, `DevMenuEntry` (`kinds`, `results`, `closes`, `show_on`, `follow`,
`choose`): `DevMenuCorner`, `DevMenuControls` (Controls, Hands, Pitch), `DevMenuComfort`,
`DevMenuBreak`, `DevMenuUpdate`, `DevMenuRestart` (Level, Restart level, Flight test, and the
fade), and the existing `DevMenuRefresh` and `DevMenuSwivel`, which now extend `DevMenuEntry`.
The Close entry, and Refresh without a refresh rate, are plain `DevMenuEntry`s that take their
result and do nothing, as before. `apply` hands a result to the helper that takes it; a new entry
is one helper file and one line in the wiring's `entries` list.

The names `main.gd` and the tests use (`corner_store`, `controls_store`, `scheme`, `mapping`,
`pick_seed`, `alpha`, `refresh`, `swivel`, `comfort`, `break_prompt`, `_break_shown`,
`_comfort_shown`, `FADE_SECONDS`) stay on the wiring, forwarding to the helpers. `main.gd`
is unchanged. No behaviour change intended.
