## Summary

- New `data/audio/menu_sounds.json`: `open`, `point`, `change`, `confirm`, `refused`, `back`
  and `launch`, each with its own `volume_db` and `min_gap_s`, all reusing the base's
  `hub_*.wav` files. `confirmed` names a sound for what a confirm returns (`close` is `back`;
  `level`, `restart`, `flight_test` and `return_to_base` are `launch`). Anything else plays
  `confirm`.
- New `xr/menu_sound_table.gd` (`MenuSoundTable`) reads the file. An unknown field, a missing
  sound, a wrong type or a `confirmed` entry naming no sound rejects the whole file, names the
  field, and leaves the menu silent. `event()` decides the sound from the menu's state before
  and after the frame, the actions newly pressed and what the menu returned:
  - closed → open: `open`; open → closed: `back`, or the confirm's named sound.
  - into a section: `confirm`; a Back row: `back`.
  - highlight moved: `point`; the highlighted row's words changed: `change`, or `confirm`
    when A did it (arming Return to base).
  - a press that changed nothing (left or right on a row with no value, a locked value):
    `refused`.
  - with nothing pressed, only open and close sound, so an update hiding a row is silent.
- New `xr/menu_sounds.gd` (`MenuSounds`) hangs under the `DevMenu` panel, so sounds come from
  where the menu floats, on the existing `Effects` bus. It and its voices are processed
  always. Its clock is the wiring's steady time, so sounds still play while the open menu
  pauses the session. It keeps a requested-sound log for tests and checks.
- `dev_menu_wiring.gd` grows by the call: the menu state is taken before the tap and stick
  act, the result is kept, and `sounds.hear(...)` runs each frame. It goes from 290 to 297
  lines, under the 300 its size test checks. The call is in `read`, which runs in the mine,
  at the base, at the shaft choice and on the end screens, so the sounds play in all four.
- Acceptance: new part `suites/m6/menu.json` with step `menu_sounds`, added to `m6.json`'s
  include list and to the lanes' list in `acceptance/checks/README.md`. New
  `acceptance/checks/menu_sounds_check.gd`: after `basic_flight.replay` it taps the menu
  open, steps down twice, opens the Mine section, changes the Level value and taps it closed,
  all through `apply_controls` and `advance_frame`. The log must read exactly
  `open, point, point, confirm, change, back`.
