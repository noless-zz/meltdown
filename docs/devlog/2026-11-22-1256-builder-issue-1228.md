## Issue

#1228: the ESCAPED end screen is drawn twice and the level flashes back behind it
(RUN-3, CMF-8, PLT-2, ARC-3, DEV-7).

## Summary

- **Root cause of the flash, found and reproduced headless:** `DestroyedSequence`
  timed the fade out by `elapsed`, which `_enter` sets back to 0 when the prompt
  appears (CAN_RESTART). So at the end of the hold (3 s) the fade dropped from 1 to
  0 in one frame and faded out again over a second: the level, panel and map came
  back behind the text, then black again. Both end screens share the sequence, so the
  destroyed screen had the same fault. The fade out is now timed by a new
  `dark_seconds`, counted from the destruction through CAN_RESTART; `elapsed` still
  times the hold and the fade back in, so `StartChoice.open_hub` is unchanged.
- **The second ESCAPED** is the status panel's outcome row. New `EndScreenCover`
  (`game/end_screen_cover.gd`) hides the status panel and the map hologram through
  their `visible` while an end screen is up, then puts each back as it was. View
  only (ARC-3). It hides on every frame because the dev menu shows the panel again
  on every frame it is closed (#1206). The nodes are found by name under the rig,
  so `main.gd`, `dev_menu_wiring.gd` and the `xr/status_panel*` files (lane-b) are
  untouched.
- `DestroyedWiring` drives the cover (`end_screen_up()`: either screen's line, or the
  shaft panel after an escape) and records `fade_dropped` when the fade it hands out
  goes down while a screen is up; `reset` restores the panel and clears it.
- The `escape_screen` acceptance check also fails when the fade went down, or when
  the panel or the map is visible while the screen shows.
- Tests: new `tests/test_escape_screen_once.gd` (escape and destroyed screens in the
  main scene stepped frame by frame for 5 s: one end text, panel and map hidden,
  fade never down; panel back after the press; the cover restores each node as it
  was), and a new case in `test_destroyed_sequence.gd` (fade never down before the
  press, also with a hold shorter than the fade and with no fade).
