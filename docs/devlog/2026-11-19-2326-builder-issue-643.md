## Summary

- New check `frame_budget` (`acceptance_frame_budget_check.gd`) and a step of that name at the end
  of `suites/m1.json`: flies `replays/frame_budget_flight.replay` (test tunnel, no robots, a slow
  yaw and pitch sweep with a gentle drift forward and back, 3000 ticks) and measures each frame's
  duration for `measure_seconds` (30) after `settle_seconds` (2).
- Judged against the rate in use read back by `RefreshRate`, never the requested one. Below 90 Hz
  it fails with the rate. Otherwise it reports mean, p95, p99, frames over budget and their share,
  and missed refreshes (`FrameStats` dropped, 1.5 frame times); it passes when the p99 is within the
  frame time plus `budget_slack_ms` (1.0) and the share over that is within
  `max_over_budget_share` (0.01). All four values are in the suite file.
- Only on the Quest (OpenXR initialised and Android). Monado, the simulator, desktop and headless
  report skipped with the reason; a `required` step fails instead, as the other headset checks do.
- The numbers go into the step's measurements and a `summary` measurement. `Format-ResultTable`
  in `tools/acceptance_common.ps1` now shows a passing step's `summary`, so the device report
  table shows them on a pass as well as on a failure.
- `acceptance_runner.gd` was 305 lines: the `xr_session` check moved to
  `acceptance_xr_session_check.gd`, report writing and result combining to `AcceptanceReport`, the
  suite-readable test to `AcceptanceSuite.unreadable`. Now 290 lines with the new step.
- `FrameStats` gained `p95_ms`, `percentile_ms` and `count_over`; `p95_ms` is in its measurements.

## Open questions

- The 1.0 ms slack is a guess: Godot's process delta jitters around the display interval. The
  owner's first run will show whether 1.0 ms is too loose or too tight; it is one number in
  `m1.json`.
- Off the headset the step still flies its 3000-tick replay (about 50 s on a 60 Hz desktop). It
  could skip the flight when nothing is measured; left as is to keep the runner simple.
