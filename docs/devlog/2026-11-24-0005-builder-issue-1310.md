## Summary

The issue was too large for one run. This run did the reactor; meltdown fire and debris
went to #1331 (labelled `triage`).

- `view/reactor_look.json` has a new optional `machine` object, parsed by
  `ReactorMachineLook`. Every key inside it is optional and falls back to its default.
  Without the object the reactor is the bare glowing sphere it was before.
- `ReactorMachine` builds the machine once from primitives: a cage of ribs between two
  caps, a smaller core glowing through the gaps, and coolant pipes. All materials are
  physically based text materials. Sizes are fractions of the reactor's radius.
- Pipes run out to the walls: `MeltdownWiring` hands the level's `BoxUnion`, read only,
  to `ReactorView.fit_pipes` once per reactor position. The search walks each pipe's
  direction in 0.25 m steps up to `pipe_max_length_m`.
- Wreck: `breached_ribs` ribs are hidden, the rest and the caps turn scorched, the top cap
  tilts by `wreck_cap_tilt_deg`, and GPU particle flames and embers (`ReactorFlames`) rise
  from the core. The particles are additive billboards with a soft round dot generated in
  code. There is no full-screen pass, no added light and no added shadow.
- The pulse, the period driven by hull and the meltdown flicker are unchanged. Pausing
  stops the particles (`speed_scale` 0) along with the pulse.
