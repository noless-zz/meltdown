## Issue

#651 (playtest, M1): the view goes black every 20 to 30 seconds until the comfort preset is
changed. CMF-1, CMF-8, GFX-4, ARC-3, PRF-1.

## Summary

- `ViewGuard` (`xr/`): when the head looks further past the window than `guard_margin_deg`,
  or sits further than `guard_distance_m` from the frame's centre, for `guard_after_s`, the
  frame and panel are re-placed at the head's point and heading. A tracking regain does the
  same at once; a recentre puts them back at the head point. The rig is never moved, so
  the world stays where the ship puts it (CMF-8). Values in each cockpit file: 40 degrees,
  3 s, 0.45 m.
- `ViewLog` (`xr/`): a once-a-second `MELTDOWN_VIEW` line (head-to-window angle, yaw,
  pitch, eye offset, shake offset, frame age and why it was placed, re-placements, preset,
  hull, tracked) and a line on each preset change, only with `--view-log` or the
  `meltdown/debug/view_log` setting.
- `RigViews.place_guarded`; `main.gd` changes one line in `place_rig` (still 292 lines).

## Cause

Not confirmed without the headset. The code rules out the hit shake, auto-level, snap
turning and preset changes as ways to separate head and frame: all act on the rig, which
carries both, and a new 60 s test with hits from every side on each preset shows the eye
stays at the frame's centre. Two facts argue against the "inside the shell" reading: the
shell is unshaded (its colour would be about (20, 26, 33) and show no light), and a preset
change rebuilds the frame at the same point. Follow-up #664 (`triage`) asks for a
`--view-log` log from the owner.
