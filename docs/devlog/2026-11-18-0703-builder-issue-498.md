## Summary

- New `game/net/fire_detour.gd` (`FireDetour`): given the waypoints `LevelRoute` would fly,
  it checks each leg against fire entries that burn, or will burn by the time the ship reaches
  the end of that leg at a planning speed. If one does, a Dijkstra over the opening centres
  finds the shortest route whose legs cross no such fire, and it replaces the planned one when
  at most the detour factor times as long. Closed openings stay closed; fire holding the ship
  or the exit is ignored.
- `LevelRoute.weigh_fire(debris, speed, factor)` turns this on; without it `waypoints` is
  unchanged. `BotEscape` calls it once after the reactor dies, only if the debris has fire.
  `FIRE_DETOUR_FACTOR` 1.5 and `FIRE_PLAN_SPEED` 15 m/s are bot constants in `bot_escape.gd`.
- `KnownRoute`, `DebrisRouteGuard`, `core/`, `gen/` and data are untouched. The bot still
  writes only `ShipInput`.
