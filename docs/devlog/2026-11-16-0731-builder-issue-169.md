## Summary

- `LocalSession.frame_events()`: the `SimEvent`s of every tick of the latest `advance`, in
  order, returned as a copy; empty after an advance that ran no tick. The simulation is not
  changed.
- `HitShake.feed(events, ship, ship_basis)`: the hit side is read against the ship's own
  right (`ship_basis.x`) through the new static `roll_sign_for`. A wall on the ship's right
  (normal pointing back to its left, per `SimEvent`) rolls the view left (camera top towards
  its left), whichever way the ship faces.
- `ComfortWiring` owns one `HitShake` from `HitShake.load_file()`; `set_preset` and `_init`
  keep its `preset` on the active one. A rejected file prints one line and leaves it null,
  so the shake stays off. `feed_hit_shake` and `advance_hit_shake` keep `shake_offset`.
- `main.gd`: every `session.advance` (both halves of a split frame) feeds the frame's events
  and the ship's orientation; `advance_frame` advances the shake (by 0 while paused, so a
  paused view holds still), and `place_rig` applies `pose * shake_offset` before the head
  recentre.
