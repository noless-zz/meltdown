## Summary

- `CockpitFinish` (`xr/cockpit_finish.gd`) reads an optional `"finish"` object in each
  cockpit file: per surface (`frame`, `ledge`, `pillars`, `rim`) roughness, metallic,
  `seam_depth_m`, `bolt_height_m` and `wear`; beside them `wear_scale_m` and the glass's
  `glass_fresnel`, `glass_fresnel_power`, `glass_fresnel_color`. Every key is optional;
  a missing key, surface or object takes its neutral value, which is today's look.
- `cockpit_surface.gdshader`: roughness and metalness from the finish; seams pressed in
  and bolts domed in the shading normal (bump from the screen-space slope of a computed
  height, faded out with the pattern so it never shimmers); worn patches from value noise,
  lighter, smoother and more metallic. Neutral values draw exactly as before.
- `cockpit_glass.gdshader`: the flat tint plus a reflection that follows Schlick's curve
  toward the window's edge. Straight ahead the opacity is still `glass_tint_alpha`; at
  most 0.3 more at the edge. With `glass_fresnel` 0 the glass stays the old flat material.
- `CockpitDashPiece` (`xr/cockpit_dash_piece.gd`) reads an optional `"dash"` list of boxes
  and knobs placed by angle like the frame, with a tone and an optional lamp colour.
  `CockpitSpec` rejects a piece that reaches into the window, past the shell or nearer
  than 0.3 m, so window sizes and guard values are untouched (pinned in a test).
- Presets 1 to 3 get a raised console on the left of the ledge with vent slats, a switch
  row, two lamps and two knobs, and a small block far right. Presets 1 and 2 keep the calm
  rule: no bolts, shallow seams, light wear. Presets 3 and 4 have bolts and the glass
  reflection (0.08). Preset 4 has no ledge, so no dash.
- Owner's map idea (23 Nov comment): no dash piece reaches yaw 0 to 35 degrees on the
  dash, right of centre, away from the bottom-left status panel; a test keeps it free.
  The map itself is not moved.
- Instruments (#1216): the files still declare none, and their parsing is unchanged.
- Acceptance: `suites/m6/ship.json` step `cockpit_look` flies the test-tunnel sweep
  starting on preset 1 and switching to 2, 3 and 4 at ticks 750, 1500 and 2250, with
  screenshots at 700, 1450, 2200 and the end. Its hash is the sweep's own, 1960961122.

## Open questions

- Values are first guesses from a software renderer; the owner tunes them in a headset.
- The edge reflection is by angle from straight ahead, not by the true view angle on the
  glass, because the glass is a sphere round the eye that every line of sight meets
  square on. A head leaning out of the eye point sees no extra Fresnel.
