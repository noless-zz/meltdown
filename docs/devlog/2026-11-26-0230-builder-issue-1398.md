## Summary

- `tools/synth_engine.py` writes `engine_idle.wav` and `engine_thrust.wav` (2 s loops) and
  `engine_burst.wav` (0.6 s); re-running it writes identical files. Licence row added.
- `data/audio/engine.json`: offset below and behind the seat, fade and smoothing times,
  thrust weight, and the idle, thrust and burst sounds, levels and pitch range. Every key
  is checked by `EngineSoundTable`, which names the bad field.
- `EngineSoundMix` (pure): reads thrust back from two ship copies one tick apart (the
  velocity change with the tick's damping undone, full on the afterburner, none for a
  wreck), speed as a share of afterburner top speed; a smoothed drive the thrust layer's
  volume and pitch follow; a gain that fades in while flying and out otherwise.
- `EngineSound` (node): three `AudioStreamPlayer3D`s at the ship's pose and the table's
  offset, distance attenuation off, on the `Effects` bus; a burst on the local ship's
  `AFTERBURNER_STARTED`. The input has no separate hard-strafe flag, so no strafe burst.
- Wired through `MusicWiring` (owned by `CombatWiring`): it plays in the run and meltdown
  moods and fades out at the base and on end screens; a new level starts it from silence.
  `CombatWiring` and `main.gd` are both at 300 lines and #1406 edits `CombatWiring`, so
  neither changed.
- m6 `sound.json`: new step `engine_sound` on the G2 bot flight with the new check
  `engine_follows_thrust`.
