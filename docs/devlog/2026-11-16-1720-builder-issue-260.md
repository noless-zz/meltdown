## Summary

- New `game/core/projectile_hits.gd` (`ProjectileHits`, `RefCounted`, static functions,
  the same pattern as `SalvagePickup`): `advance(sim, hit_events, killers)` plus the
  private `_hit_robot` and `_hit_ship`, moved verbatim from `Sim` apart from taking the
  sim and the tick's event and killer collections as parameters.
- `Sim.step` calls `ProjectileHits.advance(self, _hit_events, _killers)` where it called
  `_advance_projectiles()`. `sim.gd` goes from 316 to 244 lines.
- Why this split: the projectile step is one cohesive pass (move, hit test, cull) that
  shares no state with the ship loop except the per-tick event list and killer map, and
  it is the largest block. The salvage step is already partly out (`SalvagePickup`) and
  its drop logic is entangled with robot removal.
