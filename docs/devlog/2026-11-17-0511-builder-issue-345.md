Scheduled Builder run in lane-a. The only open pull request, #230, closes #154 (lane-b), so

## Summary

- `ProjectileHits._hit_ship`: a robot shot whose segment enters an intact reactor before it
  enters any live ship is removed on that tick. It does no damage to the reactor and raises
  no event. The reactor is tested after the ships, and only an entry that is strictly nearer
  wins, so a ship wins an exact tie. This matches how robots win ties against the reactor
  for ship shots.
- A destroyed reactor stops nothing on either side. `ReactorState.segment_entry` already
  returns -1 once the reactor is destroyed, so both sides share that check.
- `test_reactor.gd::test_robot_shots_pass_through_without_damage` pinned the old behaviour.
  It is now `test_robot_shots_stop_at_it_without_damage`: the hull is still unchanged, and
  the shot must not get past the reactor.

## Test results

- Godot 4.7.2-stable in the run's scratch space. `--import`, then `run_tests.gd`, exactly as
  CI runs them: 1077 tests, 0 failed. 7 of them are new, in
  `game/tests/test_reactor_blocks_robot_shots.gd`:
  - A drone behind the reactor cannot hit the ship.
  - With the reactor off the drone's line, the drone hits the ship.
  - The reactor's hull is unchanged by the drone's fire.
  - Once the reactor is destroyed, the drone's shots fly past where it was. This is checked
    by where the shots fly, not by hits on the ship: once the reactor is destroyed, a ship
    sitting on its start point counts as escaped and can no longer be hit.
  - On an exact tie (a reactor with the ship's centre and radius), the ship is hit.
  - A replay with a reactor gives the same hash after being written to text and read back.
  - The m1 replay and both m2 suite replays carry no reactor and still end on their pinned
    hashes.
