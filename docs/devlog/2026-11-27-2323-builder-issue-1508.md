## Summary

- `AimTargets.find` now shows the ray in three places only, and also says which one
  (`where`): the home base only while its room holds the camera (`HubRoom.hides_mine`, so
  the mine is not drawn), the shaft choice, and the open dev menu. Everywhere else is flight
  and the ray hides. Before, the base only needed the room to be visible. A room left visible
  while the camera draws the mine could then put the ray over the mine.
- The dev menu shows the ray (criterion 1), fading at its length with no dot. #1470 adds
  landing on the menu's rows.
- `XRRig.pointing_selects` reads the new `AimTargets.selects`, so the open dev menu in flight
  shows the ray but keeps the flight labels on the hands. At the base or the shaft choice the
  base and shaft come first, so opening the menu there changes nothing.
- No simulation state, replay or comfort setting is touched (MP-2, ARC-3).

## Cause

The code path that drew the ray in the mine on the owner's recording was not found. On
`main` the hub hides its room and stations when it launches, and the shaft choice hides its
panel when it closes. The tighter rule covers the one gap found, a visible room that does not
hold the camera. The new acceptance step pins the behaviour from launch through 120 ticks of
flight. The headset check stays the proof.
