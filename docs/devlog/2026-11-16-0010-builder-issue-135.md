## Summary

- `game/xr/flight_test/plan.json`: six segments, 110 s in total: snap turns (preset 1),
  capped yaw and capped pitch (2), full yaw and full pitch (3), manual roll (4). Lengths
  are guesses for the owner to tune.
- `game/xr/flight_test_plan.gd` (`FlightTestPlan`) parses it. A missing, unknown or
  wrong-type key, an empty or duplicate id, a preset outside 1..4 or not whole, a duration
  not above 0 or over 60 s, an unknown manoeuvre, a preset lower than the segment before,
  or more than 120 s in total is rejected, with the segment index and field named.
- `game/xr/flight_test_segment.gd` (`FlightTestSegment`) holds one segment, kept in its
  own file per the one-class-per-file rule.
- `game/xr/preset_recommender.gd` (`PresetRecommender`): `recommend(plan, answers)` gives
  the preset of the last `fine` before the first `uneasy` or `stop`, and 1 when there is no
  `fine`. Unknown answers (including a different case), answers after `stop`, more answers
  than segments, or a rejected plan return `INVALID` (0); `check` names the problem.
- Nothing reads `core/`; nothing is wired into a scene (out of scope).
