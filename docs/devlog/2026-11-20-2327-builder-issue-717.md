## Summary

- New `game/acceptance/acceptance_controllers_check.gd` (`AcceptanceControllersCheck`): reads
  each hand's tracker (tracked = the `default` pose has tracking data) and the interaction profile
  the runtime reports for it, and the project's action map as "action path" pairs. Every control
  `thumbsticks_default.json` binds with a non-zero weight maps to the OpenXR action
  `ControllerSampler` reads (stick to `primary`, trigger, grip, lower and upper buttons to
  `ax_button` and `by_button`, stick click to `primary_click`) and must have a binding on that
  hand in that hand's profile. Measurements: `controllers_profile` (left, right) and
  `controllers_unbound` (`fire_primary (right_trigger)` style). Skipped without an OpenXR runtime,
  failed when the step is `required`.
- `AcceptanceStep.CHECK_CONTROLLERS_BOUND` added to `KNOWN_CHECKS`.
- `AcceptanceRunner`: dispatches the check through a public `controllers` field (tests replace
  it), and waits up to `XR_WAIT_FRAMES` for both hands to be tracked and named before judging,
  so a step started in the first frames of a session does not fail on controllers still waking.
  The `no_script_errors` body moved into `AcceptanceErrorLog.judge` so the runner stays at 299
  lines.
- `m0.json`: new step `controllers_bound` after `stereo_session`.
