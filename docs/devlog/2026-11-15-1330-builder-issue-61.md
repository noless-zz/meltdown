## Summary

- `game/xr/controller_sampler.gd` (`ControllerSampler`): reads `primary`, `trigger`, `grip`,
  `ax_button`, `by_button` and `primary_click` from two `XRController3D` nodes through two
  injected callables (`tracking`, `read`) into a values Dictionary: two sticks, two
  triggers, two grips and the `ThumbstickMapping.BUTTONS` names (A/X lower, B/Y upper, stick
  click). An untracked hand reads idle. `for_controllers()` builds the real reader;
  `idle_values()` and `map_values()` are shared by both samplers.
- `game/xr/keyboard_sampler.gd` (`KeyboardSampler`): physical keys passed in become the same
  values. WASD forward/strafe, R/F up/down, arrows pitch/yaw, Q/E roll, Shift afterburner.
  Opposite keys cancel and diagonals are limited to a unit stick.
- `game/main.gd`: `_process` samples the controllers while an OpenXR session runs (between
  `session_begun` and `session_stopping`) and the keyboard otherwise, maps through one
  `ThumbstickMapping`, and sets the result on `LatestInputSource` once per frame.
  `apply_controls()` and `sample_controls()` are public for tests.
- `game/tests/test_input_samplers.gd`: 11 tests.
- `thumbstick_mapping.gd` (lane-c) is unchanged.
