## Summary

#604 had drawn the drone and hauler and the wind-up glow; this run did the glider, needler,
skimmer and warden. The wind-up glow already covers every armed type, so nothing was added
for it.

- `view/robot_look.json`: new silhouettes, all built from body stretch and parts.
  - glider: thin fuselage under a wide flat wing, tail fin and wingtip pods (wide and flat);
    banks hard into a sideways move (roll 3.5 deg per m/s, up to 45 degrees).
  - needler: long thin body with a needle out front and cross tail fins (long and narrow,
    scale 1.4 so it is over a metre at 20 m); dives nose down as it closes (pitch 3 deg per
    m/s, up to 35 degrees).
  - skimmer: flat disc with a low dome and a rim ring (flat and round); stays level and skids,
    turning its nose into the slide (new `yaw_deg_per_mps`, 3, up to 40 degrees).
  - warden: tall block with shoulder pods, a crown cone, a short barrel and a visor eye
    (tall); never leans, and its eye goes down to 15 % once it has no line to its target
    (new `watch_dim`, 0.85, from `unseen_ticks`).
- `view/robot_tells.gd` (`RobotTells`): the lean moved here from `RobotView`, plus the skid,
  the drawn basis and the eye share. `RobotView.lean_degrees` delegates so existing tests and
  callers stay. `robot_view.gd` 287 → 278 lines.
- `view/robot_look.gd`: `tell` takes the two optional keys, defaulting to 0, with ranges.
- Nothing in `core/` changed. The tells read `velocity`, `orientation` and `unseen_ticks`,
  which the session snapshot already copies.
