## Issue

CMB-3, LGT-1, LGT-2, GFX-4, ARC-4, ARC-5, NAM-3).

## Summary

- `view/robot_finish.gd` (`RobotFinish`): parses two new optional objects on each
  `robot_look.json` entry.
  - `finish`: `albedo`, `roughness`, `metallic`, `panel_scale`, `panel_depth`, `wear`.
  - `thrust`: `idle`, `full_mps`, `energy`.
  It also adds a new part flag, `"thruster": true`.
- Missing keys:
  - An entry without `finish` keeps today's glowing body. This includes the `default` look
    used for unknown types.
  - A missing key inside `finish` or `thrust` takes the value from `NO_FINISH` or
    `NO_THRUST`.
- `robot_skin.gdshader`: with `lit_body`, the body is shaded metal.
  - Panel seams and worn patches come from position in the robot's own frame (value
    noise, no texture).
  - Only the skin pattern's lines emit, in the type's `emission_color`. The face plate
    stays dark.
  - The hit flash, damage dim and wind-up glow still act on both albedo and emission.
  - Without `lit_body`, the old code path is unchanged.
- Thrusters: an unshaded emissive material in the type colour. Its energy goes from
  `idle × energy` to `energy` at `full_mps`, using `velocity.length()` of the robot copy
  the view already gets. View only.
- Data changes in `robot_look.json`:
  - Every type gets a finish and 2 or 3 extra parts (plates, fins, thrusters). Glider and
    warden reach 8 parts, and `MAX_PARTS` went from 8 to 12.
  - The hauler's skin pattern changed from `checks` to `dots`. Under a lit body, `checks`
    would have made half the hull glow.
  - Every model still fits inside its collision radius (`test_robot_look_fits`), and the
    outline-proportion test still passes.
- `RobotView.build_mesh` moved to `view/robot_meshes.gd`, with a one-line delegate left in
  `RobotView`, to keep `robot_view.gd` under 300 lines.
- Acceptance: new step `robot_look` in `suites/m6/ship.json`. It runs
  `bot_test_tunnel.replay`, which places all six types 3–26 m ahead for its whole length.
  Screenshots are taken at ticks 60, 200, 290 and at the end. Pinned hash: 517117812.
