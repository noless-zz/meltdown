## Summary

- `view/ambient_rules.gd` (`AmbientRules`, new): parses the optional `ambient_rules`
  section of `level_look.json`. A box's size class comes from its middle side (tunnel up to
  13 m, room up to 30 m, large room beyond), and a reactor rule casts red on boxes whose
  centre is within `reach_m`. A bad rule names its field, for example
  `ambient_rules.sizes[1].energy`.
- `view/ambient_bake.gd` (`AmbientBake`, new): one ambient per box, plus a field over the
  mine. The ambient at a point is a weighted mean of its own box and the boxes overlapping
  it, fading out `blend_m` (4 m) from each. Coincident vertices on either side of an
  opening get the same light, so no seam shows. Walls within `blend_m` of a differently lit
  neighbour are cut into pieces of at most `piece_m` (2 m) so the blend has vertices to show
  on. Everything else stays whole. Input is the union, the rules and the reactor position
  only, so the same level bakes the same.
- `level_wall.gdshader`: `render_mode ambient_light_disabled`. The shader adds
  `albedo * (1 - metallic) * ambient` itself, as Godot does. Without a bake that is the
  look's global ambient (`ambient_light`, the fallback); with one it is the per-vertex
  CUSTOM0 value. CUSTOM0 is RGB float, because 8-bit vertex colour bands at these dim values.
- `LevelLook` accepts `ambient_rules` and passes it on. A bad section no longer rejects
  the whole look; `LevelView.ambient_error` names it and the walls keep the global ambient.
- `LevelView.build(union, reactor)`: `level_switch.gd` passes the reactor's position. The
  box-mesh helper moved to `LevelViewMeshes.add_box`, and `LevelView.triangles` delegates
  to `LevelViewMeshes.quad_triangles`, keeping the node near 300 lines.
- Surfaces per box are unchanged (walls and bevels, two draw calls), and no light is added.
  Vertex counts grow only near openings. The `wall_depth_shown` check and two tests now
  compare against `LevelView.wall_pieces()` instead of one quad per wall.
- New check `ambient_tints_baked` (`acceptance/checks/ambient_bake_check.gd`) on the m6
  `wall_depth` and `portal_visibility` steps. It requires at least two tints, every vertex
  baked, at most two draw calls per box and no lights.

## A tuning finding

At the global ambient's cap (0.3) a baked ambient changes the dark walls by under 1/255:
my first tuning rendered identical before and after. Rule energies therefore go up to 2.0.
`test_rooms_differ_visibly_and_stay_dark` keeps a tunnel and a room at least 3/255 apart on
a wall, keeps every surface under 60/255 lit by ambient alone, and keeps boxes away from the
reactor under 25/255 (LGT-1). The global `ambient_energy` and its cap are unchanged.
