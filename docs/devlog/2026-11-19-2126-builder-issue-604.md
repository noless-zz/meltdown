## Summary

- `RobotHitTracker` (view): finds hits and kills from the snapshots alone, because
  `ROBOT_HIT` and `ROBOT_DESTROYED` name the ship, not the robot. A hull drop against the
  paired robot of the previous tick is a hit; a robot of the previous tick with no partner
  now was destroyed. Pairing scans forward, so a robot keeps its flash when an earlier one
  is removed and the array shifts. A level reset (previous and current are the same new
  copies) makes no burst.
- Hit flash: the body goes to the flash colour and fades out over `hit_flash_seconds`
  (0.12 s). Below `damaged_below` (half hull) the body dims to `damaged_brightness` and
  sputters darker for a quarter of every 0.6 s (period kept at 0.34 s or more, as the
  reactor's flicker, CMF-3). Destruction: `RobotBursts` draws a sphere that swells to 3.5
  radii and fades over 0.7 s.
- Wind-up glow (#612 merged): body emission grows with `windup_elapsed / windup_ticks`,
  and eye parts brighten with it, for every type whose weapon has a wind-up.
- Silhouettes: look entries may add `stretch`, `parts` (shape, size, offset, eye) and
  `eye_color`. Drone: small sphere inside a flat ring, an antenna, one lit eye on its
  facing. Hauler: long low hull, two side pods, a scoop under the nose, a slit eye. New
  shapes `cylinder`, `cone`, `torus`.
- Tells: an entry's `tell` leans the drawn body with the snapshot velocity, nose down
  when closing and banked into a sideways move. The hauler (chaser) leans up to 22°. The
  drone holds its station and turns its eye on the target; the eye is dark while it
  sleeps (`awake`).
- Parsing moved to `RobotLook`, so `robot_view.gd` stays under 300 lines. Robot nodes are
  still `MeshInstance3D`, carrying the pose; the body and parts are their children.
- No change in `core/`, `net/` or `combat_wiring.gd`.
