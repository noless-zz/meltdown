## Summary

- New `view/map_marker_shapes.gd` (`MapMarkerShapes`): three named solid shapes,
  original designs (NAM-3): `diamond` (tall four-sided double point, half as wide as
  tall), `pin` (the miner's square pyramid on its point, #1072) and `flame` (a slender
  three-sided spike pointing up). It lives in `view/` because `view/map_fires.gd` uses
  it and view may not depend on xr.
- `map_look.json` gains `reactor_shape` (diamond), `miner_shape` (pin) and
  `fire_shape` (flame). `MapLook` rejects an unknown name and any two keys naming the
  same shape. The ship keeps its dart (#679), which no key can name.
- The reactor marker is now the diamond (`MapShapes.reactor_marker`, height three
  radii) instead of a ball; it keeps no depth test and its render priority over the
  exit cube (#913). `MapShapes.ball` was unused afterwards and is removed.
- Fires are flames, no longer octahedrons, and their map colour moves from yellow
  (28 degrees of hue from the ship's orange) to blue `[0.2, 0.4, 1.0]`, the colour
  family fire burns in the tunnel (`debris_look.json`). The miner violet moves a
  little redder, `[0.7, 0.3, 1.0]`, to keep clear of the blue.
- New `tests/test_map_marker_shapes.gd`: the four drawn markers have different
  corner counts (dart 7, diamond 6, pin 5, flame 4); each marker draws whatever shape
  its key names; shape sizes and widths; flame points up, pin down; the four colours
  in both the shipped file and the built-in look are at least 40 degrees of hue apart
  (the old yellow fire fails that); bad or shared shape names are rejected by key.
- `test_map_reactor_sight.gd` reads the reactor's material from the mesh surface,
  since the marker is no longer a `SphereMesh`.
