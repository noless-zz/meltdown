## Summary

- **The grid:** the canopy glass was built from 10 degree panels drawn 5% larger than their
  cell so neighbours overlap. On opaque panels that is invisible; on the tinted glass every
  overlap was drawn twice, so a darker line ran every 10 degrees across the window. The glass
  is now one code-built mesh whose triangles share their edges: no overlap, one draw call.
- **Shapes:** the even strut ring is gone. New parts, all boxes in front of the shell: `Ledge`
  (dashboard under the window), `Pillars` (one each side, ledge bottom to rim top) and `Rim`
  (above). New file `game/xr/cockpit_geometry.gd` holds the angle maths; `keep_out` turns
  each piece about the eye until every corner is outside the window. That also fixed the
  shell: its panels next to the window reached up to a degree or so into it.
- **Data:** `xr/cockpits/*.json` gain `ledge_top_deg` (the value that moves the ledge with the
  status panel in #605), `ledge_height_deg`, `ledge_half_width_deg`, `ledge_distance_m`,
  `pillar_width_deg`, `pillar_distance_m`, `rim_height_deg`, `rim_distance_m`,
  `part_depth_m`, `interior_color` and `structure_color`; `strut_width_m` is removed. A shape
  that would reach past the shell is rejected. Presets 1 and 2 have all three shapes, preset
  3 only a ledge, preset 4 none.
- **Depth and tint:** ledge 0.36/0.40 m, pillars and rim 0.44/0.48 m, shell 0.5/0.55 m
  (presets 1/2). The shell is now a dim blue-grey `[0.08, 0.1, 0.13]` instead of
  `[0.16, 0.17, 0.19]`, which was almost the tunnel wall colour `[0.16, 0.15, 0.14]`.
