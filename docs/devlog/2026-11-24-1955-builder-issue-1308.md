## Summary

- New `view/pickup_model.gd` (`PickupModel`): a look file's optional `model` block,
  `{"body": {"albedo", "roughness", "metallic"}, "pieces": [{"shape", "size", "offset",
  "turn"?, "glow"?}]}`, at most 8 pieces. It is parsed with every bad field named and built
  as one primitive per piece: lit metal body pieces, and glow pieces that use the view's
  own glowing material. One shared mesh per shape, one body material per look. It adds
  no light.
- Salvage (`salvage_look.json`): each of the four parts has a `model` shaped like its
  family. Arc lance: housing, thin barrel, two glowing coils and an emitter tip. Needle
  gun: housing, twin needles, glowing magazine. Pulse cannon: housing, wide barrel,
  glowing muzzle ring and core. Slug thrower: heavy housing, thick barrel, grip, glowing
  chamber band. It still spins on its tilted axis, and `SalvageRing` is unchanged.
- Components (`component_look.json`): a new `models` key with one shape per kind.
  Cell salts: a canister with a glowing band. Circuit shards: green board shards with
  glowing traces. Core glass: a glowing crystal cluster on a rock. Scrap alloy: bent
  plates with a glowing seam. The heap glow is softened from 2.5 to 1.8.
- Miners (`miner_look.json`): a new `pod` key, read by the new `view/miner_pod.gd`. It
  draws a cabin with a canopy, skids, a hatch ring, and a pale cyan front window and
  side windows, under a thinner rescue beacon. The window is not orange (#1208). The
  ring and label are unchanged.
- An entry without `model`, a kind without a model, or a look without `pod` is drawn
  exactly as before.
- Acceptance: new step `pickup_look` in `suites/m6/ship.json`. It flies m4's
  `crew_joins` flight (run 12345, drops and rescue on) and pins that step's hash,
  3375022947. Screenshots are taken at ticks 360 (needle gun at 7 m), 990 (cell salts at
  9 m), 1230 (pulse cannon and needle gun), 5670 (arc lance) and 7230 (miner at 8 m).
  Only cell salts drop on that level, so no replay shows the other three kinds.
