## Summary

- `game/xr/hit_shake.gd` (`HitShake`, `RefCounted`): `feed(events, ship)` takes one tick's
  `SimEvent`s and keeps the strongest `WALL_IMPACT` on the player's ship, scaled by impact
  speed and capped at full strength; `advance(delta)` returns the view offset (`Transform3D`)
  for the frame. Shake is a small three-axis wobble whose size is `hit_shake` times the hit
  strength, limited to `max_offset_m`; with `hit_spin` the view also rolls by up to
  `max_roll_deg`, away from the side that was hit. Both fade linearly to nothing over
  `decay_s`, so the identity returns exactly on time.
- `game/xr/hit_shake.json`: 0.02 m shake, 6 degree roll, 0.4 s decay, full strength at
  15 m/s, 18 Hz. Parsed and range-checked like `CockpitSpec`; a missing, unknown, wrong-type
  or out-of-range key is rejected with the key named.
- Preset 1 has `hit_shake` 0, so it always answers the identity (CMF-3). Only `hit_spin`
  rolls, so presets 1 to 3 never rotate. The class reads `SimEvent` and nothing else from
  `core/` (ARC-3, CMF-6).
- Not wired into the rig or `main.gd`; the issue leaves that to a later lane-b issue.
