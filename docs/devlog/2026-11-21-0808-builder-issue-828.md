## Summary

- `xr/haptics.json` gains `wall_impact` (0.5, 0.06 s, runtime frequency),
  `wall_impact_min_speed` (2 m/s) and `wall_impact_full_speed` (10 m/s).
- `HapticsSpec`: `wall_impact` is one more pulse kind, checked like the others. The two speeds
  are top-level numbers with their own ranges (min 0..50, full 0.1..100), and full must be
  above min; a missing or bad one rejects the file naming it. New `wall_pulse(speed)`: empty
  below the minimum (and for NaN), otherwise the entry with its amplitude scaled linearly from
  a quarter at the minimum to all of it at the full speed and above, capped at 1.
- `HitHaptics.wall(side, speed, xr_running)`: same `hands_for` as a shot. `hit` and `pickup`
  now share one `_send(pulse, hands, xr_running)`.
- `CombatWiring.feed_events`: the strongest `WALL_IMPACT` of ship 0 in the advance pulses
  once, its side from `HitFlash.side_for(normal, orientation)` (the normal points back into
  the tunnel, as a shot from the wall would fly). Reads events only.
