## Issue

#606 (playtest, M1, PLT-5, SLV-1): stick labels do not say which way the sticks act, or
click versus hold.

## Summary

- Each stick has one cross label (`<hand>_stick`) instead of one row per axis: the push-away
  action on top, pull-back at the bottom, left and right on the middle line. When both
  sides share an action word, the line reads `left  <  strafe  >  right`. The full form
  `strafe left  +  strafe right` pushed the left cross past the anchored window's edge.
- Stick clicks: left `click: labels, hold: map`, right `click: slot held part`. The label
  toggle's button is now `ThumbstickMapping.LABEL_BUTTON` / `label_button()`, which
  `ComfortWiring.label_button()` reads. The labels and the wiring use one definition.
- `PickupHint` (`xr/pickup_hint.gd`, `pickup_hint.json`, `distance_m` 12): while the hold is
  empty and a part is within that distance, the slot click reads `fly through to pick up`.
  `main.gd` sets `XRRig.pickup_hint` each frame from session copies.
- `ControlLabelSet`: the cross is centred on itself and moved by its half width, so its inner
  edge sits at the column like the other rows. It is centred on the old top row, and the
  rows below keep their places. Its leader ends at the top of the stick.
- `controller_model.gd` is left alone because open PR #623 edits it.
- Twelve default labels instead of fourteen.
