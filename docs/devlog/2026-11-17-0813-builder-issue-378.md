this run took the lower, #378. Named in Israel time (UTC+2).

## Summary

- New `game/data/gen/robots.json`: types drone, glider, needler, skimmer and hauler (the
  warden is left out as the strongest type), 4 to 8 robots per level, at most 2 per module,
  at least 20 m from the start and 8 m from the reactor's centre. Numbers are placeholders for
  the owner to tune.
- New `game/gen/robot_plan.gd` (`RobotPlan`): reads the file with the robot files' text by id
  (gen never opens files), so an unknown or broken type is named. Unknown, missing and bad
  keys are named; distances are held as whole millimetres. Each type's radius comes from its
  robot file.
- `GeneratedLevel.build` places robots after the reactor: a count from the plan's range, then
  per robot up to 64 draws of type, module (not the start module, under its cap), box and
  whole-millimetre point. A point needs the robot's radius plus a ship's diameter clear of the
  box's faces, of the reactor's surface and of every robot already placed, and the plan's
  distances from the start and the reactor's centre. Facing is one of the six axes. Spawns are
  unresolved, as a parsed level's are; `RobotSpawnList.resolve` gives them types.
- The robot RNG is a fresh `RandomNumberGenerator` seeded from `hash([output_hash(),
  "robots"])`. `output_hash` is fixed by the level seed, so this is the seed's randomness, and
  building the level twice gives the same robots without disturbing the generator's own RNG.
- `MineGenerator.to_level_def` takes `robot_plan` as a new last optional argument. One field
  was added beside `reactor_note`: `robot_note`, set when fewer robots than drawn fit.
