## Summary

- New `game/net/bot_fallback.gd` (`BotFallback`, 111 lines): when the bot gives ground
  instead of pressing on. It owns the retreat state (last safe point, retreating flag)
  and the wait counter that used to sit in `BotPilot`, with `update(ship, threatened)`,
  `shelter(ship)`, `waits(...)`, and the static `robots_closing(sim, ship)` and
  `under_fire(sim, walls, ship)`. The constants `RETREAT_SHIELD`, `OUTNUMBERED`,
  `CLOSING_ROBOT_SPEED` and `MAX_WAIT_TICKS` moved with it.
- `BotPilot` (258 lines) keeps every public name: the four constants are aliases of
  `BotFallback`'s, and `robots_closing` and `under_fire` delegate. The private
  `_reaches` helper (one caller) is inlined into `shot_reaches`. The order of the
  per-tick calls is unchanged (the fall-back update still runs before the escape step
  takes over), so every flight gives the same inputs.
- New `game/tests/test_bot_fallback.gd`: 5 tests (aliases match, falls back only with a
  low shield and to the last safe point, the wait cap and its reset, no wait without a
  reason, `robots_closing`).
