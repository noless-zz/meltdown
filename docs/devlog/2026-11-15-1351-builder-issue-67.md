## Summary

- `game/xr/comfort_vignette.gd` (`ComfortVignette`, `RefCounted`):
  `strength(preset, angular_speed, linear_speed)` in 0..1, scaled by the preset's
  `vignette_strength`. Each trigger ramps from a start to a full threshold so the vignette
  does not pop at a threshold: `ROTATION` 5 to 90 deg/s, `FAST_ROTATION` 90 to 180 deg/s,
  `ALL_MOTION` the larger of the rotation ramp and 0.5 to 20 m/s of travel. Inputs are
  radians per second and metres per second; constants carry the units in their names.
- `game/xr/vignette.gdshader`: unshaded spatial shader on one 2x2 quad written straight to
  clip space, so it covers each eye whatever the field of view. No screen texture, no
  post-process pass.
- `game/xr/vignette.gd` (`Vignette`) and `game/xr/vignette.tscn`: the quad, with
  `set_strength()`; hidden at strength 0 so presets without a vignette draw nothing. The
  material is local to each scene instance.
- `game/xr/xr_rig.tscn`: `Camera/Vignette` added, hidden until something sets a strength.
- `game/tests/test_comfort_vignette.gd`: 9 tests.
