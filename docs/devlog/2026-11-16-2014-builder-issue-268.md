## Summary

- `ShipInput.slot_held: bool`. New `game/core/part_slotting.gd` (`PartSlotting`, static),
  called from `Sim.step` for that ship after it moved and hit walls, before it fires, so a
  slotted weapon can fire on the same tick.
- The held part goes into the first empty primary slot; with both full it replaces the
  selected one, and the replaced weapon is dropped as a `SalvageItem` at the ship's
  position and velocity. The drop joins `Sim.salvage` after the tick's items have moved,
  as a robot drop does. A part not in the catalogue stays held; an empty hold does nothing.
- `ShipState.primary_ids` (not hashed) names the weapon in each primary slot so it can be
  dropped under its id; `ShipLoadout.equip` fills it. A weapon placed with no id (tests
  only) is replaced without a drop.
- Beyond the criteria, but needed for them to mean anything: a dropped item carries
  `ignored_ship`, so the ship whose hold just emptied does not take the part straight back
  on the next tick. It clears once the ship's centre ends a tick out of pickup range;
  hashed only while set.
- `SimEvent.Kind.PART_SLOTTED` (appended): `magnitude` the slot, new field `dropped`, and
  `vector` the drop point when dropped.
- Replay format 11: a tenth column per ship; versions 1 to 10 read it as off.
  `NetMessages` carries `slot_held` as flag bit 3; `PROTOCOL_VERSION` 2.
- `sim.gd` is 294 lines.
