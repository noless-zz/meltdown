  missing file is never a rejection.

- `ComfortWiring` holds the notice and reports in `set_preset` and `read_preset_button`
  (kept at 300 lines by tightening two comments). `RigViews.show_custom`, called from
  `place_guarded` beside `show_swivel`, hands the row the base and the notice. Nothing in
  `core/`, the session or `main.gd` changes (ARC-3).
