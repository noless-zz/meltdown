## Issue

#687 (M1, CMF-1, CMF-3, CMF-8, ARC-3, DEV-6, umbrella #153): a `comfort_motion` check that
measures the view's rotation in the running game, and four `m1` steps, one per preset.

## Summary

- New `acceptance/acceptance_comfort_check.gd` (`AcceptanceComfortCheck`). The runner hands it
  the rig camera's world rotation after every frame it flies (`XRRig/Camera`, the XR camera on
  the headset and the flat camera on a desktop; composed up the parents so it also works outside
  a tree). It measures the per-frame turn in the view's own axes and reports `max_yaw_deg_s`,
  `max_pitch_deg_s`, `max_roll_deg_s`, `smooth_rotation_frames`, `snap_frames` and
  `comfort_frames`. Limits are read from the active `ComfortPreset` in `ComfortWiring`: preset 1
  fails on any frame turning more than 0.5° that is not a jump; preset 2 fails on yaw or pitch
  more than 5 % over its caps; presets 3 and 4 only report. A jump is a frame turning at least
  the preset's snap angle (or its auto-level snap threshold when smaller): a snap or the reset.
- `comfort_motion` is a known check and needs a replay (`AcceptanceSuite` rejects one without).
  `AcceptanceRunner` gets the per-frame hook (297 lines).
- New m1 steps `comfort_preset_3` and `comfort_preset_4`: `m1_flight_axes.replay` under the
  preset, checks `comfort_motion` and `final_hash`, hash 2052785774 (the `flight_axes` hash).

## Deviation from the criteria

The steps for presets 1 and 2 are not added. The runner feeds a replay's recorded inputs to the
session past the comfort filter, so the replay's smooth turns reach the view on every preset:
preset 1 shows 840 smooth frames, preset 2 yaws at 90°/s against a 60°/s cap. The issue's "same
hash on all four" and "preset 1 passes" cannot both hold. A test pins the failure, and follow-up
#697 (`triage`) lays out the options for the owner or Planner.
