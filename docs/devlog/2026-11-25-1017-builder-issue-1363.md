## Summary

- `data/audio/robot_voices.json`: one row per robot type (loop, volume, pitch range, range
  in metres, `muffle_db`) and table values: `max_voices` 6, `reference_m`, the distance
  low-pass `far_cutoff_hz`, and the through-a-wall low-pass `muffle_cutoff_hz`.
  `RobotVoiceTable` / `RobotVoiceRow` read it, naming the field on error.
- `RobotVoicePicker` (pure): every live robot with a row within its range is a candidate;
  the nearest `max_voices` sound, ties to the lower index. Pitch follows speed over the
  type's `max_speed`. Muffling is read from the level boxes: a box is convex, so a box
  holding both robot and pilot means a clear line; otherwise the voice is muffled. No ray.
- `RobotVoices` (Node3D): a pool of `AudioStreamPlayer3D`s on the `Effects` bus, so the
  room reverb applies. Inverse-distance fall-off from `reference_m` and the player's
  distance low-pass make a voice louder and brighter as it nears. A muffled voice drops by
  its `muffle_db` and moves to a `RobotVoicesMuffled` bus with a low-pass, which sends to
  `Effects`. A player keeps sounding the same type, so a loop carries on rather than
  restarting; a robot destroyed (hull 0) or gone stops its voice.
- `CombatWiring` attaches it beside the cues and feeds it the session's robot copies and
  ship 0's position after each advance; a new level stops every voice.
- Six 2 s loops by the new `tools/synth_robot_voices.py`: light hum (drone), airy whistle
  (glider), low grinding engine with clanks (hauler), thin chattering whine (needler),
  warbling saw (skimmer), deep slow throb (warden). Imported with `loop_mode=2`. Re-running
  the script rewrites identical files. Licence row added.
- Acceptance: step `robot_voices` in `suites/m6/sound.json` on `bot_g2_level.replay`
  (hash 802400918, unchanged). The new `robot_voices_heard` check expects all six types,
  at least one voice heard through a wall and quieter for it, and the cap held. On that
  flight: six types, at most 2 at once, 2014 ticks with a muffled voice.
