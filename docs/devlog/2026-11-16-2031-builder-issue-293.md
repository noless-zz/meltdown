## Summary

- New `game/gen/module_def.gd` (`ModuleDef`, `RefCounted`, parses text only): `id`,
  `boxes` (the `g2_level.json` box form, metres read in whole millimetres) and
  `connectors`. Rejected with a message naming the problem, keeping nothing: unknown or
  missing keys, a bad id (the weapon/robot id rule), a box whose min is not below its max,
  boxes not joined into one piece (`LevelDef.joined`), no connector, a bad axis or size
  class, a connector not on a box face on its outward side, an opening that does not fit
  inside that face, and an opening covered by another box beyond the face (so the face is
  not outer).
- New `game/gen/module_connector.gd` (`ModuleConnector`): position in mm, outward axis
  (`+x` ... `-z`) and size class. Two square classes: `small` 8 m and `large` 12 m. The
  starter modules all use `small`; `large` is there for bigger rooms later.
- Five starter modules in `game/data/modules/`: `straight_tunnel`, `corner_90`,
  `vertical_shaft` (top, bottom and a side entry at the foot), `room_four_way` (30 x 16 x
  30 m) and `t_junction`. Plain boxes, original shapes (NAM-3).
- Connector checks are on integer millimetres so they are exact (ARC-1). Key checks reuse
  `LevelDef._check_keys` rather than copying it.
