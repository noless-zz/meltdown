## Summary

- New `game/xr/map_zoom.gd` (`MapZoom`, `RefCounted`): a factor starting at 1.0;
  `change(direction, delta_s)` multiplies it by `zoom_per_second ^ (direction * delta_s)`
  (direction clamped to -1..1, negative frame times ignored), clamped to
  `[min_zoom, max_zoom]`; `reset()` returns to 1.0; `use_look(look)` takes the limits and
  rate from a parsed look; `scaled(fit, zoom)` scales a fit about the hologram origin.
- `MapLook` / `map_look.json`: optional `min_zoom` (0.5), `max_zoom` (3.0) and
  `zoom_per_second` (1.5). Ranges 0.1..10, 0.1..10 and 1.01..10. A file is rejected, naming
  the key, when the limits cross or are equal, or do not hold 1.0.
- `MapHologram.set_zoom(factor)`, clamped to the look's limits, and `zoom()`. The lines node
  is scaled; the ship, exit and reactor markers, the route, blocked crosses and fire markers
  are placed by the zoomed fit, so they move about the hand anchor while their sizes keep
  their floors (the fire marker's `fire_size_m` floor still holds at `min_zoom`). `build`
  keeps the zoom. `map_hologram.gd` is 297 lines (some doc comments were tightened to fit).
- Nothing is wired: no layout or main scene change.
