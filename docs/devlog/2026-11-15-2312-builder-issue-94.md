## Summary

- `game/core/weapon_def.gd` (`WeaponDef`): fire interval in whole ticks, projectile speed,
  lifetime, damage, energy cost, parsed from JSON like `ShipTuning`; bad files are rejected
  with the key named.
- `game/data/weapons/pulse_cannon.json`: 10 ticks between shots (6 per second), 120 m/s,
  1.5 s, 8 damage, 2 energy per shot. Six shots a second cost 12 energy/s, just under the
  default 12.5 energy/s recharge, so the cannon alone never runs the pool dry.
- `ShipInput.fire_primary`. `ShipState` gets two primary and two secondary slots (empty),
  `selected_primary`, `fire_cooldown` and `fire_primary(held)`, called by `Sim` after the
  ship's step so a shot spends what the afterburner and shield leave.
- `game/core/projectile.gd` (`Projectile`) and `Sim.projectiles`: in-flight projectiles move
  first each tick, expire after their lifetime, and are removed when outside the box. A new
  shot starts at the ship's position, flying along its nose at the weapon speed without the
  ship's velocity, so a weapon's range is exactly what its data file says.
- Hashes: weapons join the ship hash, tagged `"weapons"`, only once a slot is filled or a
  cooldown runs; projectiles join the sim hash, tagged, only while any exist. No committed
  replay or expected hash changed.
- `Replay` format version 6: a ninth column per ship for fire. Versions 1 to 5 still load
  with fire off.
