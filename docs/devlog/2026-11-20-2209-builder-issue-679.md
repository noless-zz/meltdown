## Issue

#679 Playtest: the ship marker on the held map points along the ship's heading. MAP-1,
NAM-3, ARC-4. Milestone M3.

## Summary

- New `MapShipMarker` (`game/xr/map_ship_marker.gd`): an original dart, "the kite", built
  from seven triangles: a swept delta with a swallowtail notch, a dorsal fin aft as the
  only thing above the wings, and a shallow keel. Both faces render, so it never vanishes
  when it rolls. Sized from the look's `marker_radius_m` (nose to tail 3x the radius,
  24 mm with the shipped look) and drawn in `marker_color`; no new look keys, so old look
  files load unchanged.
- `MapHologram` draws the dart instead of the ball and gains `set_ship_orientation`; the
  fit only scales and moves, so the ship's basis carries over as is. It stays under 300
  lines (296).
- `MapWiring.update` passes the ship's interpolated orientation (previous to current,
  slerped by the interpolation fraction) each frame, next to the position.
- `docs/art-direction.md` records the silhouette for reuse as the M6 hull.

## Tests

- `test_map_ship_marker.gd`: basis matches yaw 90°, pitch 45°, roll 90° within 0.01;
  nose along the ship's forward and fin along its up; later orientations and zoom keep
  it right; the shape has one centred nose and a top clearly taller than the bottom;
  the mesh length comes from the look, the material is the marker colour, culling off.
- `test_main_map_heading.gd`: in the main scene on g2, after 45 frames of yaw and roll
  the marker's basis equals the session's interpolated orientation.
- Full suite locally with Godot 4.7.2 headless, as CI runs it: 1939 tests, 0 failed.
  The existing `test_map_*` tests pass unchanged.
