## Summary

- `SessionSnapshots.debris` gains `DEBRIS_KINDS` (`"kinds"`): `MeltdownFire.KIND_SOLID` or
  `KIND_FIRE` per entry. Every other key keeps its values.
- `DebrisView.show_debris` takes optional `kinds` and `tick`. A fire entry gets a new
  `BURNING` state from its fall tick: its own shape (sphere or slab) and size, with a
  see-through, emissive `fire_*` material. Before that it shows the shared sphere warning
  within `warn_seconds`. A held fire is not drawn. No collision and no full-screen pass.
- The flicker is `DebrisLook.fire_energy_at(look, tick)`, two slow waves (0.9 Hz and 2.3 Hz,
  both under 3 Hz). It only changes when the tick changes, so a paused session keeps the
  same frame.
- New look keys `fire_color`, `fire_energy` (0..16), `fire_alpha` (0..1) and
  `fire_flicker` (0..0.5), with built-in fallbacks matching the shipped file.
- `debris_view.gd` is now 295 lines. The next change to it should split it.
