## Summary

- New `gen/wall_clearance.gd` (`WallClearance`): `WallGap`'s rules in whole
  millimetres for one candidate. A new box and a placed box that are apart (or
  touching) on one axis while overlapping on the other two must be at least
  `min_wall_mm` apart unless a third box overlaps both (the doorway into the parent, a
  bridge's doorway). Only placed boxes inside the candidate's bounds grown by the
  minimum are compared.
- `MineGenerator._draw` rejects a candidate that fails it, like an overlapping one, so
  the next candidate is taken. `LoopBridges` applies the same check to a bridge: the
  first try left seed 32 with a 0 m wall between a bridge and a room.
- `min_wall_m` is a new key in `data/gen/generator.json` (1.0), read by `LoopBridges`
  with the other generator keys (0..100, 0 turns it off). `MineGenerator.MIN_WALL_MM`
  (1000) is the default without settings, as `DEAD_END_MIN_OPEN` is.
- `LevelValidator` gains a `wall` check (reported with `piece`'s timing). Its
  `min_wall_m` is handed over by `SeedLevel` from the generator's file, as
  `min_escape_fraction` is from the reactor plan, so generator and check cannot
  disagree; hand-built levels keep 0 (off).
- `PlacementFit.moved_boxes` gives the candidate's boxes for the check, keeping
  `mine_generator.gd` at 290 lines.
