## Summary

- `xr/hub_sound_table.gd` (`HubSoundTable`, new): reads `data/audio/hub_sounds.json` (voices,
  per sound a file, `volume_db` and `min_gap_s`, and a `pressed` map from a hub row's action to
  its sound; `fit:empty` is clearing a slot). A bad file names its field. `event()` turns one
  frame of the stations (state before and after, the action pressed, `HubStations.update`'s
  result) into a sound: `refused` for any refused press, the `pressed` sound for a successful
  one (`select` when the action has none), `select` on taking a station into use, `back` on
  stick left inside one, and `point` when the stick or the aim moves onto another station or row.
- `xr/hub_station_sounds.gd` (`HubStationSounds`, new): a pool of `AudioStreamPlayer3D`s on the
  existing `Effects` bus, each sound placed at the screen of the station it happened on. A sound
  repeats no sooner than its `min_gap_s`. It keeps a log of what it requested (`requested`,
  `requested_at`) for tests and the acceptance check, and plays nothing without an audio device.
- `HubStations.last_acted` (two lines) says which named action the frame pressed.
  `HubWiring.update` takes the stations' state before the aim and stick act and hands it to the
  sounds after `stations.update`. Its class comment was shortened to keep the file near 300 lines
  (304).
- `tools/synth_hub_sounds.py` (new, standard library): seven original wavs (`hub_point`, `select`,
  `back`, `refused`, `crafted`, `unmounted`, `launch`); mounting reuses `part_slotted.wav`.
  Re-running writes byte-identical files. Licence row in `docs/licences.md`.
- Acceptance: new check `hub_sounds` (`acceptance/checks/hub_sounds_check.gd`) follows
  `hub_stations` on the m6 `hub_stations` step. That step crafts, mounts and launches through the
  scene's own controls, and the check requires `select`, `crafted` at the craft station, `mounted`
  at the fit station and `launch`, in that order.
