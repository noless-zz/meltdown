## Summary

- `game/core/salvage_item.gd` (`SalvageItem`): part id, position, velocity,
  `state_hash()`, `dropped_by(robot)` (null for an empty `salvage`) and `advance(bounds)`.
  Items lose `DAMPING_PER_SECOND` (2.0) of their speed per second, are put at rest below
  `REST_SPEED` (0.01 m/s) and are held off the walls with `RADIUS` (0.3 m) the same way
  `RobotState.keep_inside` holds robots. They never expire.
- `game/core/sim.gd`: `Sim.salvage`, filled in robot order in the removal step at the
  robot's position and velocity. Existing items move after the robots and before the
  drops, so a new part appears exactly where its robot died. Salvage joins the hash only
  while non-empty, so every pinned hash stands.
- `game/core/sim_event.gd`: `SALVAGE_DROPPED`, appended. `ship` is the ship whose shot
  destroyed the robot, `magnitude` the item's index in `Sim.salvage`, `vector` where it
  was dropped. It is the last event of its tick, after `ROBOT_DESTROYED`.
- `test_robot_hits.gd`: the killing-tick event test now expects the drop as a fourth
  event, since the drone it kills carries a cannon.
