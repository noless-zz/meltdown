## Issue

#1459: the cockpit glass darkens and tints the view (playtest, the owner's priority). Second in

adds only glass keys to the cockpit files.

## Summary

The cause: the #1306 glass mixed a light blue over the view (`blend_mix`, opacity 0.05 to
0.15). On a dark mine that is not a darker view, it is a haze. Worked out for a tunnel pixel
at 0.1 display brightness, preset 4's old glass roughly doubled the brightness and flattened
the contrast, which matches the "brown, low-contrast" screenshot. Real glass multiplies the
light through it, so the glass now does that.

- `xr/cockpits/*.json`: `glass_tint_alpha` is replaced by `glass_transmission` (anchored
  0.92, guided 0.94, open 0.95, glass 0.96) and `glass_tint` ([0.96, 0.98, 1.0], near
  neutral). All four presets now have the faint edge reflection (fresnel 0.06, power 6, so it
  stays near the edge). Before this, guided and anchored had none.
- `xr/cockpit_spec.gd`: parses both keys. A transmission below 0.8 is refused and the error
  names the field (`MIN_GLASS_TRANSMISSION`). The old key is now an unknown key.
  `glass_opacity()` and `tint_spread()` added.
- `xr/cockpit_glass.gdshader`: now a `blend_mul` filter. The edge reflection moved to the new
  `xr/cockpit_glass_reflection.gdshader`, an additive next pass.
- `xr/cockpit_materials.gd`: `glass()` builds the filter and the reflection pass.
  `glass_filter()` turns the tint into the multiplier: the transmission on the brightest
  channel, the other channels in proportion.
- `acceptance/checks/glass_clear_check.gd` and the m6 `look` step `glass_clear`: after m6's
  flight, for each of the four presets the same frame is drawn with the glass shown and
  hidden, and both pictures are saved. The middle 60% of the window is judged on mean
  luminance and contrast. Glass on must stay within 10% of glass off, and the message prints
  both numbers for every preset.
- The cockpit interior, its light and its colours are unchanged. Presentation only; the
  hashes are unchanged.
