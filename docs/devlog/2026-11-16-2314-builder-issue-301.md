## Summary

- `RobotDef.wake_range` (optional key, metres, read in whole millimetres, 0.001..10000).
  Zero (key absent) means awake from tick 0, as before. It joins `values_hash` only when set.
- `RobotState.awake`: false at spawn when the type has a wake range. `RobotTick.advance`
  wakes a robot once the nearest live ship's centre is within range; a sleeping robot
  neither moves, turns, nor fires (its fire cooldown does not count down either).
  `apply_damage` with a positive amount wakes it. It never falls asleep again. The flag
  joins `state_hash` only for robots with a wake range.
- The replay's robot line writes `wake_range` only when set, so lines for robots without
  one are byte-for-byte as before; committed replays and pinned hashes are unchanged.
- The wake range is set per placement: a level entry may carry `wake_range`, which gives
  that spawn its own copy of the robot type with that range. I chose the level file over
  the robot files because the robot files are fixtures for many movement and firing tests
  that expect a robot active from tick 0, and how near a ship must come depends on where
  the robot is placed, not on its type.
- `test_tunnel_robots.json`: needler 9 m, drone, skimmer, glider and hauler 8 m, warden
  10 m. Each is at least 5 m shorter than the robot's distance from the start. At full
  forward thrust, headless, they wake one after another at ticks 37, 49, 57, 63, 70
  and 75, and the needler first hits the ship at tick 40.
