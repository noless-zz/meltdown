## Summary

- `view/debris_effects_look.json` (new, beside `debris_look.json`) and
  `DebrisEffectsLook`: four optional sections, `fire_flames`, `dust`, `rock` and `metal`.
  A missing section leaves that part as it was before; a missing or rejected file turns
  all of them off with a warning naming the key.
- `DebrisEffects` (view): a burning fire gets GPU particle flames and embers, built by
  `ReactorFlames` (its builder is now public as `build`), so the wreck and the meltdown fire
  burn alike. The flames stay in the fire's cold blue; `test_look_hues` now checks both
  particle colours against the reactor (the first hot colour I picked was too grey and the
  test caught it). A rock or slab that lands raises one dust puff (one-shot, normal blend,
  hidden once settled). A rock takes the walls' tint (`wall_mix`) and roughness. A slab takes
  the theme's `metallic` and `metal_roughness` (#1292). The faint glow stays (LGT-1).
- Particles hang under their entry, scaled back to uniform, so they keep their shape on a
  stretched slab. `DebrisView` (277 lines) only works out "burning" and "landed" for
  each entry. An entry that is already down the first time it is drawn raises no dust.
- `fire_debris_look` acceptance check (`acceptance/checks/debris_look_check.gd`), added
  to the m6 `reactor_and_fire` step.
- One existing test changed: `test_debris_view_fire` asserted a fire entry has no
  children at all ("fire must add no collision"); it now asserts no collision under it.
