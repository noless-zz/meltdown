# 2026-11-21 Builder, lane-c, issue #850

**Issue:** #850, m1 suite: the vignette follows each preset's trigger, and is never on in
preset 4 (CMF-1, CMF-2, ARC-3, DEV-6).

## Summary

- New check `vignette` (`game/acceptance/acceptance_vignette_check.gd`). Once per replay
  frame it reads the strength shown on `XRRig/Camera/Vignette` (the existing read-only
  `Vignette.get_strength()`), the turn rate of the tick on screen (none on a snap tick, as
  the owner asked in #931) and the speed interpolated like the rig's pose. It judges them
  against the preset in force, loaded with `ComfortWiring.preset_for`, not the wiring's
  live `preset`.
- Rules per frame: preset 4 (or any preset with trigger never or strength 0) shows exactly
  0; no preset shows more than its `vignette_strength`; while the trigger is met the
  vignette shows; while it is not, it may only fade, and must be at or below 0.01 once
  the trigger has been unmet for five easing time constants (read from the scene's
  `vignette_feel.json`, so tuning that file does not fail the check). The fade allowance
  is there because #931 eases the shown strength.
- `m1.json`: `vignette_preset_1` to `_4`. Presets 1 and 2 fly their own m1 comfort
  replays, 3 and 4 fly `m1_flight_axes.replay`, each with the hash it already had.
- No change to `game/xr/` or `main.gd`.
