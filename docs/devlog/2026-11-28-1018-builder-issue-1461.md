## Summary

- `xr/cockpit_instrument.gd`: an instrument entry may now also set `distance_m`, `size_m`,
  `label`, `color`, `low_color`, `low_below`, `low_pulse_s` (1..10 s) and `low_pulse_min`
  (0.5..1). Each bad value is rejected with its field named. An instrument at or behind the
  shell is rejected. The bar's fill, the low test and the pulse are pure functions.
- New `xr/console_gauges.gd` (`ConsoleGauges`): the cockpit frame builds it as a child from
  the file's instruments, so it is part of the cockpit model and moves only with the frame. It
  is rebuilt with the frame on a preset change. Each instrument is a dark bezel with its label
  and a bar. The empty part of the bar is drawn dim in the same colour. All parts are unshaded,
  and the whole console is 5 draw calls.
- Below `low_below` (0.25, the panel's low-hull fraction) a bar and its track turn orange
  and pulse smoothly between 70 % and full brightness every 1.6 s.
- `combat_wiring.gd` takes the one `ShipGauges` snapshot each frame and gives it to both the
  panel and the console. The floating panel is unchanged, so nothing is lost before #1462.
- All four cockpit files declare HULL, SHIELD and ENERGY at yaw 0°, 11.5° and 23°, pitch
  −34°. Each sits just in front of its preset's ledge (0.34 / 0.38 / 0.40 / 0.40 m), and the
  size is scaled so each spans the same angle on every preset. They clear the anchored and
  guided switch cluster (yaw −9° to −26°), the status panel in every corner and the map mount.
- New m6 step `console_gauges` in `suites/m6/look.json`, with its check in
  `acceptance/checks/console_gauges_check.gd`. It uses the `hull_warning` step's low-hull
  replay. The hull instrument showed its low state for 248 frames of flight. Then on each
  preset the three bars match the core's fractions in the same place, and the lost hull shows
  low. One screenshot per preset, skipped headless.
