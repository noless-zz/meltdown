## Summary

- `dev_menu.json` has a "Comfort" entry right after "Panel corner". `DevMenuSpec` knows the
  `comfort` kind, and the built-in menu includes it.
- `DevMenuModel` reads the four preset files' `display_name`s. The right stick steps and wraps
  through presets 1 to 4, A returns `comfort:<n>`, and `open` takes the active preset.
- `DevMenuWiring` takes the `ComfortWiring` and applies a confirmed preset through
  `ComfortWiring.set_preset`, the same call the preset button makes. `FlightTestWiring` then saves
  it in `ComfortChoice` on the next frame, the way it saves a button change. The menu stays open so
  the change can be seen. In the custom mode (#806) the entry opens on the custom file's base
  preset.
- `main.gd` passes `comfort` to the wiring on the existing line, so it stays at 300 lines.
  `ComfortWiring` and `ComfortChoice` are not edited (lane-c).
