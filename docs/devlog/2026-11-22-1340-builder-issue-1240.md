## Issue

#1240: measure the thinnest wall between tunnels across 100 seeds, follow-up to the
owner's playtest #1225 (GEN-3, GEN-5, ARC-1, ARC-2, DEV-7).

## Summary

- New `gen/wall_gap.gd` (`WallGap`, `RefCounted`, no nodes): for a box union, the
  smallest distance between parallel, facing-away wall faces of two boxes that overlap
  in projection, with the gap, the two box indices and the axis; `INF` and -1 when no
  pair qualifies. Ties keep the lowest-index pair.
- **"Does not open into it"** covers two boxes that overlap, and two boxes that a third
  box overlaps both of (a doorway or loop bridge). The generator places modules face to
  face with a doorway box across the shared face, so the literal reading gives 0 m on
  every seed: the rim around each doorway. `count_joined` keeps that reading, and the
  sweep prints it on its own line.
- New `tests/wall_gap_sweep.gd`, run like `validate_seeds.gd` (`-s`, `--seeds`,
  `--first`), not part of `run_tests.gd`.
- Nothing in the generator changed.
