## Summary

- New `game/map_wiring.gd` (`MapWiring`, `RefCounted`) beside `main.gd`, like
  `CombatWiring`. `attach(rig, mapping)` creates a `MapHologram` and hangs it in the hand
  `MapHologram.hand_for` picks; with no such hand it frees it and stays unattached.
- `update(session, delta, shared)` once per frame: on a new session it takes the walls back
  from the replay (`replay.walls()`, a fresh union: the level's boxes or the tunnel's one
  box) and starts a new `ExploredBoxes`; it visits the current ship copy's position,
  rebuilds the hologram only when the explored list changed (`rebuilds` counts), moves the
  marker to the interpolated ship position, follows the `show_map` hand if left-handed mode
  or a remap moves it, and calls `MapHologram.read` with this frame's buttons and the label
  button's `ClickHold` (`ComfortWiring.shared_click_hold()`), as #335 asked.
- `main.gd`: a `map` field, `map.attach($XRRig, mapping)` in `_ready` after `use_level`,
  `map.buttons = ...` after the label button is read in `apply_controls`, and
  `map.update(...)` after `combat.update` in `advance_frame`. To stay at 300 lines, three
  doc comments (tunnel offset, `log_afterburner`, `on_session_visible`) were reworded
  shorter with the same content.
