## Summary

- Suite field `preset_changes`: a list of `{"tick", "preset"}` in rising tick order. Parsed by
  the new `AcceptancePresetChanges` (`acceptance_preset_changes.gd`), which rejects unknown
  keys, presets outside 1..4, ticks not after the previous one, and a tick past the replay
  (it reads the replay to check), each naming the step's field. Documented in
  `acceptance_suite.gd`; the step holds two lists in `acceptance_step.gd`.
- The runner makes each change through the scene's `set_comfort_preset` when the simulation
  reaches the tick, before the frame that flies it, and tells the comfort check.
- `AcceptanceComfortCheck.change_preset`: frames from the change on, including the change's
  own frame, are judged against the new preset. The result names the chain ("preset
  4→1→3→2") and the measurements gain `comfort_presets`.
- m1 step `preset_change_mid_flight`: `m1_flight_axes.replay` from preset 4, to 1 at tick
  1712 (straight flight just after the roll turns stop), 3 at 1900 (the 90° yaw), 2 at 2030
  (straight flight to the end); hash 3139276631 as `comfort_preset_3`/`_4`. It sits just
  before `first_launch_flight_test` (#762, merged during this run), which a test keeps last. Preset 1 is put
  on a stretch without turn inputs because the raw replay turns smoothly, which preset 1
  forbids (that is the existing `test_the_preset_1_step_fails_on_the_raw_replay`).
