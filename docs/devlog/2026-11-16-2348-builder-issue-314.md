## Summary

- `game/xr/map_hologram.gd` (`MapHologram`, `Node3D`): draws `MapWireframe` segments,
  fitted into a cube of `size_m`, as one emissive `PRIMITIVE_LINES` mesh, plus a small
  emissive sphere at the ship's position fitted the same way. Hidden until `show_map` has
  been held for `HOLD_SECONDS` (0.4 s), hidden again on release. `attach_to(rig, mapping)`
  hangs it under `LeftHand` (or `RightHand` when left-handed mode or a remap moves the
  click), 12 cm above and 8 cm ahead of the controller, so it turns with the hand.
- `game/xr/map_look.json`: line colour and energy, size, marker colour and radius; read like
  `level_look.json`, a rejected file keeps the built-in look and the warning names the field.
- `show_map` is a new view action in `ThumbstickMapping` (`VIEW_ACTIONS`), bound to the left
  stick click in both layout files, remappable and swapped by left-handed mode like every
  binding; it never reaches `ShipInput`. New `action_held` and `physical_controls`.
- Label toggle (`ComfortWiring.read_label_button`): when the label button also shows the
  map, labels flip on release of a click shorter than 0.4 s, so a hold for the map never
  flips them. If `show_map` is remapped elsewhere, the old flip-on-press stays. The click is
  timed on a steady clock (`clock`, replaceable in tests), so `main.gd` (lane-b) is unchanged.
- Controller labels now show "map (hold)" on the left stick click (13 labels instead of 12).
