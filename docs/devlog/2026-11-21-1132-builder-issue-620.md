## Issue

#620 (M3, RUN-4, RUN-5, ARC-4, ARC-2): fire lights at fractions of the level's own
countdown (the owner's answer on #517).

## Summary

- `fires.after_seconds` is replaced by `fires.after_fraction: [lo, hi]`, a fraction of the
  level's meltdown countdown, held in thousandths (`DebrisFires.FRACTION_SCALE`). A fire
  lights at `countdown × fraction`, at least tick 1 and never past the fall limit
  (`latest_fall_fraction`). Errors name `fires.after_fraction`; the old key is now an
  unknown key.
- `DebrisPlan.countdown_ticks(level)` (split out of `fall_limit_ticks`) is the countdown
  for the reactor-to-start escape path, which is what the generator can know. In play
  the countdown is set from the ships' positions when the reactor dies; with the ship at
  the reactor it is close to this.
- The fraction is still one draw from the same generator, so every fire's box is the
  same as before; only its lighting time moves. `output_hash`, reactor, robots and solid
  entries are unchanged (the existing sweep test checks this on seeds 0..999).
- Shipped values: `[0.03, 0.12]`.

## Measurement (seeds 0..999, bot pilot, no robots, no debris)

percentile 11.7 s, longest 21.2 s; countdowns 45 to 97 s (median 53.6 s). Bot time inside
