## Summary

- `dev_menu.json` gains `{"kind": "break", "label": "Break reminder"}` after "Comfort", and
  `break_on_text` / `break_off_text` ("On" / "Off"). `DevMenuSpec` knows the kind and the two
  optional texts; the built-in menu has the entry too.
- `DevMenuModel`: left / right flip the choice, A returns `break:on` or `break:off`; `open`
  takes the reminder's state as a last, defaulted argument.
- `DevMenuWiring` applies `break:` through `BreakPromptWiring.set_enabled` (which saves it in
  `BreakChoice`) and keeps the menu open; the entry opens on `reminder.is_enabled()`, so a
  saved choice or a `--break-reminder=` switch shows. `main.gd`: one line, in place, passes
  `break_prompt` to the dev menu wiring.
- Split: the model was at 293 lines, so the Level entry's choices and seed moved into a new
  `xr/dev_menu_levels.gd` (`DevMenuLevels`), unchanged in behaviour. `test_dev_menu.gd` and
  `test_dev_menu_wiring.gd` now read `model.levels.*` and `DevMenuLevels.*`; the shipped
  entry count there is 10.
- No edit to the break prompt files (lane-c) or to `core/`.
