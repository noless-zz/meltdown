## Summary

- `data/hub/hub.json`: `max_rows_visible` 5 and `max_row_chars` 28 (optional in
  `HubConfig`, defaults 5 and 28, whole numbers of at least 1). Every row cut to a short
  label: "Launch", "Locker empty", "No crew yet", "Ready / Locked / Built", "Back to
  stock", "Resume level 3". New note keys hold the longer words: `crew_note`
  ("+5 shield"), `store_note` (tier), `locked_note` (the reason), `resume_note` (the
  seed), `empty_crew_note`, `empty_store_note`; `launch_discard_note` and `stats_empty`
  became notes too.
- Rows that are numbers: the Logbook reads "Runs 12", "Won 3", "Lost 9", ...; the locker
  reads "Cell Salts x7".
- `HubText`: each row may carry a `note`; `note()` gives the selected row's,
  `rows_shown()` and `first_shown()` give the window. `HubWindow` scrolls only as far as
  the selection needs and pulls a header above the selected row into view. The Logbook
  rows and the empty crew/locker rows are now selectable (they still do nothing) so the
  Logbook scrolls and the empty pages' hints can show.
- `HubRows` (new) holds the crew, Logbook and locker rows and the row shapes, so
  `hub_text.gd` stays under 300 lines (291 before, 277 now).
- `HubStations` draws the hub text's window and the note on one line under the selected
  row; `HubStationScreen` no longer picks its own 8-row window. The flat `HubPanel`
  (fallback when the room fails) shows the note under the selected row too.
- Acceptance: the m6 `hub_stations` check now also fails if the station in use shows
  more than the title, `max_rows_visible` rows, a note and a reason.
