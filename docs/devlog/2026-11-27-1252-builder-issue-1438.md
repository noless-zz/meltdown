## Summary

- `tools/acceptance_progress.ps1` (new): turns the game's step lines and report blocks into
  progress lines, for example `[00:17] m1  1  basic_flight ... running`, then
  `[00:50] m1  1  basic_flight     PASS  (33 s)`. There is a `== m1 ==` line when a suite
  starts. When it ends there is `== m1: 1 passed, 1 failed ==`, with each failed step's message.
  Failures are red, passes green and skips yellow. It reads only the lines and blocks it has
  not seen yet, so each line prints once.
- Until #1444 merges, the step lines carry no suite or count. The label then shows the suite
  asked for (or `suite <n>` for `all` and `nightly`, until that suite's report names it), and
  the step number counted so far, with no total. Once the lines carry
  `suite=… index=… count=…`, it prints `3/9` and the time-out message. Both forms are tested.
  `acceptance/acceptance_runner.gd` is not touched, as the Planner asked.
- `device_acceptance.ps1`: it prints the progress lines after each poll, before the exit
  check, so the last steps are printed too. The drop and return lines from #1436 now start with
  the time since the start. There is a new red line when the headset does not come back
  (`acceptance_headset.ps1`).
- Headset label: `xr/acceptance_step_label.gd` and `.json` hold the text (`m6  4/31  step_label`)
  and the look (ARC-4). `acceptance_step_label_wiring.gd` reads the runner's suites, reports
  and printed lines, and places the label at the cockpit frame each frame. The label hangs
  under the rig, not the camera, so it stays fixed in the cockpit (CMF-8). `main.gd` changes
  one line: it hands `start_if_requested`'s runner to the wiring. So there is no label without
  `--acceptance` (ARC-3: presentation only).
- Placement: low and centred, 0.30 m below and 0.50 m ahead of the eye point (31° below the
  line of sight). That is under the status panel, at the dash edge, and drawn over the dash.
  It keeps clear of the middle of the view, which the screenshot checks judge.
- Acceptance: the step `step_label` is added to `suites/m6/effects.json`. It flies the weapons
  replay, takes screenshots at tick 12 and at the end, and runs the new check
  `step_label_shown` (`acceptance/checks/step_label_check.gd`). The check needs a visible
  label with this step's text, not under the camera, within 40° of straight ahead.
