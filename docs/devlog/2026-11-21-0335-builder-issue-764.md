  and the answer -> button map of `FlightTestAnswers`, in `PresetRecommender.ANSWERS` order.

  `FlightTestPanelText` accepts `answer_line` as an optional key and passes it through, so a
  bad template costs only the button names, not the rest of the panel's words. Its built-in
  words carry the same `answer_line`.

  (lane-a) builds it with `FlightTestPanel.new()` and must not change; `set_buttons` lets the
  wiring pass its loaded answers later. A bad line pushes one warning naming the field and
  shows the plain line. `main.gd` and `flight_test_wiring.gd` unchanged.
