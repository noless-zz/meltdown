## Summary

- `xr/destroyed_sequence.gd`: the state machine ALIVE → DESTROYED → CAN_RESTART → RESTARTED →
  ALIVE, with the fade and hold times from `xr/destroyed_screen.json` (1 s, 3 s). A restart
  needs a fresh press: A is also the afterburner, so a button held through the death does not
  restart. Paused frames (headset off) hold it still.
- `xr/destroyed_screen.gd`: `SHIP DESTROYED` and the prompt ("Press A to restart", or
  "Press Enter to restart" in the flat build), under the rig origin at the head point, 1.5 m
  ahead, no depth test, priority above the fade. The fade quad moves one priority step down
  (`RENDER_PRIORITY_MAX - 1`); the status panel stays below it.
- `xr/defence_haptics.gd`: the low-hull pulse (once per crossing of the mark) and the shorter
  shield-break pulse (once each time the shield reaches zero), on both hands, every preset.
  New `low_hull` and `shield_break` entries in `xr/haptics.json`.
- The mark is a new optional `low_hull_fraction` key in `xr/status_panel_look.json` (0.25).
  At or below it the hull bar now turns red as well as its label (`PanelBar.set_color`).
- `destroyed_wiring.gd` joins the session to these, beside `main.gd`. `main.gd` keeps the
  launch's level source and gains `restart_level`: a new session through `use_level`, or the
  tunnel session (`tunnel_session`, now also the field's initializer). Nothing writes into the
  old session.
