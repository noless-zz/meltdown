## Summary

- `game/xr/control_labels.gd` (`ControlLabels`, `RefCounted`): physical control -> label from
  a `ThumbstickMapping` layout, left-handed flag and invert pitch. Words come from one table
  (`WORDS`, negative and positive word per axis). Triggers, grips and buttons show the word
  for the sign of their weight; stick axes show both directions, negative first
  ("strafe left / right", "back / forward", "nose down / up"). The afterburner reads
  "boost". Zero-weight and unbound controls get no label; a control with two actions names
  both. Left-handed mode shows each binding on the other hand's control.
- `game/xr/controller_model.gd` (`ControllerModel`, `Node3D`): an original, generic shape from
  primitive meshes (cylinder handle, box head, stick, two buttons, trigger) in the hand's
  colour, mirrored for the left hand, plus a `Label3D` per bound control at a named position
  constant. Labels are billboarded, drawn without depth test, 32 px font at 0.5 mm per pixel
  (about 16 mm tall).
- `game/xr/xr_rig.gd`: the boxes are replaced by the shapes. The rig has `mapping` (default
  `ThumbstickMapping.new()`) and `show_labels` (on). It rebuilds the labels when the mapping
  is replaced, and each frame compares a content hash of layout, hand and pitch flag so an
  in-place remap is noticed. It only reads the mapping.
- `game/tests/test_control_labels.gd`: 13 tests.
