## Issue

#1212: controller labels float directly over the controller, each beside its button
(PLT-5, PLT-2, ARC-3, DEV-7).

## Summary

- New `xr/control_label_layout.json` and `ControlLabelLayout` (ARC-4) replace the fixed
  column constants in `ControlLabelSet`. Each label starts at the height of its part (plus
  a `rise_m` per control from the data). The stick's cross is centred over the stick.
  Every other label sits just outside the controller at `column_x_m` (30 mm), and
  overlapping rows are pushed down, `row_gap_m` apart. All labels of a hand share one
  depth, the mean depth of their parts, so rows that are apart on the hand stay apart in
  view on any model.
- Leader lines start under the cross and at the inner edge of a column label, and still
  end on the part's point from `controller_anchors.json` (or the measured node).
- On the Quest 3 table, each label is now 23 to 41 mm from its part. The data limit is 60 mm.
- `LABEL_POSITIONS` keeps its name (`controller_model.gd` reads it) but now says how each
  label is placed (`over` or `column`). `TOP_ROW_Y`, `COLUMN_X`, `COLUMN_Z` and `ROW_PITCH`
  are gone.
- `held_hand_m` in the layout file is the usual pose the panel check uses: the right hand
  0.18 m right, 0.32 m below and 0.34 m ahead of the eye, the left hand mirrored.

## Open questions

- The panel check passes because of the held pose. With the hands raised to the older
  neutral pose (0.15 m below the eye, 0.40 m ahead), the controller itself is in front of a
  bottom-corner panel, so its labels cover that panel whatever the layout. If the owner
  holds his hands higher than `held_hand_m`, the labels will still cover a bottom-corner
  panel. The headset check should say whether this pose is the usual one.
- `column_x_m` (30 mm) assumes the Quest 3 controller's head is about 50 mm wide. If text
  overlaps the model, raise it in the data file.
