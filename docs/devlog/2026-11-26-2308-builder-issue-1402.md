## Summary

- `xr/hub_craft_moment.gd` (`HubCraftMoment`, new): the moment over the drawn tree. The cost
  rows' component icons fly into the node and shrink as they arrive; the node's square frame and
  a see-through plate fill it over `craft_fill_seconds` (0.5 s); once the components arrive the
  item's icon grows to `craft_item_peak` (1.25) and settles to rest. Nodes the craft unlocked
  start looking locked (no frame, small icon), then come up to buildable under a soft disc glow
  that fades in over `unlock_glow_seconds` (0.6 s), holds, and fades out as slowly. Everything is
  drawn on the screen's face; nothing else moves.
- `HubTreeView`: compares each drawing with the last one shown. A node that went from buildable
  to built starts the moment; nodes that went from locked to buildable in the same drawing are
  the ones it unlocked. A redraw mid-moment (the stick moving) keeps it, a hub shown again after
  closing starts afresh and never replays it, and hiding the view ends it. `moments_enabled`
  turns it off for the comparison test. The view runs its own `_process`, so `hub_stations.gd`
  (299 lines) is untouched.
- `xr/hub_screen.json` and `HubScreenLook`: ten new keys for the times, sizes, opacities and the
  glow colour, with ranges. `unlock_glow_seconds` must be 0.1 to 0.95 s, so a glow can neither
  flash nor take a second to come up.
- The crafted sound (#1401) was already requested on a successful craft and still is.
- Acceptance: new m6 step `craft_moment` with its own check (`acceptance/checks/craft_moment_check.gd`)
  and profile (`acceptance/fittings/craft_moment_profile.json`, a bank for the needle gun and the
  arc lance it unlocks). The step builds the needle gun through the scene's controls, takes a
  screenshot half way through the fill, plays the moment out, and requires the needle gun drawn
  built and the arc lance drawn buildable. It is its own step rather than a check on
  `workbench_tree`, because that step's profile banks for exactly one build, so nothing it builds
  unlocks a node that can then be built.
