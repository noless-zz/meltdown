## Issue

#1291: room reverb follows the room the ship is in (AUD-1, ARC-4).

## Summary

- `data/audio/reverb.json`: `blend_seconds` and a list of points from a span in metres to
  the reverb's `room_size`, `damping` and `wet`, read along straight lines between them
  and held flat past the ends.
- The reverb comes from the boxes, not from module names (ARC-4): a box's span is the
  geometric mean of its two shorter sides, so a long tunnel still sounds narrow. Where
  boxes overlap the widest is heard, so the change comes at the room's wall either way.
  Hand-built levels (test tunnel, G2) get their reverb the same way. Spans today: the
  straight tunnel 10 m, the six-way room 24 m, the G2 rooms 18 to 19 m.
- `RoomReverbTable` reads the file and gives the setting at a point; `RoomReverbBlend`
  moves from the old room's setting to the new one's over `blend_seconds` of simulation
  time (ticks), so a pause holds it; the first room of a level is heard at once.
- `RoomReverb` (view) makes an `Effects` bus at run time, sending to Master, with one
  `AudioEffectReverb`, and sets it each frame. The sound cues now play on that bus.
  `CombatWiring.feed_sound` follows the ship and resets the blend on a new level.
- Acceptance: `suites/m6/sound.json` step `room_reverb` on the existing
  `bot_g2_level.replay` (hash 802400918, unchanged). The check `room_reverb_changed`
  (`acceptance/checks/room_reverb_check.gd`) re-flies it with the reverb followed: it
  must move by at least 0.1 from the start (it moves 0.365 as the bot leaves the first
  tunnel for the rooms) and never by more in a tick than a blend allows (largest step
  0.009 of 0.0126 allowed), and the hash must stay the pinned one.
