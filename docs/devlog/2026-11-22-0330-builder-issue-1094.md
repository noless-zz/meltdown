## Issue

#1094: the Fitting Bay shows and fits the shield, hull and engine slots (CRF-4, CRF-3,
CRW-2, ARC-4).

## Summary

- `HubModel.fit_slots()` lists `shield`, `hull`, `engine` after the weapon and utility
  slots, with the fitted upgrade or "" (stock) and `part` always "".
- `HubModel.fit("shield" | "hull" | "engine", id)` sets the part slot and runs
  `Fitting.check`; a refusal passes the reason through unchanged and leaves the fitting
  as it was; "" returns the slot to stock. Every successful fit now copies `parts` too,
  and the candidate copy carries `parts`, so a weapon fit keeps a fitted part (and the
  check now sees the parts it would keep).
- `HubText.FITS` maps each part slot to its own kind, so the Fitting Bay offers only
  crafted upgrades of that kind there. An empty part slot reads `slot_stock` ("stock").
- `data/hub/hub.json`: `slot_names` gains Shield, Hull, Engine; new row `slot_stock`.
  `HubWords` requires both.
