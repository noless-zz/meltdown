## Issue

## Summary

- `ShipState.is_destroyed()` (hull at zero). A destroyed ship's `step` ignores thrust, turn and
  afterburner and **stops** (velocity set to zero, not left to damping, which never quite reaches
  zero); energy and shield are frozen. `fire_primary` returns nothing and leaves the cooldown.
- `PartSlotting` and `SalvagePickup` skip a destroyed ship; `ShipReset.apply` refuses one (the
  wreck stays where it was lost).
- `SimEvent.Kind.SHIP_DESTROYED` (appended to the enum): once, on the tick the hull reaches zero,
  for any cause, after every other event of the tick, `vector` the wreck's position. `ExitRule`
  compares the hull at the start and end of the tick, so robot shots, walls, fire, debris and the
  meltdown running out (after SHIP_LOST_IN_MELTDOWN) all report it.
- `ExitRule.outcome` is `LOST` once every ship is destroyed, with or without a reactor; the escape
  and countdown rules are unchanged. `state_hash` adds the outcome only for levels with a reactor,
  as before, so for levels without one the hash moves only through the ship's own state.
- `BotPilot.next_input` sends an empty input for a destroyed ship.
- New acceptance check `ship_destroyed_inert` (`AcceptanceWreckCheck`) and m2 step
  `ship_destroyed_in_test_tunnel`: a slow silent drift into the tunnel's robots (destroyed on tick
  175), then three seconds of full thrust, turn, afterburner and fire. Checks: final hash, outcome
  LOST, and the wreck neither fired nor moved.
