## Summary

- `game/xr/flight_test_session.gd` (`FlightTestSession`, `RefCounted`): built from a
  `FlightTestPlan`. `advance(seconds)` counts the current segment's time and stops counting once it
  has flown its `duration_s`; then `awaiting_answer()` is true. `answer()` takes `fine`, `uneasy` or
  `stop`, refuses anything else, an answer before the segment ends and any answer after the end.
  The first non-`fine` answer or a `fine` on the last segment ends the test. `preset_now()`,
  `seconds_left()`, `current_segment()`, `finished()`, `recommended()` (via `PresetRecommender`),
  `skip()` (preset 1). A rejected or empty plan is over at once with preset 1. No clock, node or
  input device.
- `game/xr/flight_test_panel_text.gd` (`FlightTestPanelText`) and `game/xr/flight_test/panel.json`:
  an instruction per manoeuvre (every one of `FlightTestPlan.MANOEUVRES`, no other), the question,
  the answer choices, the time-left text, the result text and "Change it any time with X", and
  `raise_m`. A bad file gives the built-in words plus an error naming the field.
- `game/xr/flight_test_panel.gd` (`FlightTestPanel`, `Node3D`): three labels drawn with
  `StatusPanelLabels` at the status panel's pixel size and font sizes; `rig_transform()` is the
  status panel's, raised `raise_m` (0.12 m) in its plane. `show_session()` shows the instruction and
  whole seconds left, the question and choices while an answer is due, and at the end the preset's
  number and display name from its preset file. It only reads the session.
