## Summary

- New `game/gen/debris_doors.gd` (`DebrisDoors`, 210 lines): reads the `doors` section and
  places doors. `debris_plan.gd` stays under 300 lines (299) and only calls it.
- `debris.json` gains `doors`: count [0, 2], thickness 0.5 m, after_seconds [10, 60], and the
  spheres' start, reactor and robot distances (15, 6, 2 m). The section is optional at the top
  level (no section, no doors); inside it every key is required and named as `doors.<key>`.
- A door is a box entry centred on the plane where two modules meet (the centre of the
  doorway box), covering the whole opening square, `thickness` deep through it.
- Route rule: any module join except those of the start's module may get a door, the escape
  route's included. A door that would close the last route out is held by `DebrisRouteGuard`
  when it falls due, as spheres are. Loop bridges get no doors.
- RNG: doors draw from their own generators (`output_hash` + "debris doors" and "debris doors
  fall") after the spheres, so `output_hash`, reactor, robots and every sphere are unchanged.
  Fall times are capped by `latest_fall_fraction` the same way as spheres.
