## Issue

#1323: walls with visible thickness, frames at openings and bevelled box edges instead of
edge lines (GFX-2, LGT-1, ARC-4, PRF-2; owner's note on #1292).

## Summary

- New `view/level_faces_frames.gd` (`LevelFrames`): each straight edge of a hole in a face
  that borders wall, and meets wall at one end at least, gets a band `frame_width_m` wide
  sunk `frame_depth_m` into the space behind: a front quad in the wall's plane, a back
  quad, and reveals along the open side. Corners come from a cell grid, so bands never
  overlap. A tunnel floor that opens into a room below is not framed, so a mouth gets one
  frame, not one on each side.
- Limits: a band takes at most a quarter of the hole straight across from it, so frames on
  opposite edges leave at least half of every opening clear; the depth is at most half of
  the space behind, so a frame's back never lies in another wall's plane.
- New `view/level_faces_bevels.gd` (`LevelBevels`): a 45 degree strip `edge_width_m`
  across each wall along every box edge where both walls exist, capped at a quarter of the
  smaller wall side. It replaces `LevelFaces.edge_strips` and the `Edges` mesh.
- Decision on the old keys: `edge_color` and `edge_energy` stay as a dim glow in the bevels
  (new `glow` uniform in `level_wall.gdshader`, black on the walls), so box shapes still
  read in the dark (#609). `edge_width_m` is now the bevel's width (0.3). Energy went from
  0.4 to 0.15. New keys `frame_depth_m` (0.6) and `frame_width_m` (0.3); the width may not
  be more than the depth.
- `LevelView` draws frames in the three wall surfaces and the bevels as one surface in the
  wall material, which casts no shadow.
- Acceptance: step `wall_depth` in `suites/m6/effects.json`. It flies m2's
  `bot_clears_g2_level` (G2, hash 802400918) and takes screenshots at ticks 1275, 1815,
  2340, 2625 and 2745, where the ship faces a doorway 8 to 12 m away. The new check
  `wall_depth_shown` (`acceptance/checks/wall_depth_check.gd`) reads the level view.
