## Summary

- `data/audio/shaft_sounds.json` (ARC-4): its own table, not a section of lane-c's
  `hub_sounds.json`. It plays the base's `hub_point`, `hub_select`, `hub_refused` and
  `hub_launch` files, so no new sound file and no licence entry.
- `xr/shaft_sound_table.gd`: reads and checks the table (a bad key names its field and
  leaves the choice silent), and maps a frame to its sounds. A highlight change is `point`.
  A taken choice is `select`, then `launch`. A confirm the run does not take is `refused`.
- `xr/shaft_choice_sounds.gd`: hangs under the shaft panel, so the sounds come from the panel.
  It plays on the `Effects` bus, applies each sound's `min_gap_s`, and logs what it requested.
  Its clock is the frames' own lengths, so the gaps hold the same in the headset and in a
  scripted run.
- `shaft_choice_wiring.gd`: makes the sounds on `attach`, clears their log on `open`, and
  calls `hear` once per open frame. The call comes after the stick and after the aim's
  pointing, so both kinds of move count. `update` takes the frame length as a new optional
  last argument, and `escape_end_wiring.gd` passes it on.
- Acceptance: new check file `acceptance/checks/shaft_sounds_check.gd` and the
  `shaft_sounds` step in `suites/m6/sound.json`. `acceptance_run_step.gd` gives that step's
  run the shipped shafts, as it does for `shaft_choice_look` (one constant and one
  condition).
- Presentation only: nothing in `core/` changed, and no panel, run or comfort state is
  written (ARC-3, MP-2).
