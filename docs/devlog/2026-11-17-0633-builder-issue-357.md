## Summary

- `game/data/gen/validator.json` gains `escape_speed_mps` (10, accepted 1..100) and
  `escape_margin` (0.8, accepted 0.1..1). A file without them is rejected naming every
  missing key, not only the first.
- `LevelValidator.validate(level, ship_radius, check_reactor = true)` adds two checks after
  `reachable`:
  - `reactor`: fails with no reactor; fails unless one box holds the reactor's position with
    its radius plus a ship's diameter clear; fails if no such box is reachable from the start.
  - `escape`: `EscapePath` from the reactor to the start (the exit, RUN-3), flown at
    `escape_speed_mps`, must take at most `escape_margin` of
    `ReactorState.meltdown_ticks_for_path(path)`, which is the #337 formula when the scaling
    keys are set and `meltdown_seconds` otherwise. An exact tie passes.
  - `check_reactor = false` turns both off; the existing hand-built tests use it.
- `CHECKS` lists the check names. `level_validator.gd` is 247 lines, so the checks stayed in
  it rather than moving to `escape_checks.gd`.
- `seed_sweep.gd` loads `data/gen/reactor.json` and passes it to `to_level_def`, so every
  swept level carries the generator's reactor. Results gain `by_check` (failing seeds per
  check, plus `level`), and the summary line that `tools/validate_seeds.sh` prints ends with
  them.

## Choices worth a look

- **10 m/s escape speed** is half the default ship's top speed (20 m/s), allowing for turns
  and robots. Over seeds 1 to 1,000 the longest reactor-to-exit path is 324 m (32 s at
  10 m/s) against 72 s allowed (80 % of 90 s); the mean is 165 m. G2's path is 149 m (15 s)
  against 71.6 s (80 % of its scaled 89.5 s). So the check has plenty of headroom today; the
  Planner may want a tighter speed or margin once the timer scales on generated levels.
- **Ranges are kept as plain floats**: a `Vector2` stored 0.1 as 0.10000000149, which would
  reject a margin of exactly 0.1.
- When the `start` check fails, `validate` still returns early, so `reactor` and `escape` are
  not reported for that level (both need the start).
