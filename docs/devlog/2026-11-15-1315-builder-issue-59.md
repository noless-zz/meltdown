## Summary

- `Sim.last_events`: rebuilt by every `step`, in ship order and per ship in kind order.
  A refused step (wrong input count) empties it.

- `BoxBounds.contain_impact()`: returns the into-wall velocity; `contain()` keeps its
  float return. Wall impact magnitude is the impact speed, vector the unit normal back
  into the box (the average normal on a corner hit, reported as one event).
