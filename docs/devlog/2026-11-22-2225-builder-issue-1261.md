## Summary

- `ShipState.selected_primary` is gone. `fire_cooldown` became `fire_cooldowns`, one per
  primary slot. `fire_primary(held)` now fires every filled slot that is ready and whose
  energy cost the pool still holds, slot 1 first, and returns the slots that fired. A slot
  not ready or short of energy does not stop the other.
- Hash: the weapons entry keeps its old shape (`["weapons", 0, cooldown of slot 1, ...]`,
  the 0 where the always-0 selected slot was) and slot 2's cooldown joins only once it
  runs. Every pinned replay where slot 2 never fired keeps its hash.
- `ShipState.aim_primary()`: the filled slot that reaches furthest (ties to slot 1). The
  bots (`bot_aim`, `bot_pilot`, `bot_flank`, `bot_escape`) aim and judge reach with it, and
  `CombatWiring.weapon_range` (the reticle) uses it.
- `Projectile.slot` (not hashed) records the slot that fired. The simulation has no gun
  points and still fires from the ship's centre; the view's muzzles (#602) are the game's
  gun points, so `BoltMuzzles.for_slot` draws slot 1 from the right muzzle and slot 2 from
  the left, replacing the old left/right alternation.
- `PartSlotting` with both slots full replaces slot 1 (what `selected_primary` always
  gave) until #1262 adds the choice.
- `CombatWiring.active_weapon` (read `selected_primary`, used only by a test) is removed.
  The status panel puts the fire mark on every filled slot; `show_weapons` lost its
  `selected` argument.
- Left trigger: no change; `test_left_trigger_is_unbound_until_secondaries_exist` still
  holds.
- Acceptance: new m2 step `both_primaries_fire` (after `pickup_in_test_tunnel`) with a new
  check `both_primaries_fired`. The replay is the pickup step's flight, then a slot press
  that puts the needle gun in slot 2, then the trigger held until both slots fire on one
  tick (158 ticks). Placed before the last step because
  `test_m2_suite_passes_in_the_flat_main_scene` expects no robots left after the suite.
