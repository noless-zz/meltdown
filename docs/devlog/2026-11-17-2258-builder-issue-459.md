## Issue

#459 Debris view draws box-shaped meltdown entries as door and cave-in slabs (RUN-4, ARC-4,
CMF-8). The open lane-c pull request #483 (#458) had green CI, merged cleanly with `main`
and had no change request, so nothing needed fixing first. Prerequisite #457 is merged.

## Summary

- `SessionSnapshots.debris` has a new sibling key `DEBRIS_SIZES` (`"sizes_m"`): a box's
  full size in metres, `Vector3.ZERO` for a sphere. The other keys keep their values.
- `DebrisView.show_debris` takes an optional `sizes_m` array. A box entry is a unit
  `BoxMesh` scaled to its size, axis-aligned at its position, with the same hidden,
  warning and solid stages and the shared `warn_seconds`. A held box is not drawn: a
  see-through slab across an opening would read as a closed door where the route is open.
  Held spheres keep their cracked-shell look.
- New look keys in `debris_look.json` with ranges and built-in fallbacks: `slab_color`,
  `slab_energy`, `slab_warn_color` (hazard yellow), `slab_warn_energy`, `slab_warn_alpha`.
- Not wired into the main scene: `meltdown_wiring.gd` is lane-b and #479 passes the sizes.
  Without them every entry is still a sphere, as before.
