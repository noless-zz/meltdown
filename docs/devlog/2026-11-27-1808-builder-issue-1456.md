## Summary

- New `xr/hull_warning_table.gd` (`HullWarningTable`) reads `data/audio/hull_warning.json`:
  threshold (optional; without it, the status panel's `low_hull_fraction`), `interval_s`,
  `fast_threshold`, `fast_interval_s`, `sound` and `volume_db`. A wrong value or an unknown
  key names its field and leaves the warning silent. `interval_for(hull)` is 0 above the
  threshold.
- New `xr/hull_warning.gd` (`HullWarning`): the beat. The first tone sounds on the frame the
  hull reaches the threshold, then every interval. Falling into the faster band brings the next
  tone within the faster interval. It stops on the frame the hull rises or the caller silences
  it. The speaker is an `AudioStreamPlayer3D` on the `Effects` bus, placed each frame 0.35 m
  below and 0.5 m ahead of the head point, on the dash. It keeps a log of tones (count and
  hull at each), for tests and the acceptance check.
- `destroyed_wiring.gd` holds one. It is fed the hull fraction the low-hull pulse already
  reads, with paused frames counted as 0 s. It is silenced while any end screen or the shaft
  choice is up, at zero hull, or once the run's outcome is no longer RUNNING. `main.gd` does not
  update `DestroyedWiring` while the base is open, so it never sounds there. `attach` hangs the
  speaker under the rig and `reset` clears it. Nothing in `core/` changes (MP-2).
- `tools/synth_hull_warning.py` writes `hull_warning.wav` (0.26 s two-note chirp, B5 then G5).
  Re-running it writes the same bytes. Recorded in `docs/licences.md` (NAM-3).
- Acceptance: a new `hull_warning` step in `suites/m6/sound.json`, with check
  `acceptance/checks/hull_warning_check.gd`. The step flies through the scene's frames, so the
  real wiring drives the tone. The check wants at least two tones, each while the ship had
  hull, and none over 180 more frames under the lost-ship screen.
- New replay `low_hull_test_tunnel.replay`. The only replay that hurts the ship
  (`ship_destroyed_test_tunnel`) loses it 10 ticks after the hull crosses 25 %, too soon for a
  second tone. Backing away at several points did not help, because the robots finish the ship
  within a second either way. The new replay is that replay with `max_hull` 200 and 148 idle
  ticks added. The hull stays low for about 1.8 s and the ship is lost on tick 459, so the step
  hears 3 tones.
