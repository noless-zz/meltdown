## Summary

- `acceptance/acceptance_time_limit.gd`: a step's limit is its own `time_limit_seconds`, else
  its suite's new `default_time_limit_seconds`, else 120 s. The default is counted on top of
  the time the step is told to wait (`settle_seconds`, `measure_seconds`, `view_seconds`,
  `pause_seconds`), so the soak gets 1625 s and needs no special case. The clock is wall time
  from the step's first frame, since a stuck step may fly no ticks.
- `acceptance/acceptance_step_record.gd`: recording a step moved out of the runner, plus the
  timeout path. A timed-out step is failed with `timed out after N s at tick T`, its replay is
  let go, the scene processes again, `end_step` puts back what the step changed, and the next
  step starts. Its checks are not run.
- `acceptance/acceptance_run_output.gd`: the step lines' `suite=<name> index=<n> count=<total>`
  fields, after the existing text; the suite report write (unchanged: still as each suite
  ends); and `acceptance_exit.json` (`build`, `suites`, `passed`, `failed`, `skipped`,
  `exit_code`, `finished`), written just before the exit line. The fields match what lane-b's
  `tools/acceptance_recover.ps1` already reads.
- `acceptance_runner.gd` uses these and stays under the 260-line cap that
  `test_acceptance_checks.gd` sets for it.
- `acceptance/checks/README.md` documents the two keys and what a run leaves for the scripts.
- No suite file, no `tools/` file and neither pause nor view-flat check was edited.
