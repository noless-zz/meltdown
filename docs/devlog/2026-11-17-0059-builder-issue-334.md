## Summary

- The bot could not beat G2 as it was: every G2 robot is awake from the start, the bot
  slowed ever more on its way into each opening and sat at the first corner while the drone
  and skimmer shot it from the shaft; it died at tick 393 with five robots left. Fixed in
  `game/net/bot_pilot.gd`, in the level (routed) branch only, so the test tunnel flight is
  unchanged:
  - `CORNER_SPEED` (3 m/s): the slowest it flies towards an opening it is passing through.
  - `shot_reaches`: a shot is dropped once it ends a tick outside every box, so the bot
    checks those same points. It fires only when a shot gets there, keeps its nose on a
    robot in sight (thrust is in ship space, so the route is flown anyway), leads a moving
    target, and answers the nearest robot it can reach before a nearer one behind rock.
  - It waits where it stands while a robot out of sight closes on it or its shield
    recharges, and falls back to where it last stood out of fire once its shield is below
    `RETREAT_SHIELD` (20) under fire.
  - With this it clears G2 in 4064 ticks (68 s) with hull 7.6. The margin is thin; the
    threshold sweep (5, 10, 20 win; 15, 25 lose) shows the fight is sensitive.
- `game/acceptance/replays/bot_g2_level.replay`: the recording (six boxes, start at the
  origin, the six robots as `LocalSession.for_level` arms them, and its part catalogue from
  #320). No reactor (see open questions).
- #310 and #320 merged while this PR waited for CI; rebased, re-recorded (the part lines
  change the hash to 2319032124) and taught the bot that an intact reactor takes its shots.
  A robot behind the reactor is out of reach, and the bot closes in on an unreachable target
  rather than holding off at 15 m.
- `m2.json`: new step `bot_clears_g2_level` with `level: "g2"` and checks `final_hash`,
  `robots_cleared`, `ship_alive`.
- `AcceptanceStep.level` / suite key `level` (needs a replay; name checked like `--level=`).
  The runner calls `main.use_level` before the replay, then flies the replay in a session
  that takes over all of its boxes (`ReplayInputSource.new_session`).
- `AcceptanceStateChecks` (new file) holds `final_hash`, `robots_cleared` (names every
