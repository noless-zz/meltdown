## Issue

#870 (M3, RUN-4, CMB-3, ARC-2): the bot's route goes round a cave-in that splits a tunnel.

## Summary

- `LevelRoute.close_blocked` now also asks `BoxSplit.splits` (the core helper from #868,
  not a copy) for each box's splits, in whole millimetres.
- `LevelRoute.chain` searches over parts of boxes (`BoxSplit.part`): an opening leads on
  from a box only from the part its centre lies in. A box may appear twice in a chain
  when the route leaves a split tunnel by one end and comes back in by the other. With no
  splits every part is zero and the search visits the same boxes in the same order as
  before.
- `FireDetour` is unchanged. It knows only openings, so `LevelRoute.waypoints` drops a
  fire detour whose leg has its two ends in different parts of a split box and keeps the
  plain route. With no splits this never happens.
