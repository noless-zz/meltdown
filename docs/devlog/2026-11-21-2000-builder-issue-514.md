## Summary

- `debris.json` gains a `caveins` section (`count` [0, 1], `thickness` 1.0 m,
  `after_seconds` [10, 60], distances 15 / 6 / 2 m), read by the new
  `game/gen/debris_caveins.gd` (`DebrisCaveins`); errors name `caveins.<key>`. No section,
  no cave-ins.
- A straight tunnel is a placed module with exactly two connectors facing opposite ways on
  one line; only `straight_tunnel` qualifies today. The cave-in is centred mid-way along
  that line, `thickness` deep, and covers the module box's whole cross-section, so
  `BoxSplit` splits the box (#868) and the route model, guard, map and bot see it closed.
  The start's module gets none.
- To know a placement's connectors without its `ModuleDef`, `ModulePlacement` now keeps
  `connectors` (every connector as placed), filled in `MineGenerator._place`. Not part of
  `output_hash`.
- `DebrisPlan.place`: cave-ins go in after the doors' trim and before fire, from their own
  streams ("debris caveins", "debris caveins fall"), then `DebrisEscape.trim` runs again.
  Cave-ins are the highest-numbered solid entries, so the trim drops them before any earlier
  entry. Fire is drawn after, so its entries are as before, one index later.
- `test_generated_doors.gd` and `test_door_loop_weight.gd` leave cave-ins out of the file
  they read (they are boxes and would count as doors); the doors sweep's door list also
  skips them, so it keeps passing when #988's shared levels (which keep cave-ins) land.
- The validator sweep line gains `caveins N, caveins_held N` after the fires
  (`sweep_debris_kinds.gd`): a box at a straight tunnel's middle is a cave-in, otherwise a
  door. `seed_sweep.gd` passes the generator for that (one line).
- m3 `bot_generated_fire_escape` (seed 29): level hash 3117525271 → 1212183063, re-recorded
  with `MELTDOWN_RECORD_M3=bot_generated_fire_escape`. Final hash 3137378879 and the replay
  are unchanged. Every other m3 step's hashes are unchanged.
