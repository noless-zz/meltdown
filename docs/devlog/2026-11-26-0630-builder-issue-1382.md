## Summary

- `view/visible_boxes.gd` (`VisibleBoxes`, new): takes a cull's per-box flags and says
  whether a thing at a point, reaching a radius round it, is drawn: yes when its reach
  touches a shown box, or when no box holds it; no when only hidden boxes hold it. Shown
  boxes are tried first, so the common case is cheap. It counts hidden answers per cull.
  `VisibleBoxes.of_world(world)` finds the level view and culls it now.
- `view/level_view.gd`: keeps a `VisibleBoxes` per build and feeds it each cull.
  `cull_camera` culls once per process frame. The combat wiring calls it after the rig is
  placed and before the item views draw, so walls and items use the same frame's boxes.
  `_process` still covers frames nobody asked. Culling off shows everything.
- Each item view has a `boxes` field and applies it where it already sets `visible`:
  robots, robot blasts and their sparks, floating parts, their rings, component heaps,
  miner markers, bolts (reach = bolt + trail), impact, hit and muzzle flashes, wall and
  hit sparks, scorches, and debris (lane-c file, allowed by the Planner's note).
- Bolt sparks and scorches used `visible` to mean "still running". They now go by age
  (`is_running`, `is_fading`), so a burst or mark hidden with its box keeps going and
  shows again when its box is drawn. The same goes for riding flashes in `follow`.
- `combat_wiring.gd` and `meltdown_wiring.gd` hand the boxes to the views. The test tunnel
  has no level view, so they hand null and everything is drawn.
- The `portal_visibility_counted` check now also reports, per frame, the robots, parts,
  heaps, miners and bolts in the flight and how many sit only in hidden boxes
  (`portal_items_mean`, `portal_items_hidden_mean`, `portal_items_hidden_most`).

Not changed: bolt lights (they light the drawn boxes through openings), pickup marks and
miner labels and leave copies (they start at the ship), debris dust.
