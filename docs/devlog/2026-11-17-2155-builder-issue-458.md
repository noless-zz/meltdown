## Summary

- `OpeningClearance.blocked`, the one test that `DebrisRouteGuard`, `KnownRoute` (map) and
  `LevelRoute` (bot escape) all use, now handles box entries. Across the opening a box keeps
  the ship's line out of its cross-section grown by the ship's reach, which shrinks with
  the box's distance along the crossing axis as a sphere's does. That shape is a rectangle
  with round corners, kept as two rectangles and four corner discs. The candidate points
  are now every crossing of the lines of fixed u and v (opening sides and rectangle edges)
  with each other and with disc edges, plus disc-disc crossings. With spheres only these
  are exactly the points tested before, so sphere answers are unchanged.
- `DebrisRouteGuard._fallen` copies `sizes_mm`, so the guard tests a door as a door and not
  as a point.
- `LevelRoute._meets_debris` tests a straight leg against a solid box grown by the ship's
  radius with square corners. That is a little conservative at the edges, so the bot never
  grazes a door.
