#1309: weapon effects. A shot, trail and impact per weapon, muzzles flash per slot (GFX-2,
GFX-4, GFX-6, CMB-1, PLT-6, ARC-1, ARC-4, ARC-5, NAM-3), plus the owner's relayed note of

## Summary

- `projectile_look.json` gains a `weapons` section keyed by weapon id (robots use their
  weapon's id, then `robot`) and new keys: `bolt_shape` (cylinder, capsule, ellipsoid,
  dart), `trail_length_m`, `trail_color`, wall `spark_*` and `scorch_*`, and `hit_*` for
  hits on robots and the ship. Every key is now optional: a missing key keeps today's look
  (`ProjectileLook.DEFAULTS`, with trails, sparks and scorches off). The shipped file gives
  the four player weapons, the four crafted ones and the robot weapons their own shape,
  colour, trail, muzzle flash size and spark colour.
- `ProjectileWeaponLooks` parses and merges the rows; `BoltMeshes` builds one head and one
  trail mesh per weapon from primitives. A trail never reaches further back than the bolt
  has flown from its muzzle, so a new shot does not trail into the cockpit.
- `BoltImpacts` tells where a bolt ended, from the walls (`LineOfSight.exit_fraction`, read
  only), the robots a tick before and the ship poses: a wall gets the impact flash, sparks
  thrown off the face (`BoltSparks`, one multimesh per burst) and a scorch decal that fades
  over `scorch_fade_s` (`BoltScorches`, radial gradient made in code, capped at 24); a
  robot gets the hit flash and hit sparks every way; the ship gets hit sparks thrown away
  from the pilot and still no flash in the face.
- Muzzle flashes already flashed per slot (#1261); each now takes its weapon's size.
- `RobotBursts`: the flat translucent sphere becomes a shell with a bright rim and a
  see-through middle plus a hot core that shrinks first (`blast_glow.gdshader`, additive,
  so robots behind stay visible) and a spark burst. The balls stay 0.75 m short of the eye
  and fade as the viewer gets close. `CombatWiring` gives the bursts the camera and the
  projectile view the scene (ship, previous robots, walls).
- No light added: bolt lights stay under `max_lights` (test and acceptance check).
