## Summary

- New `game/view/debris_view.gd` (`DebrisView`, `Node3D`) with
  `show_debris(positions_m, radii_m, solid, held, seconds_to_fall)`. It takes plain arrays
  only (MP-2) and is not wired into the main scene; that is #422 (lane-b).
- One `MeshInstance3D` per entry, reused across calls; nodes past the last entry are freed.
  Two shared unit spheres (whole rock, 10x5 facets; cracked, 5x3) and three shared
  materials, so per-frame calls build nothing.
- Looks: solid is an opaque rock colour with a faint glow (the mine is dark, LGT-1); an
  entry still to fall is hidden until `warn_seconds` before it falls, then a see-through
  orange glow; held is a coarser see-through shell in `held_color`. Held wins over solid.
  The glow is steady (no flashing) and no lights are added (GFX-4 budget untouched).
- New `game/view/debris_look.gd` (`DebrisLook`) and `debris_look.json`: three colours,
  three emission energies, `warn_alpha`, `held_alpha`, `warn_seconds` (0.5..10). Every
  missing, unknown or out-of-range key is named; the view keeps the built-in look on a
  rejected or missing file.

## Open questions

- Held debris is drawn see-through (`held_alpha`), not as an opaque rock: the entry never
  became solid, so an opaque sphere would show a sealed tunnel where the route is open. The
  issue asked for "a cracked, unfallen look"; the owner may prefer something else in the
  headset.
- The warning is a steady glow, not a ramp toward the fall: the material is shared between
  entries, so a per-entry ramp would need per-entry materials or a shader parameter.
- Headset (with #422): debris reads as danger before it falls; a held cave-in reads as
  "this one held" and not as a wall.
