## Summary

- `nightly-seeds.yml` gains two jobs beside the validator job, neither waiting for it:
  - `bot-escape`: a matrix of eight shards, each flying its slice of seeds through
    `game/tests/bot_escape_sweep.gd --robots --debris` and uploading its output.
  - `bot-escape-rate`: adds up the shards, writes the rate and every shard's summary line
    to the step summary, and fails below 0.95, when a summary line is missing, repeated or
    does not parse, when it comes from a run without robots or debris, when the seeds do not
    add up, when nothing was cleared, or when a shard job failed.
- New `workflow_dispatch` input `bot_seeds` (default 1000).
- New `tools/bot_escape_rate.sh` (`range` splits the seeds over shards, `combine` parses and
  judges) and `tools/test_bot_escape_rate.sh`, which each shard runs first. Both are covered
  by CI's `bash -n` and `shellcheck` over `tools/*.sh`, as `validate_seeds.sh` is.
- Owner's limits kept: `ubuntu-latest` only, no secrets, `permissions` unchanged at
  `contents: read`, no `pull_request` trigger. `ci.yml` is not touched.

## Test results

- `tools/test_bot_escape_rate.sh`: all 24 checks pass. `shellcheck tools/*.sh` and
  `actionlint` on the workflow are clean.
- Godot 4.7.2-stable in the run's scratch space, `--import` then `run_tests.gd`: 1362 tests,
  0 failed (no game file changed).
- Timing on this two-core machine with robots and debris: 20 seeds in 26 s and 40 seeds in
  45 s, about 1.2 s a seed, so 1,000 seeds would be over 20 minutes in one job. Eight shards
  of 125 should take about 2.5 minutes of flying each. The real run time is recorded after
  the first nightly or manual run.
- A local three-shard run of seeds 1..30 through `range`, the sweep and `combine`: 3 escaped
  of 3 cleared. One shard cleared nothing and its sweep exited 1, which the shard job
  tolerates as intended.

## Open questions

- With robots and debris the bot clears few levels: 3 of 30 and 1 of 40 seeds here; most
  flights end with the ship destroyed before the reactor. Over 1,000 seeds that is roughly
  50 cleared levels, so the 0.95 bar rests on a small sample, and the G3 rate says nothing
  about how often the bot dies on the way in. The Planner may want an issue on the bot's
  clear rate with robots.
- The workflow cannot be run from a pull request; its first run is the next nightly at
  23:17 UTC or a manual dispatch after merge.
