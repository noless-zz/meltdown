## Summary

- `level_wall.gdshader` plates every surface under #609's grid: panels with dark seams and a
  slight per-panel tone, all worked out from the world position (no texture). The shader picks
  floor, wall or ceiling from the face normal with the same 45 degree rule as
  `LevelLook.surface_class` (threshold passed in as `floor_min_up`), so one material per class
  still works and the BoxMesh shell of the test tunnel is classed per face.
- Shipped shapes: floor 1 m square plates; walls 1 m high, 2 m long, every row shifted half a
  plate (running bond, laid level); ceiling 0.5 m wide, 2 m long strips. Tones stay the existing
  `floor_color`, `wall_color` and `ceiling_color`.
- Anti-shimmer: seams are softened by `fwidth` like the grid lines, and all plate detail fades
  out once a plate is under about three pixels on screen.
- `level_look.json` gains eleven keys: `plate_<kind>_m`, `plate_<kind>_aspect`,
  `plate_<kind>_stagger` for floor, wall and ceiling, `plate_seam_m` and `plate_contrast`
  (capped at 0.25; shipped 0.12). A seam wider than a quarter of any kind's plate is rejected
  by name. Fallback matches the file.
