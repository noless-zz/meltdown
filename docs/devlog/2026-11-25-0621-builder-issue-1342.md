## Summary

- `core/alarm.gd` (`Alarm`): rules from `data/alarm/alarm.json` (rise per second, per
  ship shot, per robot woken, cap, event steps), every key checked and named on error.
  The sim owns a copy with the shaft's risk multiplier, the level and the steps passed.
  Each tick: quiet rise plus shots fired this tick plus robots that woke this tick,
  times the risk, capped. Nothing rises once the outcome is decided. Crossing a step adds
  one new `SimEvent.Kind.ALARM_RAISED` (appended last, so earlier kinds keep their numbers).
- `Sim.set_alarm_rules(rules, risk)`, before the first tick only. `SimHash` joins the
  alarm only while it is set, so every sim without one keeps its hash.
- Run levels get the alarm through `RunLevelRules`, at the chosen shaft's `risk.alarm`
  (`RunWiring.level_alarm_scale`). A level flown alone, the tunnel and replays without the
  line have no alarm.
- Replay format version 20: an optional `alarm {...,"risk":r}` line
  (`ReplayAlarmLine`, called through `ReplayRulesLine` because `replay.gd` must stay under
  300 lines). A replay without it is written as before.
- `SessionSnapshots.alarm_level` is the view's copy (a plain number), and
  `MusicWiring.alarm_level` reads it. The music check follows it too.
- Acceptance: `music_follows_alarm` now flies the new `bot_g2_escape_alarm.replay` (the G2
  escape with an alarm line, same inputs). `checks/music.json` expects `calm`,
  `tension_low`, `meltdown`, `faded`. The alarm passes 0.35 at tick 3446 and the reactor
  goes at tick 4113 (alarm 0.446).
- `ALARM_RAISED` cue row in `cues.json` with `alarm_raised.wav`, a two-tone chime written
  by `tools/synth_sounds.py`. The other wavs come out byte-identical. Licence row updated.
