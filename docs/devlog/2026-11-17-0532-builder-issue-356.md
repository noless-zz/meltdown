## Summary

- New data file `game/data/gen/reactor.json`: `radius` 2.5, `hull` 400, `meltdown_seconds` 90
  (as G2), `min_hops` 2.
- New `game/gen/reactor_plan.gd` (`ReactorPlan`): reads that file. Everything except
  `min_hops` goes through `ReactorState.parse`, so the keys and checks are those of a level's
  `reactor`; `position` and `exit_radius` are refused as unknown keys.
- Level building moved out of `mine_generator.gd` into `game/gen/generated_level.gd`
  (`GeneratedLevel`), as the issue suggested. `MineGenerator.to_level_def(reactor_plan, ship_radius)`
  delegates; both arguments are optional, so existing callers (seed sweep, validator tests)
  still get a level without a reactor.
- Placement: joins from the first module come from one pass in placement order (parents are
  always placed first, so this equals the breadth-first walk); the furthest placement wins,
  ties to the later one. The reactor sits at the centre of that module's largest box (ties to
  the lower box), in whole millimetres. It is left out, with `MineGenerator.reactor_note`, if
  the furthest module is under `min_hops` joins away or that box can't hold the reactor's
  radius plus a ship's diameter on every side. `reactor_placement` gives the module index.
- `output_hash()` is unchanged (placements only).

## Choices worth a look

- **`min_hops` is 2, not higher.** Over seeds 0 to 999 with 10 modules, the furthest module is
  2 joins away on 4 seeds and 3 on 153 (the four-way room makes bushy levels). The criterion
  "every 10-module level has a reactor" therefore rules out 3. The Planner may want the
  generator to prefer longer chains later.
- **"Centre of the module" is the centre of its largest box.** The bounding-box centre of
  `corner_90` lies in rock, so it could not be used.
- **The note is `reactor_note`, not `note`.** `note` means "stopped short" and the seed sweep
  counts it, so a reactor note there would be miscounted as a short level.
