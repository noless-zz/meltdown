## Issue

#22 Test tunnel: 5 m and 20 m marker labels overlap (PLT-2).

## Summary

- `game/view/test_tunnel.gd`: `MARKER_OFFSETS_X` changes from `[-0.5, 0.0, 0.5]` to
  `[-0.5, 1.0, 0.0]`. The 5 m marker moves 1 m to the right and the 20 m marker to the
  centre line, where its wide label stays clear of both walls. Distances stay 1 m, 5 m
  and 20 m. Nothing else in the tunnel changed.
- `game/tests/test_tunnel_markers.gd` (new, 4 tests): builds the tunnel and measures the
  view angle between every pair of markers, and every pair of labels, and fails below
  3 degrees; checks the three distances; checks that the measurement reports the old
  layout as too close.

Smallest angle between two markers, in degrees:

| Seen from | Old balls | Old labels | New balls | New labels |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Floor origin | 11.7 | 13.7 | 15.9 | 17.2 |
| 1.2 m (seated) | 2.2 | 4.5 | 11.4 | 12.0 |
| 1.4 m (marker height) | 1.4 | 2.9 | 11.3 | 11.5 |
| 1.7 m (standing) | 2.9 | 1.4 | 11.6 | 11.3 |

The issue asks for the angle "from the origin". From the floor origin the old layout
already measured 11.7 degrees, because the markers hang at 1.4 m and spread vertically
when seen from below. A test from that point alone would have passed on the layout that
failed in the headset, so the test also measures from three eye heights.
