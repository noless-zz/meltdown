## Summary

- New `game/acceptance/acceptance_hit_motion_check.gd` (`AcceptanceHitMotionCheck`,
  check `hit_view_motion`). Each rendered frame it takes the camera's world transform,
  removes the tracked head, the recentre offset, the ship's interpolated pose and preset
  4's inertia drift (#840 merged during this run; the drift is the acceleration row, not
  hits), exactly as `main.place_rig` builds the rig, and splits what is left into shake (eye movement,
  over 0.5 mm) and spin (view roll, over 0.05°).
- The limits come from `ComfortWiring.preset_for(number)`, the preset file for the
  number in force, not from the wiring's live `preset`. Reading the live preset would let
  a stand-in (a wiring fault) judge itself; the issue's own stand-in tests need this.
- `hit_view_motion` joins `KNOWN_CHECKS`, needs a replay like `comfort_motion`, and has
  one judge in `AcceptanceChecks` plus a sample in `after_tick`.
- `test_acceptance_runner.gd`: the flat-scene m1 frame limit goes to 30000 (24000 after
  #818), since the three new flights do not fit. Rebased once on main for that conflict.
- `m1.json`: `comfort_hits_preset_2`, `_3`, `_4` on the destroyed-ship replay, hash
  3298909347.
