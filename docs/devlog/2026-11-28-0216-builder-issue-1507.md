## Summary

- New `xr/eye_views.json` and its loader `xr/eye_views.gd` (`EyeViews`): each eye as its
  own point (IPD apart) with its own asymmetric frustum, the right eye the left's mirror,
  and a margin kept clear inside every edge. Starting values for the Quest 3: 52° outward,
  42° inward, 44° up, 52° down, 63 mm IPD, 3° margin. On a headset the
  `status_panel_place` check reads the runtime's own projections instead
  (`EyeViews.from_interface`) and reports them in the step's measurements.
- The model reproduces the owner's report: the old bottom-left corner reached 43° to the
  left, 5.2° past the right eye's inner edge (less the margin); the right corners clipped
  the left eye by about 1.5°.
- The four corners in `xr/status_panel_look.json` (and `FALLBACK`) moved to 0.65 m out,
  24..31° to the side, 18..27° up or down, found by a search over every rule at once.
- `StatusPanelBounds`: the panel's centre stays outside the 25° cone, as the criterion
  says; drawn rows stay outside a narrower 20° cone (`ROW_CONE_DEG`). Keeping every row
  outside 25° could not also fit both eyes and the 30° glance down that the bottom-left
  default needs. New `centre_angle` and `eyes_problem`.
- The `status_panel_place` step now flies `status_panel_flight.replay` (the first 300
  ticks of `basic_flight`: straight, then turning and rolling) and checks both eyes per
  preset, the centre cone and the row cone.
