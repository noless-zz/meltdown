## Issue

#1292: physically based mine walls from text materials, triplanar, per theme (GFX-2,
LGT-1, ARC-4, ARC-5, PRF-2).

## Summary

- New data file `view/level_wall_material.json` with a default theme and a `themes`
  table; one theme (`rock`) for now, as the game has one mine theme (GEN-4 comes in M7).
- New `LevelWallMaterial` (`view/level_wall_material.gd`) parses it. Ten keys per theme:
  noise scale, albedo and roughness noise, metallic, metal share and metal roughness,
  normal strength, bevel strength and width, triplanar sharpness. A missing key takes its
  `NEUTRAL` value; a missing or rejected file, or an unknown theme, gives `NEUTRAL` for
  every key with a warning naming the field. `NEUTRAL` draws the walls exactly as before.
- `level_wall.gdshader`: three octaves of sin-free value noise with analytic derivatives,
  projected on the three axis planes and blended by the normal (axis-aligned faces skip
  the two unused projections). It varies albedo and roughness around the `level_look.json`
  tint and roughness and dents the normal; a share of the plates (chosen per plate) is
  bare metal; plate edges are chamfered in the normal so seams read as cut edges. Noise
  octaves and bevels fade out where they get under about three pixels, like the plating.
  No textures (ARC-5). All `level_look.json` keys keep their meaning.
- `LevelLook.wall_material` takes an optional theme; empty means the shipped default, so
  `LevelView` and `TestTunnel` pick it up without changes.
- Acceptance: m6 `look` part has its first step, `wall_material`: the m1 frame-budget
  sweep of the test tunnel (headlight on the walls), with `final_hash` and
  `no_script_errors`, screenshots at ticks 300, 900 and 1800 and at the end.

## Owner's note on thin walls

The owner's 23 Nov comment asked to make wall thickness part of this issue. The plate
bevels are in here. Visible depth at openings and tunnel mouths, and bevelled box edges
in place of the emissive edge lines, change the level geometry (`LevelFaces`) and the
meaning of the `edge_*` look keys, so they are a follow-up issue labelled `triage`
(builder routine step 8).
