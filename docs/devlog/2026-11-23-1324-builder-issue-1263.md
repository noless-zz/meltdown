## Summary

- `xr/slot_popup_input.gd` (`SlotPopupInput`): takes the mapped `ShipInput`. With a part
  held and both primary slots full, the slot click opens the popup; while open, pitch and
  yaw are zeroed (roll, thrust and fire pass through) and a flick past `flick_threshold`
  sends left = slot 2, right = slot 1, down = dump. Up chooses nothing. A second click
  cancels. It closes by itself, sending nothing, when the hold empties, a slot empties,
  the ship is destroyed or escaped, `ShipHold` says the ship is held (menu, flight test,
  pause, lost ship) or the sim's outcome is no longer running.
- Two choices the issue left open:
  - A frame's input flies every tick until the next frame, and a 90 Hz frame often flies
    none, so the choice is sent until the sim's tick has moved on, then dropped. #1262's
    `PartSlotting` acts on it once.
  - The stick must rest (inside `rest_threshold`) after the popup opens before a flick
    counts, and after it closes before it turns the ship again. Otherwise a click that
    nudges the stick could choose, and the stick still held out after a flick would yaw
    the ship at once.
- Which stick chooses: the hand whose stick click is bound to `slot_part`, after
  left-handed mode's swap (`mapping.physical_controls`), so left-handed mode and a
  remapped click both follow (PLT-5). No layout file changed.
- `xr/slot_popup.gd` (`SlotPopup`): three `Label3D` choices and a "Click: cancel" hint,
  under the choosing hand's controller, `height_m` above the `stick_click` point of
  `controller_anchors.json` (mirrored for the left hand). It turns to face the eye each
  frame so left reads as left. View only.
- `xr/slot_popup.json` and `xr/slot_popup_look.gd`: wording, sizes, distances, colours
  and the two thresholds, validated by name (ARC-4).
- `combat_wiring.gd`: owns the step and the view; `slot_input` reads the stick from the
  raw values and the ship, tick and outcome from the session.
- `main.gd`: two lines in `apply_controls`. The issue asked to keep out of `main.gd`, but
  no lane-b file sits between the mapping and `comfort.hand_input`; `ShipHold` and
  `CombatWiring` are never handed the input. The change only routes the input through
  `combat.slot_input` and computes `ShipHold.is_held` once. `main.gd` is now 300 lines.
