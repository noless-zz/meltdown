## Summary

- New `game/gen/loop_shapes.gd` (`LoopShapes`): the straight tunnel (unchanged shape) and a
  Z for openings offset sideways: a leg out of each doorway running half a tunnel width past
  the middle of the gap, and a cross box a tunnel width deep along the axis that covers both
  legs' squares. Every bend overlaps by at least the connector's width on every axis. No Z
  when the gap is shorter than a tunnel width (it would reach into the far module).
- `LoopBridges` reads a new key `loop_max_offset_m` (0..1000, named when missing or bad),
  compares the sideways offset exactly in squared whole millimetres, and checks every tunnel
  box against every placed box; doorways as before. Pairs in line still get the straight
  tunnel. Placement is untouched, so seeds that gain no loop keep their `output_hash`.
- `generator.json`: `loop_max_offset_m` 40.

## The 10% target is not reached

Seeds 0..999 with a loop on the shipped modules, by `loop_max_offset_m`:

| offset (m) | 0 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 60 | 100 | no limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| looped seeds | 3 | 8 | 23 | 28 | 35 | 38 | 47 | 58 | 67 | 77 | 80 |

The rate stops at 8.0% with no offset limit at all. Over the 1,000 tree levels there are
about 11.8 open connectors per level, but only ~550 same-axis pairs facing each other with a
positive gap in all; 276 of those are more than 40 m apart sideways, 131 are closer along
the axis than a tunnel width, and of the ~150 left within 40 m, about 70 fit (the rest are
blocked at the cross box, a leg or a doorway). The limit is the placement, not the shape.
As the issue asks, the best rate was commented on #411 and option 2 (biased placement) is
left to the Planner. The shipped 40 m gives 5.8%, up from 0.3%; past 40 m the cross tunnel
gets long for little gain.
