## Summary

- `SessionSnapshots.reactor(source)`: a copy of the reactor for the view, null for null.
- New `game/meltdown_wiring.gd` (`MeltdownWiring`) beside `main.gd`. `attach(world, session)`
  creates a `ReactorView` under the main node (the simulation's space) when the session has a
  reactor, so no frame goes without it; the test tunnel gets none. `update(session, panel)`
  runs every frame: it feeds `show_state` from the snapshot (position, radius, hull fraction,
  destroyed, `meltdown_ticks_left / 60.0`), calls `show_meltdown(seconds)` while the reactor
  is destroyed and `show_meltdown(-1.0)` otherwise, and `show_outcome(sim.outcome())` with or
  without a reactor. A session that loses its reactor has the view removed.
- `main.gd`: three lines for the wiring (field, attach, update). It was at exactly 300 lines,
  so the one-line `log_afterburner` wrapper was inlined into `advance_frame` first (no test
