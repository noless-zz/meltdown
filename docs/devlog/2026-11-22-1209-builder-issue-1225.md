## Summary

- New `view/robot_look_reach.gd` (`RobotLookReach`): the furthest point of each body and
  part, sampled on the same unit shapes `RobotView.build_mesh` draws, in units of the
  collision radius. Tells only rotate the model about its centre, so the rest pose is
  the worst pose.
- `view/robot_look.json`: every robot reached past its radius (drone 1.50, glider 2.08,
  hauler 1.67, needler 4.48, skimmer 1.62, warden 1.98 radii). Each now shrinks
  uniformly through `scale`: drone 0.66, glider 0.48, hauler 0.54, skimmer 0.61,
  warden 0.45, so shapes and arrangement are unchanged. The needler is restated at
  scale 0.5 with all sizes and offsets divided by 3.2 and its needle drawn as a thin
  ellipsoid whose tip lands exactly on the radius, so it stays one metre long (the
  "a metre across" rule in `test_robot_types_apart.gd`); its skin `scale` and
  `face_depth` are restated in its new unit frame (2.5 to 4.0, 0.85 to 0.53125).
- New `tests/test_robot_look_fits.gd`: every robot data file's look must fit; the old
  glider entry is caught as `"glider" parts.2 (box) reaches 2.075 radii`; scale over 1
  is caught; reach of every unit shape.
- No `core/`, `gen/` or `data/robots/` change.
