## Summary

- New `game/core/level_def.gd` (`LevelDef`, `RefCounted`): parses level text into a
  `BoxUnion`, a start (position and facing, whole millimetres) and a `RobotSpawnList`
  (the `robots` value is re-encoded and handed to `RobotSpawnList.parse_json`, so the
  spawn format has one parser; `robot_spawn_list.gd` untouched).
- Rejects, naming the problem: an unknown or missing key (top level, box, start), a box
  whose min is not below its max on some axis, a box not reachable from box 0, a start
  or spawn the union does not hold with 1 m to spare. A rejected level keeps nothing.
- "Connected" is read as: the two boxes overlap by at least 2 m on every axis (the
  opening is at least 2 m both ways, and each box reaches 2 m into the other).
  `LevelDef.joined` and `neighbours` are public so tests and later tools can walk it.
- New `game/data/levels/g2_level.json`: six boxes. Entry hall (46 m) → vertical shaft
  down (48 m) → lower tunnel (66 m) with a 21 × 16 × 20 m side room off it → far
  chamber → a second shaft up. One robot of each of the six types; the needler, nearest,
  is 32 m from the start; every robot fits at its own radius.
