## Summary

- `xr/status_panel_tally.gd` (`StatusPanelTally`): from two frames of plain copies (the
  sim's `collected` counts, the rescued count, the run's banked counts), like
  `HoldMessages`, it tells what rose and returns the hold-row line: "+2 Cell Salts" or
  "Miner aboard" (a rescue outranks a pickup). The first frame only remembers; a count that
  falls (a new level) says nothing. Writes nothing back (MP-2).
- `xr/status_panel_tally_row.gd`: two labels on the panel, "Carried 1 Cell Salts | Miners 1"
  in the rows' size and "Banked …" small and grey (the level row's font and colour), only
  in a run. A rise tints the carried line green for 1 s; nothing moves or blinks.
- Placement: the lines sit **above the countdown**, on top of the panel. Under the hold they
  passed the 30° glance limit on preset 1 with two weapon slots (31.5°); on top they are the
  rows nearest the eye line.
- Words, flash colour and time are optional keys in `xr/status_panel_look.json` (ARC-4);
  `tally` joins the `rows` list and `StatusPanelRowList.FIGHT`.
- `CombatWiring.update` feeds the tally each frame and takes the run (`main.gd` passes
  `level_start.run`); the tally's line wins the hold row over the part message that frame.
- Rescue sound: `MINER_RESCUED` now plays the new `miner_aboard.wav` (a slower, lower rising
  arpeggio than `pickup.wav`), written by `tools/synth_sounds.py`; the other wavs come out
  byte-identical. Licence row updated. The `COMPONENT_COLLECTED` pickup cue from #1290 was
  already wired and plays at the heap.
- Acceptance: new check `tally_counts` (`acceptance/checks/tally_check.gd`) on the m6
  `pickup_look` step (lane-a's `ship.json`), before `crew_joins`. That flight collects cell
  salts and rescues a miner; locally it reported "Carried 1 Cell Salts | Miners 1; 1 pickups
  and 1 rescues confirmed". The step's pinned hash is unchanged.
