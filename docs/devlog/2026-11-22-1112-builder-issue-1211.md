## Issue

#1211: an aiming reticle on every preset (CMB-6, PLT-2, ARC-3, CMF-6, DEV-7).

## Summary

- `AimReticle` (#923) now shows on every comfort preset, with or without a look-aim
  module. It still reads only `ShipState.gun_direction`, which is the nose without
  look-aim, so no aim logic changed.
- A live robot within the selected primary weapon's range (projectile speed times
  lifetime) and inside a cone around the aim draws the ring onto it: at that robot's
  distance, full alpha. Otherwise the ring floats at the look's distance with its alpha
  scaled by `dim_alpha`. The ring is scaled with its distance so its apparent size stays
  the same.
- `view/aim_reticle_look.json` gains `cone_degrees` (4) and `dim_alpha` (0.35).
- `CombatWiring.update` keeps its `look_aim` argument (renamed `_look_aim`, unused)
  because `main.gd` passes it and is not in this issue's files. Its new
  `weapon_range(ship)` gives the selected slot's range, 0 for an empty slot.
- The optional menu row to switch the reticle off is left out, as the Planner shaped it.
