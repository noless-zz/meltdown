# 2026-11-17 Builder, issue #395 (lane-a)

Scheduled Builder run in lane-a. The only open pull request was #230, which closes a lane-b
issue, so there was nothing to fix. The `ready` lane-a issues were #395, #396, #397 and #398,
all in M3; this run took #395. Named in Israel time (UTC+2).

## Summary

- Three new module files in `game/data/modules/`, all with `small` connectors:
  - `room_six_way`: one 24 m cube with a connector in the middle of each of its six faces.
  - `offset_shaft`: two 15 × 24 × 10 m and 15 × 22 × 10 m boxes overlapping by 8 × 6 × 10 m.
    The bottom connector (-y) is at x = 0 and the top one (+y) at x = 12, one 10 m tunnel
    width and more apart.
  - `dead_end_alcove`: a 10 × 10 × 20 m tunnel with its one connector (+z), ending in a
    20 × 16 × 23 m room.
- `MineGenerator.DEAD_END_MIN_OPEN = 6`: a module with one connector joins only while six or
  more connectors are open. This is a change outside the issue's file list, and it was
  needed. Without it, dead ends closed the last openings: 71 of seeds 0..999 stopped short and
  49 had no opening to start in. With a threshold of 2, 3, 4 or 5, some seeds in 0..999 or
  0..9,999 still stopped short, because their few remaining openings were walled in.
  With 6, none did.
- Pinned generator hashes: none changed. No test pins a generator `output_hash` value. The
  pinned acceptance hashes fly G2, which is not generated.

## Test results

- Godot 4.7.2-stable in the run's scratch space, `--import` then `run_tests.gd` as CI runs
  them: 1181 tests, 0 failed.
- New `game/tests/test_module_kit.gd` (6 tests). Each file parses under its own name. The
  room opens on all six axes. The shaft has two joined boxes, and its ends are at least a
  tunnel width apart sideways. The alcove has one connector and a box that holds the reactor
  with a ship's diameter of clearance. Every kit box is at least twice the largest default
  ship's diameter on every axis. Over seeds 0..999, no level stops short, every level passes
  every `LevelValidator` check, and each kit module is used.
- `test_mine_generator.gd`: a new test checks over 200 seeds that a dead end never joins
  while fewer than `DEAD_END_MIN_OPEN` connectors are open, and that the open count always
  stays at one or more. `test_stops_short_and_says_so` (all dead ends) now expects one module
  placed instead of two.
- `test_module_def.gd`: modules listed in `DEAD_END_IDS` may, and must, have exactly one
  connector. All other modules still need two.
- `validate_seeds.gd --seeds=10000 --first=0`: 10,000 passed, 0 stopped short, 15.1 s.
