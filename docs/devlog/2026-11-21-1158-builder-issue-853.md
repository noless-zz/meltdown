## Summary
- `game/xr/swivel_choice.gd` (`SwivelChoice`): `user://meltdown_swivel.json`
  (`{"enabled": bool}`). A missing file reads as off. A bad file gets a warning naming its path
  and reads as off. `applied` sets only `swivel_enabled` on presets 1 to 4. It changes a copy,
  because the preset files are shared cached resources. In the custom mode it leaves the preset
  as it is.
- `game/comfort_wiring.gd`: `swivel_choice` and `set_swivel_choice`. `set_preset` passes every
  preset through `SwivelChoice.applied`, so the choice survives later preset changes. The status
  panel's swivel tag (#792) reads `comfort.preset`, so it follows without a change.
- `game/dev_menu_swivel.gd` (`DevMenuSwivel`): applies the saved choice at start. It shows the
  entry, follows a Comfort choice into or out of the custom mode while the menu is open, and
  saves and applies a choice. In the custom mode the entry shows "(custom file)", left and right
  do nothing, and a choice is neither saved nor written to the custom file.
- Dev menu: a `swivel` kind, an entry after "Break reminder", and the texts `swivel_on_text`,
  `swivel_off_text` and `swivel_custom_text` in `dev_menu.json`. `DevMenuWiring` gains about
  10 lines.
