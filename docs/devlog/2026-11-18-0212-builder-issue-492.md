## How the uncleared flights ended before the change

`bot_escape_sweep.gd --robots --debris --seeds=300` on `main` at 4c181bd: 19 cleared, 19
escaped, 281 not cleared. All 281 ended **destroyed before the reactor**; none ran out of ticks
and none ended otherwise. 133 of them died within 10 s of the start, 129 between 10 s and a
minute, 19 later.

Tracing seeds 1..15 tick by tick showed the main cause: the bot took nearly every robot shot.
Robots fire along their facing at where the ship is, but the bot flew mostly straight at its
target, so a needle gun (45 points a second) emptied its 150 points of shield and hull in two to
three seconds. Two more causes made it worse: the nose lagged any robot crossing it by about
9 degrees, outside the 5 degree fire cone, so it rarely fired at strafers (seed 7: no shot in
135 ticks); and a ship under fire from its first tick had no "last safe" point, so it never fell
back and fought a whole room at once.

## Summary

- `bot_dodge.gd` (new): while a robot can hit the ship, the bot adds a 15 m/s weave across the
  line to the nearest such robot, keeping its sense until a wall stops it. This is the main fix.
- `bot_aim.gd` (new, split from `bot_pilot.gd`): `track` adds the rate at which the lead point
  crosses the nose to the turn, so the nose keeps up with strafers; `cost` picks the robot
  cheapest to aim at (angle to turn plus distance over 30 m) instead of the nearest.
- `bot_cover.gd` (new): the nearest point within three openings that no armed robot can hit.
  The bot now also falls back when two or more robots can hit it, and uses cover when its last
  safe point is exposed or it never had one.
- `bot_pilot.gd`: 291 to 282 lines; aiming moved out, with thin wrappers so callers and
  existing tests are unchanged. No `core/`, `gen/`, `data/`, sweep script or `tools/` change.

## Open questions

- The sweep is slower: about 3 s a seed instead of 1.2 s with robots and debris, because
  flights last longer and the stalled seed 164 flies all 36,000 ticks. A nightly shard of 125
  seeds should still finish well inside its 30 minutes; the Planner may want to check the first
  nightly run.
- 51 flights still die within 10 s, mostly starts in a room where five or more robots, several
  of them needlers, see the ship at once and no cover lies within three openings. Whether that
  is fair to a human pilot is the robot density question on Status #1.

  possible, since 143 seeds clear.
