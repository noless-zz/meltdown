# 0007 The VR-sensitive tester moves from gate G1 to gate G6

Date: 2026-11-20. Decided by the owner (issue #675).

## Decision

Gate G1's human exit criteria are now only "15 minutes at each preset", flown by the
owner. "A VR-sensitive tester finishes 10 minutes on preset 1" moves to gate G6 (Look,
sound, speed).

## Why

At M1 the game is grey boxes with placeholder feedback. The owner's judgement is that it
is "not polished enough to make sense" to the people who would test it, so their reaction
would say more about the missing polish than about comfort. Holding G1 open for it also
blocks planning of M4.

## What this leaves open

- Preset 1 has not been checked by the person it exists for. Until G6, treat its comfort
  as unproven: do not loosen its limits (CMF-1, CMF-3) on the owner's impressions alone.
- The owner found preset 1's snap turning "very confusing" at 72 Hz and deferred that
  question to the 90 Hz build and this tester. With the tester moved, the 90 Hz comfort
  session is the evidence at G1; the tester's verdict comes at G6. Any change to preset 1's
  rotation before then needs his explicit decision.
- If a sensitive tester tries the game earlier, record the result on the open gate; it
  counts.
